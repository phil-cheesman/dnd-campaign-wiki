I. Of His Person & Nature

Pirate captain of Fortune’s Favor — sparrow flag, big poofy hat, short and very fast, with high cheekbones, piercing blue eyes, and a scar. Under Divine Sense he reads as inherently good but complicated, darkened by pressures. Confirmed to be the same person as “Shauwn O’Malley,” who delivered the royal wedding letter in the twentieth chronicle.

II. The Stolen Artifact

His sparrow calling card was left where the final Zeben artifact was stolen: drunk Archivists tipped him to a relic beneath the seas, and he navigated the fog-bound Veiled Ocean to the underwater Temple of Fenreal and retrieved it — then traded it away for something very important to him. It now lies in the Jazacha jungles with the artifact-obsessed Yuan-Ti of Oojezz.

III. The Kraken's Price

At the Crow’s Ledger in Anchors Rest he set his price: capture the Kraken plaguing the Pirate Trinity. The party did; Mally controlled it with a scepter and chain, delivered it at Krakens Reef, and so leads the pirates of Anchors Rest.

IV. The Jazacha Expedition

He guided the four-ship armada to Jazacha and the jungle expedition — five of his men died — and burned the Wish spell stored in his bracelet to wish Zuk’taal away, leaving him dejected. He also leaked the party’s destination to the Trinity, and warned that Zax, the Merchant, and possibly Sir Trent Hightower had contacted them.

V. Companions & Whereabouts

  • Active ally and guide as of E161, on the Jazacha expedition; left the party on floor four with business back in Oojezz.
  • Old hand with Jameti Costco as a jungle guide — “something went real wrong.”
  • Renee at Airsteach is an ex-employee.
