SURVEY № 047 · LAT. UNKNOWN · LAST FIX: JAZACHA INTERIOR, FLOOR IV

Mally “Molly” Grisham

the Sparrow · Master of Fortune’s Favor · Lord of Anchors Rest
VERIFIED · TRANSCRIPT-ENRICHED

1Sighting reports

Pirate captain of Fortune’s Favor — sparrow flag, big poofy hat, short, very fast, with high cheekbones, piercing blue eyes, and a scar. Under Divine Sense he reads as inherently good but complicated, darkened by pressures. Confirmed the same man as “Shauwn O’Malley,” who delivered the royal wedding letter (E20).

2Course of the stolen artifact

His sparrow calling card was left where the final Zeben artifact was stolen. Drunk Archivists tipped him to a relic beneath the seas; he navigated the fog-bound Veiled Ocean to the underwater Temple of Fenreal and retrieved it — then traded it away for something very important to him. Present position of the artifact: the Jazacha jungles, held by the artifact-obsessed Yuan-Ti of Oojezz.

UNCHARTED WATERS What did Mally trade the final artifact for? The survey leaves this blank.

3The Kraken contract

At the Crow’s Ledger in Anchors Rest he set his price: capture the Kraken plaguing the Pirate Trinity. The party did; Mally controlled it with a scepter and chain, delivered it at Krakens Reef, and so leads the pirates of Anchors Rest.

4The Jazacha expedition

He piloted the four-ship armada to Jazacha and guided the jungle expedition — five of his men died — and burned the Wish spell stored in his bracelet to wish Zuk’taal away, leaving him dejected. He also leaked the party’s destination to the Trinity, and warned that Zax, the Merchant, and possibly Sir Trent Hightower had made contact.
