Mally “Molly” Grisham

THE SPARROW — CAPTAIN OF FORTUNE’S FAVOR

01Overview

Pirate captain of Fortune’s Favor — sparrow flag, high cheekbones, piercing blue eyes, and a scar. Confirmed to be the same person as “Shauwn O’Malley,” who delivered the royal wedding letter in E20. Now leads the pirates of Anchors Rest after delivering a captured Kraken to Krakens Reef.

02The stolen Zeben artifact

His sparrow calling card was left where the final Zeben artifact was stolen. Drunk Archivists tipped him to a relic beneath the seas; he navigated the fog-bound Veiled Ocean to the underwater Temple of Fenreal and retrieved it — then traded it away for something very important to him. It now lies in the Jazacha jungles with the artifact-obsessed Yuan-Ti of Oojezz.

Open thread What did Mally trade the final artifact for? The notes leave it deliberately blank.

03The Kraken’s price

At the Crow’s Ledger in Anchors Rest he set his price for cooperation: capture the Kraken plaguing the Pirate Trinity. The party did. Mally controlled it with a scepter and chain, delivered it at Krakens Reef, and so claimed leadership of the pirates of Anchors Rest.

04The Jazacha expedition

He guided the four-ship armada to Jazacha and the jungle expedition — five of his men died — and burned the Wish spell stored in his bracelet to wish Zuk’taal away, leaving him dejected. He also leaked the party’s destination to the Trinity, and warned that Zax, the Merchant, and possibly Sir Trent Hightower had contacted them.
