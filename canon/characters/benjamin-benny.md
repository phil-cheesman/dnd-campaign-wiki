# Benjamin

A party PC played by Steve (his third character, after Zook and Evac). A gold-loving, Robin Hood-style thief from Goldcrest in Gidia, kicked out of town for trying to steal from Marquise Avesy. The party freed him from orc guards near the Shimmering Grotto (E75) and he became their cleric/healer. In the Evershire arc he kept the party alive with furious mass healing (dropping to 3 HP himself against Blackwood), killed the witch Viola with a critical javelin throw (E87), and cast Path of the Grave on the dragon Valadon. He woke imprisoned in Blackwood's manor when the lord revealed himself as the dragon, and walked off in disgust when Cruucar killed the captive Julian with the Soul Leech dagger. In Avernus he escaped with a jeweled scepter spear twice his size — identified as Windvane, a +2 lightning spear with flight on Dash, lightning bolt charges, and a flaw making its wielder mercurial. He attended the Purple Party (a lovely time with Joyce), and in the Vault of Izdar found himself looking through a patchwork flesh golem's eye. Benjamin died in the beholder fight (E110); his dead body held the ruby ring. (Earlier notes guessed the Avernus "Betty" head-of-guard (E97) was his nickname — that's wrong; Betty was a separate PC played by Phil.)
