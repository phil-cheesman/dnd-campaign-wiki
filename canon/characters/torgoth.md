# Torgoth

## Identity
- Goliath: gray skin, 8.5 feet tall, underbite, ponytail, big beard; thin armor on his right side, thick armor on legs and chest (E114).
- Cleric/fighter (?) — never stated as a class in the notes, but he casts Revivify, Healing Word (6th-level), Control Water, Tongues, and Lesser Restoration, and uses Action Surge, Second Wind, and Fighting Spirit charges (E128, E143, E148, E160). Snores awfully loud (E118).

## Story highlights
- First appears in the notes receiving 2 of 4 small diamonds from the Vault of Izdar loot (E111); formally met in the Tarlif market while the party hunted "the Clockmaker" (E114).
- Officially joined the crew in Tarlif the night Squinton and the Scorpions played their banger show (E115).
- Given a Pearl of Power during the Tarlif gear-up (E117); covered himself in bog mud for the night ambush near Moonbright (E118).
- Passed out after three swigs of liquor in Grimsburg, earning a level of exhaustion (E121).
- Shadowfell madness arc: attacked the whole party in his sleep-madness and ran away (E122); turned sad and suicidal until Berrian's Remove Curse (E123); gave the Dwemerlake a small bow (E126).
- Revivified Vane at the bridge after the Tower of Illmore escape, burning three Fighting Spirit charges and an Action Surge (E128, "The Death of Vane").
- Battered to 0 HP in the Dark Glen, made two death saves, and was healed back up; Berrian passed him a diamond (E130).
- Gifted the Forged Amber — a live lightning bolt, or "shock a friend back to life" (E131).
- Flew over Mag Stormeach with Berrian using Locate Creature to pin Rasheed Soltar's location (E141).
- In the Rasheed compound battle: threw the Windvane spear and healed Quinton with a 6th-level Healing Word, expending one of four diamonds (E143).
- Saved the ship Wrecked at the Reef with Control Water, at a cost of 8 texair (E148).
- Ran the invisible front assault on the Mandalonia docks with Quinton — Control Water plus Disintegrate (E146).
- Helped pry open the Kraken's jaws with Vane and Quinton using the Rod of Lordly Might (E155); heavily injured in the Kraken capture (E156).
- Killed the piranha swarm with a lightning attack into the water — nearly killing Quinton and Vane in the process (E158).
- In the Oojezz pyramid fighting: took heavy damage, burning Action Surge, two Tongues, Second Wind, and a Lesser Restoration (E160).
- **E167 — talking to Slaad.** Tor was reluctant to surrender his weapons and gave them up only after being talked round. Later, in the Quiet Vaults, he used **Tongues** on the four caged **Slaad**, *"offering grunts and groans and croaks"* — and was interrupted mid-attempt by the return of Commander Vaelor, who *"glances at Tor, gives just the slightest frown."* No reply from the Slaad is recorded, and they were executed off-screen shortly after. **Whether he actually got a response is still open** `(?)` — the question was put to Elliott (who ran Torgoth) in the E167 review and came back unanswered. Also of note out of character: Jon's AI-generated session images refused to render Tor as anything other than a Black man with dreadlocks, which became a running table joke and the source of an in-fiction jab from **Rhen Sarik** about *"your friend there with the dreads."*

On floor three of the Great Sun Temple (E162) he cleaved the hostile bleeding vines off Berrian with Windvane and anchored the fight with Call Lightning and Windvane strikes (a 38-damage, 10-ft-push hit on the Anathema). E164 was a rough one: **polymorphed into a common garden snake** by an Anathema (freed when it lost concentration), dropped by the Avatar's poison-dagger rain, rope-hauled ~100 feet through the air by Noctis's Rod-of-Lordly-Might pole vault (face-planting for a death-save failure), and revived by Noctis's last potion — while his own Healing Words twice pulled Cruucar off death's door.
