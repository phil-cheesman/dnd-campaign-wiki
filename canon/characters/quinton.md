# Quinton Shackleford

## Identity
- Half-elf bard, chaotic good, worships Avandra, Goddess of Freedom (backstory doc). 23 years old at campaign start, 5'5", 150 lbs.
- From the village of Norbury in the Gidian Empire countryside (backstory doc; confirmed in-game E33 — "his hometown Norbury, NE of Kingswood").
- One of seven children, 2nd youngest; father Lou (human blacksmith), mother Carissa (elf who taught him basic healing and simple spells); eldest brother Gareth introduced him to music (backstory doc). His magical music ability appeared suddenly when young, around the time his mother dabbled in potion-making (E31).
- Lute-playing performer, master of the common tongue — persuasion, songwriting, impersonation, seduction (backstory doc). The party's frontman, charmer, and chronicler-in-song.
- Level-15 College of Glamour bard, Folk Hero background (sheet extracted in `sources/character-sheets/quinton/quinton.md` from the D&D Beyond PDF export). A face/charisma build (CHA 18, expertise in Persuasion/Performance/Intimidation) with the body of a non-combatant (STR 4); his CON 19 comes from an attuned Amulet of Health. Wields the Mace of Terror and the Doss Lute.

## Story highlights

- Debuted the song "Evac's Whore" — a ballad begging Lorenzo not to betray the party, referencing Zook and Zanim's leg (E29). Also wrote "Take Him Home, Dragonborn" about LJ, Viola's curse, and Cruucar (E37).

- Nearly died with Evac fighting Dragoloth the Silent — "Jon was nice" (E65).

- In the Vault of Izdar: heard chains rattling in the failed rest (E108-dup2), became a woman (E112), and took the beholder-hoard mace (E111–E112) — identified as the Mace of Terror (E115).
- Fronted the band "Squinton and the Scorpions" in Tarlif — "an absolute banger of a show" with Minor Illusion smoke and fireworks (E115).
- Shadowfell madness arc: woke as a religious zealot after sleeping (E122); enthralled the bridge guard Crass with his lute to get the party into Gloomville (E123); shit himself at Withervale Estate (E125).

- Co-ran the invisible Control Water/Disintegrate assault on the Mandalonia docks with Torgoth (E146).
