**W4. Who/what are the Archivists?**

They keep Arbor Alma at Messis Cordis, train at Donum Vitae, are led by High Architect Zeenoth the Righteous, harvest texerine yearly into texere, and place one High Rector advisor in each Trine capital (Veranius/Gidia, Lucia/Carasia, Atronius/Luzonovia). *(Evidence: the-archivists-overview.md and the duplicate faction block in campaign-2-background-information-player-info.md.)* ✓ Jon: yes — DM-confirmed.

**W7. Why does transmutation require texere, and where does it come from?**
→ Transmutation was removed from the learnable weave by the faithful gods at the end of the Sundering (replaced by divination) to stop the betrayer gods overdriving magic. Because mortals could no longer cast it but needed it to survive erratic weather/famine, the gods gave the Gift: Arbor Alma, whose texerine leaves the Archivists refine into texere coins that let anyone cast transmutation. The tree grows in only one place and cannot be transplanted. *(Evidence: campaign-2-background-information-player-info.md sections "Arbor Alma", "How/Why was transmutation removed from the weave", "Weather patterns are really impacted".)* ✓ Jon: yes — DM-confirmed.
