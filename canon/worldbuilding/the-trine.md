# The Trine

**The Trine** (also rendered "the Trene") is the realm of realms that emerged after [[the-sundering-and-the-gift|the Sundering]] — three nation-states that allied with the faithful gods, defeated the Drow dynasty, and have shared the Gift peacefully ever since. The Archivists keep its peace and link its members by teleportation; the later Pirate Trinity is named as a deliberate mockery of it (E26, E29, E33, E147).

This is a framing page. The members and their territories each have their own dossiers — follow the links below rather than expecting them re-described here.

## The three nations

- **[[gidian-empire|Gidian Empire]]** — the dominant British-flavored seafaring trade empire. Vassals and neighbors include the Islands of Maadolonia ([[islands-of-maadolonia|dossier]]) and the Free States of Dalacia.
- **[[carasian-kingdom|Carasian Kingdom]]** — the wealthy royal kingdom. Vassals and neighbors include the Morian Kingdom and the [[parathia|Kingdom of Parathia]].
- **[[luzonovian-sovereignty|Luzonovian Sovereignty]]** — the insular nationalist sovereignty. Vassals and neighbors include the Kingdom of Treaborg and the Vithian Alliance.

## The Council of Reckoning

The planner notes the Trine was formed at a **Council of Reckoning** convened by **Alon the Wise** — the first Archivist, cross-referenced to "Alan the Wise" — who is credited with stopping the post-Sundering wars roughly 480 years ago (E33). The three more powerful houses among the primitive nations are what became the Trine; together with the gods they banished the Drow dynasty to another plane of existence.
