# Gods of Alambor

The post-Sundering pantheon of Alambor is a reduced one: the Great War that ended the pre-Sundering age killed many gods, and the surviving faithful gods withdrew from active life in the mortal world (see [[the-sundering-and-the-gift|the Sundering]]). The **Betrayer Gods** were struck from the pantheon entirely and persist only through the desperate faith of their followers.

Jon founds the pantheon on Corellon, Pelor, Sehanine, and the gods of the seasons, with the Raven Queen later replacing Nerull as god(dess) of Death and Winter. The intermingling of races added Melora (elves), Moradin (dwarves), and Avandra (halflings); Erathis, Bahamut, Ioun, and Kord grew followings as civilization and battle demanded.

## The four houses

### Faithful (living) gods
- **Avandra** — CG, change / luck / trade.
- **Erathis** — LN, civilization.
- **Kord** "Tempast" — CN, storm / war. Captured and tortured rather than killed in the war (see [[texere-and-transmutation|texere & transmutation]] for the wild-weather aftermath).
- **Moradin** — LG, creation / artisans.
- **Pelor** — NG, sun / harvest.
- **the Raven Queen** — LN, death / winter.

### Dead faithful gods
- **Bahamut** — LG, justice.
- **Corellon** — CG, magic / the fey; slain in the war, his Eledine forests destroyed.
- **Ioun** — N, knowledge.
- **Melora** — N, wilderness / sea.
- **Sehanine** — CG, moon / illusions.

### Betrayer gods
- **Asmodeus** — LE, tyranny.
- **Bane** — CE, war / conquest.
- **Gruumsh** — CE, destruction.
- **Lolth** — CE, lies / spiders.
- **Vecna** — NE, undeath / secrets.
- **Zehir** — CE, poison / assassins.

### Dead betrayer gods
- **Tharizdun** — CE, the Chained God.
- **Tiamat** — LE, greed.
- **Torog** — NE, the Underdark.

## Notes
- The betrayer gods whispered to the pre-Sundering Drow dynasty and sought to overthrow the faithful gods and seize their power; their sealing in the Hells is part of the Sundering's resolution (E00, E139).
- Played canon names some of these deaths and survivals differently in places and adds figures from the *Sundering* reading (e.g. the "Dawn Gods," Orcus as a god of betrayal who fell in the Sundering, E12); reconcile against this doc-derived list where they conflict (?).
- Malora, named among the gods "destroyed" in the Sundering, is described in play as speaking to Berrian (E140) — a thread the reduced-pantheon framing does not fully resolve (?).
