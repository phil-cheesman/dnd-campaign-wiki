# Texere & Transmutation

This is the "how magic money works" explainer for Alambor: why transmutation is rare, where the Gift came from, and what a spell actually costs. For the cataclysm that set it up see [[the-sundering-and-the-gift|the Sundering]]; for the currency item itself see the Texair dossier.

## The Gift and the removal of transmutation

As thanks for the faithful gods' victory in the war, the gods chose to withdraw from active life in the mortal world but left the primitive peoples **"the Gift"** — the Arbor Alma, whose leaves (**texerine**) are refined into **texere** (coin-like form), granting the ability to use transmutation magic. This is shared among [[the-trine|the Trine]] and distributed by the Archivists. Transmutation is otherwise rare in the world (only very rare cases occur naturally) (E00, E39, E108, E119, E139).

As a final act of the war, the faithful gods removed one of the seven learnable schools of magic — **transmutation** — from the weave, because the betrayer gods kept pushing magic (especially necromancy and enchantment, to dominate the people) toward the breaking point that would ultimately tear the fabric of reality. To keep the weave in balance they put **divination** in transmutation's place. They then withdrew behind the **divine gate** and sealed the betrayer gods in the Hells. Because transmutation remained essential to keeping alive the humans who could not generate it innately, the gods bestowed the Gift to compensate. The tree grows only in its one location; "more science-focused kingdoms" tried and failed to transplant it.

## Wild weather and feast/famine

**Kord** (called "Tempast") was not killed in the war but captured and tortured, leaving him a wild and untamed god (see [[gods-of-alambor|Gods of Alambor]]). The result is badly disrupted crop cycles and a world cycling through feast and famine, which drives heavy reliance on transmutation (texere) to keep populations alive. Resource scarcity is widespread, and the Trine's poor reputation as traders makes raw materials hard to come by in many regions.

## Transmutation economics

Jon's docs include a texere-cost table: casting a transmutation spell of level 1–9 consumes texere scaled by spell level and rarity. A 1st-level common spell costs **1 texere**; a 9th-level legendary spell costs **450**. This is the mechanical expression of texere as the realm's scarce magical currency — the same coinage the Archivists make and distribute equally across the Trine.

## Notes
- The cost table is a rules-facing artifact from the planning docs; in-play texere is handled more loosely (palmed pinches, carts, vault stores) (E39, E126, E139).
