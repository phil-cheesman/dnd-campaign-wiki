# Vessels of the Seven

The Ziven scholarly term for the personal artifacts of the seven archmages of [the Hive](#) — and
the point at which the party learns there is a **name and a catalogue** for the things they have
been chasing across two campaigns.

> **Source.** Jon's between-sessions research handout (17 Aug 2026), section 7, answering the
> party's declared library research from E167. Three texts.

## What a Vessel actually is

***Vessels of the Seven: Masterworks of the Late Arcane Age*, by Historian Vaelora T'seryn**,
Ilharess Academy of Material Arcana.

> *A staff containing a spell is not a Vessel. A sword bearing enchantment is not a Vessel. A
> robe capable of protecting its wearer from magic is not, by that quality alone, a Vessel.*
>
> *The distinction lies not in the power of the object but in the relationship between object and
> practitioner.*

The origin is a **limit of the body**:

> *…the mortal form could channel only a finite concentration of magical energy before the vessel
> of flesh itself became the limiting factor.*
>
> *If the practitioner could not safely contain the entirety of an arcane working, then some
> portion of that working might instead be housed externally.*

So each Vessel was built to **resonate continuously** with one branch of the Arcanum *and* with
the archmage who made it. They were not badges of office:

> *The Archmages did not carry them to demonstrate mastery. They created them because mastery had
> reached a point at which an additional instrument became necessary.*
>
> *Thus one should not ask what a Vessel does. One should ask what principle it embodies. That
> principle is the true artifact. The object is merely its shape.*

## The seven — discipline and named Vessel

From T'seryn's list and **Archivist Saelith Orr**'s *Catalogue of Lost Works of the Seven*
(Repository of Pre-Sundering Material Culture). **Every "current disposition" in the catalogue
reads *Unknown*** — except the last.

| Archmage | Discipline | Recorded Vessel | Catalogue notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **[Ninath](#)** | Evocation | ***Helm of Brilliance*** | Tied to Ninath's attempts to concentrate multiple destructive elemental energies in a single controllable matrix. Claims it could *"burn the heavens"* are called **poetic rather than technical**. |
| **[Felyn'riel](#)** | Illusion | ***Cloak of Displacement*** | Did **not** render the wearer invisible; caused *"persistent disagreement between the wearer's physical location and perceived location."* Felyn'riel reportedly thought ordinary invisibility *"an inelegant solution to a simple problem."* |
| **[Izzdar](#)** | Necromancy | ***Nine Lives Stealer*** | Made during Izzdar's study of *"the boundary between living essence and physical form."* Could sever that connection under particular conditions. Claims about **storage or reuse of severed essence remain disputed**. |
| **[Zarryne](#)** | Enchantment | ***Crystal Ball of Telepathy*** | Not a conventional scrying device — built around **transmission and reception of thought**, reportedly across extraordinary distances. |
| **[Valonim](#)** | Abjuration | ***Robe of the Archmagi*** | Simultaneously personal ward, magical amplifier, and stabilizing matrix against hostile arcane effects. |
| **[Ilharess](#)** | Conjuration | ***Robe of Stars*** | Built on **the reduction of planar distance through Conjuration**. Astral movement and projected arcane force were *"merely consequences"* of that deeper principle. Surviving descriptions explicitly record **limited independent passage into the Astral Plane**. |
| **[Omago](#)** | Transmutation | **`[CATALOGUE REFERENCE SEALED]`** | Description, last association and disposition all **`[RESTRICTED]`**. *"Authorization required: **Hive Continuity Classification**."* |

Orr's own note on the sealed entry:

> *Readers occasionally interpret the restricted classification of Omago's Vessel as evidence that
> its fate is known. No such conclusion should be drawn. Restriction indicates only that surviving
> documentation intersects with research still governed by Continuity statutes.*

**Six entries are freely available. Only Omago's is sealed.** That is the thing to notice.

## None of them went into exile

> *Of the Seven Vessels, **none are known to have accompanied their creators into exile**.*
>
> *The Sundering severed cities, laboratories, estates, and research complexes from one another
> with little regard for ownership or importance. **The Hive survived. Their greatest instruments
> did not.***
>
> *Whether the Vessels were destroyed, buried beneath the transformed world, claimed by later
> peoples, or remain where their creators last placed them cannot be established from surviving
> records.*
>
> *One should therefore resist the romantic assertion that the Seven Vessels are "waiting to be
> recovered." History does not wait. It survives where circumstance permits.*

**So: the Vessels stayed on the material side. In Alambor.** Which is exactly where the party and
[Adune](#) have been finding them.

## Resonance — the clue the paper doesn't finish

**Magister Theranis Vel**, *Resonant Architecture: On the Construction of Arcane Vessels*,
*Journal of Applied Thaumaturgical Engineering*, Vol. 947. Why no modern Ziven artificer has ever
reproduced one:

> *Enchantment begins with an object and places magic within it. **Vessel construction begins with
> an arcane principle and creates an object capable of giving that principle physical
> continuity.***
>
> *A replica of the Robe of Stars may resemble the original… It remains a replica. The original
> was constructed around a stable Conjuration resonance established by Ilharess personally.*

Then the experimental result:

> *Experiments conducted using lesser surviving Vessels demonstrate measurable **sympathetic
> behavior**. The objects begin exchanging minute quantities of arcane energy without deliberate
> activation. Their magical signatures gradually synchronize.*
>
> *This occurs **even when the objects belong to different disciplines**. Indeed,
> **cross-disciplinary resonance appears stronger** in several surviving experiments than
> resonance between objects of identical classification.*
>
> *…an Evocation Vessel and a Conjuration Vessel brought into sufficiently close proximity should
> not interfere with one another. **They should complement one another.** Three should produce a
> more complex resonance than two. Four more complex than three.*
>
> *Whether this progression continues remains impossible to determine, as no surviving collection
> contains sufficiently sophisticated pre-Sundering Vessels representing more than three distinct
> disciplines.*

And the question it deliberately stops at:

> *If each represented one of the principal arcane disciplines, **what would occur if all seven
> resonances were brought into conjunction?***
>
> *No surviving public record describes such an experiment. The possibility remains academically
> fascinating and practically untestable.*

The paper never says *collect them and open a gate*. It hands the party a true fragment and lets
them finish the sentence.

## What the party can put together

Marked **(?)** because the handout does not say any of this — it is the arithmetic the table is
now in a position to do.

- **Somebody has been collecting these.** *"No surviving collection contains… more than three
  distinct disciplines"* — the party knows one that does. [Adune](#) has held four, then five, of
  the [six gate keys](#zeben-artifacts). **(?)**
- **Six catalogued + Omago sealed = seven.** Committed canon already holds that there are **six**
  gate keys (E139) and that **Omago is the power source**, his sentience being the
  [Potentium](#) that empowers Adune. Six keys and a separate power source is exactly the shape of
  this catalogue. **(?)**
- **The item names are already in the party's own notes:**
  - ***Nine Lives Stealer*** — the long black sword with skulls, red gems and nine blades the
    party saw in **E113**.
  - ***Crystal Ball of Telepathy*** vs the **Zarryne** orb — *"orange-sized cloudy glass orb on a
    silver stand, drow word carved"* — identified in **E24/E25**.
  - ***Cloak of Displacement*** — named in **E139** as the sixth and final relic, and traced
    through Mallie Grisham to the Yuan-Ti.
  - Noctis's **Izzdar cloak** is of Ziven make (E167) and shares the archmage's name — Jon's
    reaction in E167 was *"I couldn't believe you guys didn't connect that at the time."*
  - The **Ninath chainmail** and **Felynriel ring** identified by Drow reading in E25 are named for
    archmages but are **not** the catalogued Vessels (a helm and a cloak).

## Open questions

- **What is Omago's Vessel, and who sealed the entry?** *Hive Continuity Classification* means a
  living Hive authority is still classifying records. [Ilharess](#) is alive and wants to meet the
  party.
- Does the resonance effect work **at a distance**, or only in proximity? If the latter, the
  Vessels have to be physically gathered — which is what Adune is doing.
- Do the Zivens know Adune exists, or that a collection is being assembled on the material side?
  The party's read in E167 was that **no outsider has ever reached Ilharess**, which they took as
  evidence Adune has never made contact.
- If the Vessels never left Alambor, **how did the Ziven archives keep such precise descriptions?**
