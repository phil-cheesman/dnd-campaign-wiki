# Calendar & Festivals of Alambor

The Alambor year is reckoned from [[the-sundering-and-the-gift|the Sundering]], and its festival calendar is bound to the rhythm of the [[texere-and-transmutation|texere harvest]] — the budding and falling of the Arbor Alma, announced realm-wide by the Archivists.

## The calendar

- **300 cycles per annum** (year); **50 days per sextet** (month); **6 sextets**.
- **Summer** sextets: Avandi, Pelius, Erathen. **Winter** sextets: Tempastum, Ravena, Morane.
- Dating uses **A.S.** (Ante-Sundering) and **P.T.** (Post-Texerine).

## Festivals

- **Carnival Amantes / Amantes Festival** — first day of Avandi, a floating day marked by the first bud on the Arbor Alma (announced realm-wide by the Archivists lighting purple flames from Donum Vite). Held in honor of Avandra and standing in for the lost spring; celebrated especially in the Carasian Kingdom (described as Venice's Carnival crossed with April Fools).
- **Midsommer** — 25th day of Pelius; a festival of Erathis celebrated through creation (building a new structure or institution communally), led by Erathis's followers, with readings of her books and lore. (Jon's prose gives Midsommer both as "first day of the summer sextet" in one place and "25th day" in the calendar table — minor internal inconsistency (?).)
- **Feast of Vita Messis** — a floating fall holiday (usually in the last 20 days of Erathen) marking the fall of the texerine, signaled by Archivist beacons that warn the long winter is coming; a time of reflection, meager food and drink, prayer, and the blessing/preparation of fields (which mages must do for the texere to bear fruit).
- **Festival de Jeux** — fixed on the 25th day of Tempastum; a five-day festival of games, combat, contest, feasting, and gambling, prepared for all year in Carasa; champions are crowned, with the Chief Games held in the Colisée de Juli and a winner of each category announced by the High Queen.
- **Midwinter** — 25th day of Ravena; primarily worship of the Raven Queen and the gods lost in the Sundering, observed (and frowned on in some cultures, but not by the Carasians) as a wilderness journey in groups with great fires, drink, and mind-influencing herbs, during which the spirits of the gods are said to appear.
- The sextet of **Morane** has no broadly observed festival ("Only Dwarves celebrate here").

## In play
Played canon references several of these holidays under slightly different spellings and dates — the "Festival of Amentes" (E24), the Midsommer/Midsummer royal wedding (E03), the "Feast of Vita Messies" (E100), the mid-winter festival of the party's return (E131), and the hundred-year Red Eclipse that falls at midwinter (E134). Treat the doc calendar above as the structural reference and the played dates as in-world specifics that may have drifted (?).
