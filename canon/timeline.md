## World history (pre-campaign anchors)

From Jon's worldbuilding docs (`sources/dm-docs/`) — in-world backstory predating play, not session events:

- **The Sundering** — god-war that ended with the faithful gods removing transmutation from the learnable weave (replacing it with divination); play begins ~499 years later (E00).
- **17 P.T. — Council of Reckoning / founding of the Trine** — 17 years after the Sundering, Alon the Wise (first Archivist) convened the Council of Reckoning, founding the Trine: the Gidian Empire, the Carasian Kingdom, and the Luzonovian Sovereignty, who agreed to share the Gift of Arbor Alma (texere) distributed by the Archivists. (P.T. = the docs' post-founding era count.)
- **Vassal conquests (P.T.)** — Carasia annexed the Morian Kingdom (78) and Parathia (321); Gidia took the Free States of Dalacia (102) and the Islands of Maadolonia (327); Luzonovia took the Kingdom of Treaborg (127) and the Vithian Alliance (268).
