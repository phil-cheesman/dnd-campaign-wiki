# E89 — House of the Dragon

## Summary
Through the mirror, the party explored Blackwood's Manor. Cruucar crowbarred into a corner suite under a tapestry painting of Blackwood; the study held troop ledgers tied to Viola ("the Mother") and a letter sealed with a flying-dragon seal like Pierre Beaubois's dagger. The bedroom's Bandersnatch Mountains mural opened via a pulled book into a secret room holding a Rope of Entanglement (command word "Melon") and the Mode of Elemental Energy yellow diamond. A magical wardrobe-mirror opened into a massive cavern: acid pools, a huge gold pile, Elizabeth Janis caged, and the sleeping dragon. Lore surfaced about the Potentium, Blackwood's rank as Arch Dragon of Aum Shai, Zax serving as the dragon's "dog" set to return and run Evershire, a veiled "puppet master" leading Aum Shai, and the return of the Zeeven. The party attacked: rope, Path of the Grave, Cruucar's heavy hits, Vane's Polymorph, and a giant ape (Leon) trading huge damage with the dragon. Finally the Bear threw Leon, who lanced the dragon twice, killing it — and fell limp beneath it as it dropped.
