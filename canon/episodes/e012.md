# E12 — Mortal Portal

## Summary
Still inside the Beaubois Chalet, the party found sheet music for the harp, a crumpled-up iron ball, a steel ball, and a teleport scroll. On a floor they found a mural of a demon bat creature with horns, inscribed in Common: "I will be the last creature when I am done, the cosmos will be perfect, free of —- and all living things." This is Orcus, the god of betrayal — one of the gods that fell during the Sundering — whose servants are undead and spread misery and evil; he wields a skull-topped wand, and most of his followers were necromancers. The party took 100 platinum. The note-taker watched three dark-cloaked figures go up the central tower with 12 guards and overheard Pierre's muffled voice discussing business — refinement getting close, documents to be delivered, an in-person trial still needed to test potency. Three black-cloaked figures escaped by grappling hook out a window — one locked eyes with the note-taker and smirked before zooming off — and three more black figures glided down with Pierre gliding behind them. The party left with a substantial haul.
