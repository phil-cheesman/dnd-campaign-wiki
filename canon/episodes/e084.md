# E84 — The Spires of the Kraken

## Summary
Noctis's sending stones buzzed: Maximilian "Max" Taldross was calling. Max sent Oran, a dwarf Irishman he can call or control via a badge, toward Iron Forge, then had the party create a teleportation circle on their bed mats and teleported them to Iron Forge in Wintervail — a plateau mountainside with seven iron towers, swarming with Gidian guards in full plate. Max was going rogue and needed help: six weeks earlier, Adune and the Righteous Hand had tasked him and a hand-picked group of about twelve — "the Fist" — to learn what the Luzonovians were truly up to by any means necessary. The Luzonovians, teaming with the Archivists in search of technological inventions and a source of ultimate power, had flipped a leading chemist in the Depths and assaulted the Spires of the Kraken, the mine's 300–400-foot entrance guard towers. The party retook the Spire — ball bearings, Misty Step, and a 40-foot diving tackle from Max — and freed Max's captured scouts Roland Blaze and Everdak. Max, now in their debt, promised a handsome reward and warned he would need them again within days to save the mine.
