# E42 — Big Q and Cruucar Go to Whitecastle

## Summary
The session centered on a trapped puzzle door bearing five locks, each with a hand and key beneath it and inscriptions in different languages, ringed by roughly twenty gas canisters embedded in the walls — with "Pick in order or not at all" written over the whole thing. The party took poison damage when the door exploded. Working through the lock riddles ("2 or 4", "Never trust a goblin", "Orcs can't count", etc.), they solved the order — fifth lock first, then second, fourth, first, third — and the door opened for them.
