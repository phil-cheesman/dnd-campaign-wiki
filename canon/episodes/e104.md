# E104 — Into the Vault of Izdar

## Summary
Guided by Wahle, the party descended into the Great Rift. The first two days were uneventful; on day three they reached the bottom of the Rift — a vast expanse of naturally carved spires rising 10,000 feet — running half a day ahead of schedule. On day five Wahle briefly got lost before finding the path again, and Berrian tried mapping their southwest route. As they made camp, a ruined ancient temple appeared before them — possibly the Deep, or the Vault — with something evil permeating the place. A massive intelligent snake creature and six lesser snake creatures attacked; the party killed the main snake, with Cruucar taking heavy damage. Wahle drew a return map and departed. Berrian found nine large obsidian god-symbols, and the party pushed through a rancid slot canyon to an alcove with a fiendish-aura obelisk bearing Izzdar the Undying's warning inscription and a chained book listing nine bad gods. When Vane opened the book, a breath exhaled from a side room.
