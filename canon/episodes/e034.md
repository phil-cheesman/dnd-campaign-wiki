# E34 — Way of Water

## Summary
The party set sail with Adune from Kierkenwall aboard the Tides Breath, a 100-foot man-of-war captained by James Cook with a crew of twenty (ten sailors, ten marines) and six gun ports. They learned of the Runekeepers — a blunt-instrument adventuring party used for dirty, less discrete work: Lora Fel (elf), Amara (dwarf), Talen (cleric), and an unknown fourth member. The Sundering book's code was cracked; each of its 33 pages takes about an hour to read. Leaving the bay, they passed the lighthouse holding the great Blue Sapphire gem. Captain Jarold, a shipping captain out of Jazacha, met with Cook and Adune and relayed a story: Luzonovians have been campaigning in Jazacha, where a Gidian party found a perfectly flat alien-like oval plane in the jungle with an impenetrable temple. One of twenty men got partway in and came out changed; something was brought out of the temple, crated, and whisked by magic to somewhere in Gidia. A man injured in the ensuing Gidian–Luzonovian fight died of old age at 22, three days later.
