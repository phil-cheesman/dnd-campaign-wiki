# E26 — Shopping Spree

## Summary
A preparation episode in Grellier. Noctis (based out of Autumn Fall) met Jacobo, a short chubby olive-skinned man with a ponytail who held maps of slave-trader routes — when to dock, when to move — with focus on the slave trade out of Freetown, wealthy merchants in Carasia, and sketchy overland trade routes. Milo the Wise Owl was introduced. At the weapons store the party learned the weapon "Danger" carries a minor enchantment ritually driven by blood, and that the cloak's connection must be severed or its person brought back to life. The party met the nervous Duke Athinien, who worried something would happen in Grellier. Salem, high priest of Paylor, led them to the teleportation citadel — effectively an embassy and the only teleportation between Trenes — and the party was spirited away by teleportation circle to Kierkenwall, landing in the Knights of Gidia stronghold. They took rooms at the Nessel Nook Inn to prepare for the next morning's audience with the King, with Max staying alongside them.
