# E57 — Ocean's Five

## Summary
The party ran the heist on Marquise Avesy's manor vault. Cracking the gem mechanism took three attempts — wrong gem pairings triggered "DIE" results, and the party was hit with poison, lightning, fire, and cold damage before the statues came alive. While some of the party killed statues, the other three members entered the house and basement and opened the vault door. Cruucar smashed a display case, ruining the stealth approach and waking a statue into the fight; Noctis unlocked the other case and pulled the first three items — a lute, a small golden figurine, and a chalice — with three more items taken, and Cruucar grabbing a nice dagger. The stone golem statue proved nearly invulnerable, halving roughly everything thrown at it and shrugging off many damage types, so the party fled instead: Noctis in gaseous form, Zanim as a spider, Evac and Cruucar by teleport, and Quinton flying out the second-floor window as a fly — spotting Marquise Avesy, the owner, on the way out. They needed to escape to Le Kraken and meet disguised at seven bells for breakfast.
