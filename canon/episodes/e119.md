# E119 — Paul Bunyon & the Boys

## Summary
Orgmond shared lore with the party: the planes here are dark and chaotic, and the Shadowfell's foul magic and diseases erode the mind the longer one stays — shadow dragons dwell there. He sends trees downstream to people who need them and collects special, unique trees: the Immanue tree (purple, strong sinew, for special weapons) and the Yaro tree (very resilient, used for halls and war shields). The Arbor Alma was placed by the gods at the end of the Sundering. Orgmond is from the far north; the sickness is coming from something dark and evil deep in the forest. The Forest of Correlian turned into the Vermalock, and Orgmond's father fought against the evils and fell in the Sundering. His ox is named Torbruh. Near the waterfall, two large winged creatures with black shimmering skin attacked — Shadowfell Wyverns, cousins to dragons, common around Orgmond's home Zyldron but different from most. In the fight Berrian took hits and healed Quinton with Lay on Hands, while Cruucar, poisoned and badly battered, killed a Wyvern and two undead-ish Displacer Beasts and drove off two Dead Ogres.
