# E161 — Descent Through the Pyramid

## Summary
Fleeing into the Great Sun Temple's depths, the party latched and rope-tied the trap-door room's door while Berrian prayed to Malora and raised a 7th-level bulwark of jungle roots against it, then jumped after Mallie Grisham — into minutes of pitch-black interplanar free-fall where wings, teleports, and Dancing Lights all failed. Vane's Dispel Magic materialized the floor beneath them. After Torgoth healed the party, Mallie revealed the temple has five floors, that he will leave the party on the fourth, and that the fifth holds what they seek. Floor one was a serpent sanctuary holding eleven Yuan-Ti — broodguards mid-ritual around a pool, malisons, unknown arcane "temple priests," and a whip-and-scimitar pit master. After an hour of plans, the party recast Seeming as Medusas (Quinton, Torgoth) and temple guards, with Tongues on Quinton and the Rod of Lordly Might posing as an artifact bound for the vault; Quinton intimidated and bluffed them straight through. An endless descending stair's corporeal film then stripped the Seeming. On floor two they ambushed two Yuan-Ti abominations and two arcane "nightmare speakers" — fireballs, Noctis's arrows, Cruucar's rage — until a six-headed Hydra erupted from the pool. Quinton Disintegrated it for 112; Berrian burned everything to a crisp with his sword's first Incendiary Cloud charge, dedicated to Max. The party fled the remaining giant constrictor snakes down the stairs, Berrian rear-guarding with his ring's Fear.
