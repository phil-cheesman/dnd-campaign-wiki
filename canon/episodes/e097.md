# E97 — A Soul for a Soul

## Summary
In Avernus, Cruucar sent all four souls back to the GateKeeper — "a soul for a soul." The party reached Bell's Forge: a ring of stone wall around a red stone tower rising 400–500 feet, studded with rusted spikes, with the temple itself up in the clouds reached by a switchback on the mountainside. To infiltrate, a couple of party members disguised themselves as devils while the rest put on shackles and posed as slaves, joining the pilgrimage of devils marching humans, strange humanoids, Dragonborn, Valkadans, and Minotaurs to the temple of blood sacrifice ("fuel the temple"). Vane shapeshifted into a busty Cambion. Devil checkpoints inspected blood-red badges to let people pass; the party grabbed a chain devil for its papers, but a checkpoint guard immediately killed it, and when Noctis teleported back and handed a badge to the horned devils they knew it was fake. After causing a commotion and circling the mountain, the party found two small entrances 100 feet up — one dripping ooze and blood, the other full of sewage — and Noctis led everyone crawling up the waste shaft, "the back door."
