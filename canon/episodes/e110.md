# E110 — The Death of Beltraz

## Summary
The party fought and killed the beholder Beltraz the Unseen in the orb room of the Vault of Izdar. Moonbeam was cast in the middle of the room, and Cruucar regained substantial health. Berrian flew in on the magic carpet, jumped off, and grabbed one of nine tentacles. Benjamin died in the fight. Noctis ran up the wall, jumped off, hit the beholder four times, and stunned it; the beholder was finished when Berrian punched through its body. Benjamin's bag of holding held a shield and a magic spear of lightning. Afterward the party painted a decoy magic door, then painted bricks over where the real door was, reinforcing with steel beams and bricks using 200 square feet of paint, and took a long rest with the area still left to loot.
