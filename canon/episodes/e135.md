# E135 — The War-Council Trap

## Summary
After Berrian's return, the party learned more about the Arch Dragon of Aum Shai and the politics between Gidia and Tarlif. Sir Trent Hightower, persuaded by Adune, had sent Max and the others on a mission to kill or capture "Berrian," a member of the Mageocracy ruling Tarlif under the Arch Dragon. With Cruucar's help the group reached the 6th level of the tower and confronted Nardif Darksi, who revealed himself as the Nightmare King — the same foe from the Iron Forge mines. He questioned them about their artifacts, including the rod from the Vault of Izdar and the Rod of Attenuation, and admitted he was brought into Aum Shai to destroy the Archivists and the old order. The Seal of Aum Shai revealed Sir Trent as the mole leading the conspiracy. Max's father smuggled the fugitive party into the capital, and they infiltrated the king's war council disguised under Waldo Taldross's identity to expose Trent. Before the reveal, a wall of force trapped the party and a devastating spell killed many — including King Felix. The party was framed as the assassins; Max's whole family burned to ash. They retreated to his grandfather's mausoleum, where Max claimed his ancestor's sword.
