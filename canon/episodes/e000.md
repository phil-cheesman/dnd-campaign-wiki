# E0 — Char Creation

## Summary
Session zero: character creation and worldbuilding for the Alambor campaign. The DM laid out the setting 499 years after the Sundering, when the Zeeven Dynasty of drow defied the gods. The gods gave the nations to the world powers: the Gidia empire (British-style trade monarchy), its vassal islands of Mandalonia (manual labor; slavery outlawed in Gidia proper), the free-state confederacy of Dalacia, the elven Chrision Empire, the dwarven Morian Kingdom, mage-ruled Paratha, the inward-looking Luzonovia empire with vassals Treaborg and the Vithian Alliance, paladin-heavy Zildran, mysterious Wey, lizard-folk Jazacha, and the pirate kleptocracy of the Bandit Shores. Texair — transmutation-magic currency made and distributed by the Archivists, keepers of the Arbor Alma tree's spice — underpins the economy. The pantheon once held 16–20 gods; many died in the Sundering, leaving six good and six evil. The party begins arriving by boat at the Mandalonia islands, vassal of Gidia.
