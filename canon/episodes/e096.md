# E96 — Let It Go, The Death of Olf

## Summary
A haunting buzzing announced human-sized hell wasps, 500–600 feet away and up. The party ran a skill challenge to escape — Berrian took damage on a failed acrobatics check — and the wasps chased them to a cave entrance, where Vane and Noctis pulled a Minor Illusion coyote fake-wall trick and the wasps hit the rock fall. Inside, Berrian hit the ground with two swords at his neck; seven heavily armed humans had blades at every throat ("Don't fucking move"). Their leader Harden, a one-eyed man from the Swords Coast, was hunting a noble of Boulder's Gate tied to an insurrection. He shared ways out of Avernus — Fort Knucklebow many days' march southwest, the teleportation circle at the Bronze Citadel, and the Pillar of Skulls — and warned that Tiamat, the five-headed chromatic dragon god, dwells in Avernus. Harden suggested Olf might be lying; Zone of Truth confirmed Olf had lied about his soul. Cruucar immediately attacked Olf and took his soul (four total), then flew into a soul-collection rage and attacked Grady, dropping to 9 HP before Max's Lesser Restoration brought him back. The party deescalated and paid Harden 200 gold. Fourteen hours into Avernus.
