# E158 — The Rock Titan Zuk'taal

## Summary
The party took four very fine daggers from the Yuan-Ti bodies, then snuck down the river to a pool — which turned out to be full of blood-thirsty piranhas. Vane and Quinton polymorphed into a shark and an octopus and were immediately swarmed and viciously attacked. Berrian scared the piranhas off, and Torgoth killed them with a lightning attack into the water — nearly killing Quinton and Vane (back in human form) in the process. Cruucar rode in the bag of holding while the party swam the tunnels, avoiding major peril. After a short rest they entered a cavern, hearing titanous monsters in the forests behind them. Mallie Grisham led the way but got confused at a caved-in dead end; the party cleared stones to make a small entrance. Noise set the cavern shaking, and Zuk'taal — a gigantic rock monster — pulled away from the wall and attacked. The fight nearly wiped the party: constant grapples, crushing rocks, the path blocked, and at one point all but one character down, saved only by amazing death saving throws. Mallie used a Wish spell stored in a bracelet to wish the creature away, saving the day but losing the bracelet, leaving him super dejected.
