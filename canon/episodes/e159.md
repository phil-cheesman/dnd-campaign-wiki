# E159 — Vane's Infiltration

## Summary
Mallie Grisham had business back in Oojezz. The party learned that the Yuan-Ti love magical artifacts — they would have to fight or trade something extremely valuable to get the artifact back. The Yuan-Ti are more intelligent than the lizard folk and control the animals with enchanted stones on necklaces; the Yuan-Ti are active in daytime, while the lizard folk patrol day and night and are more nocturnal. Along the route toward Oojezz the party heard a patrol coming down the trail and had to fly over the thick hedgerows and bamboo to hide. Noctis couldn't fly in time and clumsily ran only 20–30 feet up the bamboo before the creatures rounded the bend, but hid well; Cruucar went back for him and couldn't see him. The patrol: a huge-jawed crocodilian lizard monster loping and sniffing, six large 8–9 foot lizard folk behind it (all with glowing chains), and a Yuan-Ti riding a lizard creature. They reached Oojezz, a city in a hot, humid bowl-shaped valley between two fang-like mountains, waterfalls spilling from the mountains, jungle and buildings interwoven, built in three rings with a great temple at the center.
