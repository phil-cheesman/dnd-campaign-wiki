# E139 — Return of the Berrian the Raker

## Summary
Tasked jointly by Queen Beatrice of Carasia and the King of Luzonovia to learn everything possible about Adune, the party reached the Archivists' Minas Tirith-like citadel, where Reinhard Heimlick spoke to the Luzonovian guard and won them passage to the High Architect Zenith. Master Vel Alerias laid out the rules of the wild, Titus escorted them through Donum Vite — a city grown from living trees — and at Loflorien the High Architect answered their questions. Adune is Morgenrath Gafar, a 1,200-year-old drow lich who seeks to resurrect the Zeeven by opening a gate to another dimension and now holds four of the six relics; the "power source" is the sentience of Omago, chief architect of the Hive, a fragment of whom now empowers Morgenrath. The party learned the lore of the Sundering and of Potentium, raw divine power. Berrian filled an empty council seat, and when he touched the root of the Arbor Alma all noise went silent: "Welcome back, Berrian." Noctis's report set the next task: locate the Cloak of Displacement, the sixth and final relic.
