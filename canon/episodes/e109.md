# E109 — Opening Beltraz's Lair

## Summary
Continuing through the Vault of Izdar after the Tharizdun room, the party entered the Tiamat room through a jackal-mouth door. Vane ripped off a red vine; an eye popped out and petrified him. A puzzle opened the sarcophagus, yielding more eyes and five large apple-sized gems. In an ankle-deep water room they found six paintings of Hive archmages — Ninath (evocation), Faldryal (illusion), Zarryien (enchantment), Ilharius (conjuration), Velonim (abjuration), and a tall bald drow — paired with six weapons; Vane grabbed the first spear. Further rooms held a hieroglyph flip book inside a massive gold pocket watch, a stone font of green liquid with a shadow that mirrored Berrian's movements, and a chamber of five pillars with a 15-foot fiend. Having collected all ten eyes, the party used them on the mirror, opening a 50-foot-high room with nine treasure alcoves, hundreds of candles, an ice-smooth floor, and a heavily magnetized sphere hovering under a black silk drape — the lair of the beholder Beltraz the Unseen, whose maniacal cackle taunted them as the fight began.
