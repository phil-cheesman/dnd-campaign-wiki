# E88 — Mallory's Magic Mirror

## Summary
The party scavenged Airdale, recovering potions, scrolls, a yellow gem, and rope, then boated to Corwell Keep — blasted in, its rocks acid-burned, the ground boggy with strange plant growth. A Blackwood guard on the wall confronted them and a skirmish followed in which Benjamin went down and Berrian took heavy cold damage; the party tied up a guard and Berrian gave him 1 HP of Lay on Hands. Inside they found twelve prisoners, including Able Foster ("the Bear") and the dying Baron with LJ. LJ had wounded the dragon, which took Elizabeth Janis and fled to the Bandersnatch Mountains. With Mallory dead, LJ's and the Baron's minds cleared; the Baron died, leaving Leon in control, who promised the party Blackwood's Manor if they slay the dragon and return his mother. In Mallory's lair they found a blood magic book, a dragon lore book, and an odd full-body mirror — a portal into Blackwood's Manor's upper chambers.
