# E137 — Two Months Later

## Summary
Two months passed at sea, Yankee Williams and the crew flying army colors. The party made the Mandalonia island capital as the Gidian Navy made port, and word of the dead king spread fast. The journey toward the Luzonovian capital of Rynestag — a causeway atop bluffs amid crow-darkened woodlands — crossed the carnage of the great battle of Bootenhill: Carasian and Luzonovian dead dotted the 50 miles of road, the Carasians having overwhelmed the Luzonovians at mass cost, then been bottlenecked into camp by a trap at Airhark Lake where the enemy tried to starve them out. Queen Beatrice, King Rupert the Young, and an Archivist advisor (the one who helped the party with the prince) awaited an audience with the Alambor Five — so quiet you could hear a pin drop. Peace held between Luzonovians and Carasians, who looked to group up against the Gidians. In a swampy area the party met Reinhard Heimlick, advisor to the Archivists, and using the airship Cruucar secured, were sent to drop off at Feldbrook with Reinhard aboard.
