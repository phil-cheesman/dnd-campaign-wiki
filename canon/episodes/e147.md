# E147 — Sink or Swim: Wrecked at the Reef

## Summary
The party learned about the Pirate Trinity — a coalition of pirate captains and their fleets, a mockery of the Trine — and the Sparrow, the symbol of a pirate crew linked to the ship Fortune's Favor under Captain Mallie Grisham, who makes berth at Anchors Rest and confirms the final artifact is in pirate hands. Every five years the pirates gather at Krakens Reef for a council, possibly now. The party left Koru behind. Two enemy vessels pursued: a large single-deck ship with dragon-like wings as sails, and another with black skulls and a huge ram — Zax, with six main leaders and roughly thirty hired mercenaries aboard the Black Widow. Despite successful repairs to pitch, ropes, and mast, the rolls turned terrible: the ship hit a reef, the hull was blown out, and the party was broadsided from afar (the attackers missed entirely). They abandoned ship, convincing Yankee not to go down with it, and ferried everyone to high cliffs by lifeboat, carpet, and Gregory 2.0. Shipless and under cannon fire, they learned Kieran had been captured and put on the hunting ship of the Merchant.
