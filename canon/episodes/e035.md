# E35 — Wrecked @ the Wreef

## Summary
The party pieced together the story of the Black Widow (the "Black Veil") — a pirate ship whose crew wear a woman's-face tattoo, work the slave routes around south Jazacha and Freetown, and have worked with both Cruucar's old enemies the Widowmakers and his arch nemesis Zax. The ship's employer, Captain Vex Totem, lost his crew deep in Jazacha hunting an artifact, then launched a new quest for "something of immense power in the sea." Zax joined him: a key was found on the Vithian coast at heavy cost, Vex and Zax returned from an inland temple covered in blood, and Zax then stole the scroll and ran — each side left holding only half of what they needed. Searching the ship, the party recovered an enchanted singing rapier (to Noctis) and belt, three baseball-sized carved god-eye orbs (obsidian, amber, jade) radiating intense abjuration magic, maps, books — including "Cloven," about the crystals that may control the imprisoned sea god Uquatoa — and a magical Maritime Table that projects a 30-mile hologram. Overnight, Zanim's obsidian orb vanished.
