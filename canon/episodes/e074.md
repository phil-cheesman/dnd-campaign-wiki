# E74 — The Tithes Were Not in Vain

## Summary
Evac dreamed of falling into water and being pulled by a purposeful current toward the temple, entering through an eye-doorway into darkness where a massive single-slit snake's eye said "Return, Return" — then, seeming more displeased, "Disavow" — before tendrils grabbed him; he woke vomiting seawater and the orb. After breakfast at the keep, the party trailed Vane and Thomas from the well to an old abandoned farmhouse a quarter mile outside the city. They learned Atwell had ridden out that morning to meet Blackwood's guards near Tamworth, aiming for the Shimmering Grotto, and linked up with the rebellion agent Betty. With Gregory and an invisible, carpet-borne Evac scouting, they intercepted Atwell's armored wagon train at a wooden bridge over a huge chasm. Seven guards — three of them invisible — and an enemy wizard fought back. Evac died in the fight and the enemy mage escaped; the bridge collapsed, but the party saved the wagon, found Atwell burned to a crisp, and opened it to find eight chests holding a fortune in gold and silver plate.
