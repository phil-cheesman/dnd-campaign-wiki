# E69 — Enter The Sandman

## Summary
After a recap — the ship left in Yankee's hands, Quinton off to find his mother (planning to return to Kierkenwall to see Adune), General Barnabas leading forces to the battle north of Kingswatch, and the party past Murkwell into the Grasping Moore — the mud creature attacked. The Sandman-like hydra grew from five heads to seven before the party killed it, with Evac hurling repeated Fireballs. That night Noctis tried to slip the cursed dagger back into Cruucar's belt; Cruucar noticed, sowing distrust in the group. On day three the party exited the swamp without incident into golden-brown rolling fields near Tornsworth, a logging and shipbuilding town. While they camped, nine "hunters" killed Gregory and Luna. The party pressed on into Evershire — the oldest Gideon settlement, a beautiful lake valley once decimated by the "Black Dread," perhaps a dragon or plague — toward Airdale in the Embry Basin. Around midday they spotted a small town below in the valley, burning, with a woman's screams, a man's loud cry, and a woman dead in a ditch.
