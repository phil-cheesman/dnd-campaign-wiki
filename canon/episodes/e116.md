# E116 — Jameti Comes Clean

## Summary
The party inventoried their sellable gems and treasure and shopped for potions of supreme healing and a scroll of invisibility. They went to Jameti Costco's house, breaking in next door while Vane and Berrian went to the door; the house looked abandoned, but his satchel held maps of Tarlif with locations crossed off — apparently a search for the Vault. Jameti entered and came clean: Rasheed Soltar and Tinkertop are Aum Shai Vipers, with Tinkertop the mastermind and Nardif Darksi the Arch Dragon sitting on the counsel. The party's items can open a portal larger than anyone can — to the Feywild and Shadowfell. Jameti had found the White Knights, the Knights of Theydune, in the Shadowfell, and had led both the Arch Dragon and Berrian's father to them. A gateway lies at Moonbright, Berrian's hometown north of Tarlif, where a stream forks at a waterfall pool. Noctis, owing a promise to master Koru, arranged with Shaahim's Harpers to send Jameti's wife and two kids by ship to Kierkenwall, to Taldross and Adune. The party vowed to return to end Aum Shai here and end the slave trade.
