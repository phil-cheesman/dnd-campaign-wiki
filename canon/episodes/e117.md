# E117 — Marora at the Stream

## Summary
The party spent the day gearing up in Tarlif. Siad, who runs the Flying Carpet, sent them to Cumalinger for the best enhancement prices, and Yamean pointed them to the Jade Oricle in the South Sun Ward. There, the old craftsman Master Mirral Kariem explained the Galadon trees of the Vermalock — purple trees with gold leaves that grow only where the planes are thin, with natural protections and animals that guard them with their lives — and offered to craft from a six-foot branch of center-tree wood for 10,000 gold and ten days. The party spent an apple-sized gem and came away with wings of flying, a ruby ring, a Pearl of Power for Torgoth, greater healing potions, an Amulet of Health, and a good saw. By midnight they were in place across the river to head for the portal, camped well, and long rested with temporary hit points all around. Berrian felt at home, but the stream was covered in shifting, multicolored, invasive moss and fungus, and a half-zombie moose came down the river. Then, behind them, an Irish accent: "Oh jesus Berrian what are you doing back here" — Berrian's sister.
