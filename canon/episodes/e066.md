## Summary
In the monster's stomach the party recovered the canister — inside, a coded message bearing the pressed Lion seal of Gidia at the bottom. The party loaded all the texair into the Portable Hole — some put on the fire turned into very small rocks. At the town center, General Barnabas of the North Armies received them. The Widowmakers have fallen out of favor with Gidia — do not wear the mask. A red dragon dwells in the upper Callum Heights. This episode marks Berrian's arrival: his mother is very sick and needs a great deal of texair.
