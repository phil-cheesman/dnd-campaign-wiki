# E51 — THE AFTERMATH

## Summary
In the aftermath of the wedding assassination, Queen Beatrice addressed the people outside while the party was taken into a side room. They learned that Aum Shai is the name by which the Shadowed Moon organization is known — it is embedded in multiple organizations, and certain arcane effects mark its members. Justin had placed the band on the high archivist before stabbing the prince, and the portal may have led to the Shadow Plane. The high archivist trusts the party. After long rests (and a dream for Zanim), the queen declared the party unwelcome in her kingdom unless they bring her son back from the dead or bring those responsible for his death to justice. People were put on trial for the murder, and Robert Kavney placed four letters in the queen's hands. Blaming Luzonovia for the prince's death, she sent her son's body off on a boat, fired a flaming arrow to ignite it, and declared war on Luzonovia.
