# E165 — Gate to the Astral Sea

## Summary
A short, campaign-bending session. It opened mid-cliffhanger: the **Siren's Call** — the Merchant's 35-gun ship, a full deck taller than the party's 10-gun **Resurrect at the Reef** — threw three grappling lines and boarding bridges onto the starboard rail, six or seven miles off the Jazacha coast on a sunny late afternoon. The enemy's opening volleys were brutal: marksmen shot **Quickstep in the face** and put two bullets (one a nat-20, 40 total) into **Quinton, dropping him at the top of round one**. Torgoth answered with **Control Water**, parting a 100-foot cube of sea to drop the Siren's Call into the trench — but the **Defiled Gunslinger** poured three shots into the visibly-casting goliath and a single 66-damage hit **downed Tor**, ending the spell before the ship ever dipped. Yankee's hard-to-port ship action snapped two of the three boarding bridges; **Noctis popped the third off the rail with the Rod of Lordly Might's extending ladder**, flinging a marine into the sea, and blanketed the boarding melee in **Darkness** cast on his night sword. A full broadside then raked the deck (27 damage, Dex for half) and **felled the main mast**; marines stabbed the downed **Daylor** where he lay and beat **Blackfuse** to within an inch. Cruucar — at **1 HP**, raging — flew the **carpet of flying** to Tor's body and heaved him aboard. Then the Merchant himself teleported from his ship to the stump of the mast and offered terms: *"Lay down your weapons. Your wounded will receive treatment, your captain will live, and every sailor still breathing will remain so."* The party's answer was the play they had been quietly building all night: Vane — who had already Mage-Handed the **orb of Felnriel** out of the Bag of Holding to inspect it — ran to his gathered friends and **threw the Bag of Holding into the party's cauldron (?)**, invoking the item text: bag placed inside an extradimensional space → **both items destroyed, a one-way gate to the Astral Plane, every creature within 10 feet sucked through**. All six PCs were inside the circle. Jon, delighted and blindsided ("this is more fun"), let it ride — with one devastating catch: **a Mage Hand is not a creature. The hand — and the orb it held — stayed behind**, left on the deck under the Merchant's furious gaze as Vane gave him two middle fingers on the way out. The party materialized drifting in the **Astral Sea** — purple cosmos, a distant leviathan, movement by mind (3 × Intelligence score in feet) — where Berrian's nat-20 perception picked out a **gigantic floating city** approaching on a shelf of continent, its banners bearing **a crescent moon ringed by seven stars over a white flower**: the symbol Noctis remembered from the tombs beneath Sutrey's catacombs. Jon confirmed it: **the Zevan Dynasty, banished from the world by the gods, floating in the astral sea** — the very thing Adune is trying to bring back. Tor (nat-20) and Quinton (18, third success) stabilized adrift; Jon ended the session early — "I have nothing prepared for this" — as the city drifted closer. "You have stumbled upon something that you are not ready for."

## Key events
- **Round one, boarding action:** three grappling hooks and drawbridge-style boarding ramps slammed onto the Resurrect at the Reef from the higher deck of the Siren's Call (≥35 guns vs the party's 10). Enemy marksmen ("gunslingers") opened on the closest targets: **Quickstep shot in the side of the face** (nat-20, 29 to hit), **Blackfuse hit for 16**, and **Quinton downed** by 27 (nat-20) + 13 piercing from 33 HP (?).
- **Torgoth's Control Water:** a 100×100-ft parting of the sea placed to drop the Siren's Call 100 feet into a trench; Jon ruled the parting gradual ("the Red Sea… it has to move up and over") and re-castable each round to maintain. The enemy ship had bridged the gap at the bow and never dipped before the spell ended.
- **Tor downed:** the **Defiled Gunslinger** (the lead marksman) targeted the conspicuously spell-dancing Torgoth at 80 feet — one hit of the three, with everything pumped into it: **66 damage, down**, concentration broken, the sea refilling.
- **The crew fought for their ship:** **Yankee** steered hard to port (contested Strength, +3 vs the bigger ship's +5, rolled 14) and **snapped two of the three boarding bridges**; **Razortail** waded in fangs-and-claws to cover Daylor (17 damage); **Ronkros** dive-bombed a marine for 20 and wheeled back into the sky; **Blackfuse** traded blows with boarders.
- **Berrian's turn (round 1):** Bless on himself, Tor and Vane; carried the unconscious Quinton to hard cover in the stair nook, out of the marksmen's line of sight. Jon accepted Berrian's researched ruling that his **paladin aura (+5) applies to death saving throws** — Quinton rolled all his saves at +5. (Quinton's bid to Bardically Inspire his own death saves was denied.)
- **Noctis's turn:** **Darkness** cast on his night sword over the melee (later ruled with Berrian's DMG citation that attackers **can't flank in darkness** — marines at disadvantage), then a bonus-action **Rod of Lordly Might** ladder-extension angled under the last boarding bridge — popping bridge, railing and a marine into the sea and severing the final connection between ships.
- **The broadside:** at the top of round two the Siren's Call fired a full salvo — Dex save, 27 bludgeoning/slashing/piercing (half on 12+; Quinton and Berrian fully covered; Vane +2 behind a railing) — and the **main mast collapsed and went overboard** ("the damaged main mast that you had repaired so long ago is now gone").
- **The deck fight turned:** marines flanked through the melee — **Quickstep downed** (~60 damage), **Daylor downed and stabbed on the ground**, **Blackfuse at death's door** (40 damage, "barely up"). Yankee charged into the darkness to hold the line.
- **Cruucar's rescue:** raging at **1 HP** (raged at 7, halved the broadside), he flew the **carpet of flying** 60 feet, heaved Tor's unconscious body aboard (Strength 15 with advantage — his whole action), and pulled back.
- **The Merchant's terms:** the black-cloaked, purple-turbaned figure **teleported from the Siren's Call to the party's deck** (where the mast had stood) and called for surrender: fought admirably, "far beyond the point at which admiration becomes foolishness" — weapons down, the wounded treated, the captain lives, every sailor still breathing remains so. The party never answered in words.
- **Vane inspected the orb (round 2):** Mage Hand pulled the **orb of Felnriel** from the Bag of Holding (~10 lb, within the hand's limit) — a crystalline orb with a **deep black center** and sparking energy, unchanged since the temple. "I'm not going to touch it. I'm too scared."
- **THE PLAY — the bag and the cauldron:** with the party bunched within 10 feet (Berrian: "we're taking this!"), **Vane threw the Bag of Holding into the party's cauldron (?)** — invoking the rules text: a bag of holding placed inside an extradimensional space **destroys both items and opens a one-way gate to the Astral Plane, sucking in every creature within 10 feet**. Jon verified the text at the table, noted the held enemy actions he'd waived ("I fudged the rules for you and I paid for it — but this is more fun"), and ruled it worked: **all six PCs sucked through**. The bag's contents — the party's unspent platinum hoard — scattered into the Astral Plane.
- **The orb left behind:** Jon's counter-ruling — the Mage Hand is **not a creature**; neither the hand nor the orb it held passed the gate. Vane exited flipping double middle fingers at the visibly furious Merchant… and arrived empty-handed. **The orb of Felnriel lies on the deck of the Resurrect at the Reef**, in the middle of a battle the party just left. "I lost the orb. God damn it. That's six months of our lives."
- **The Astral Sea:** weightless drift "as if through heavy water," no up or down; purple wisps, stars, ethereal light everywhere; a distant **leviathan-like shape with a long tail** swimming through the void. Movement is by mind: **3 × Intelligence score feet per turn** (dash allowed; "you cannot Feline Agility your mind") — Noctis, Int 6, moves 18 feet ("Noctis basically cannot move in the astral space, which is poetic"); Berrian is 3 ft/turn faster here than on land.
- **The floating city of the Zevan Dynasty:** Berrian (nat-20 → 26 perception) and Noctis (21) made out a **gigantic city — bigger than Valon (?)** — on a floating shelf of continent, black-purple towers, drifting slowly toward them. Banners: **a small crescent moon ringed by seven stars on a black background, with a white flower in a field**. Noctis (Int check 16, with advantage) recognized the exact symbol **carved on the tombs beneath the Sutrey catacombs/Vault** — the ancient people gone from this world; "Return what is lost." Jon: **"This is the Zevan Dynasty, floating in the astral sea, banished from the world by the gods"** — the very thing Adune / Morgenrath is trying to bring back to Alambor. "You have stumbled upon something that you are not ready for."
- **Stabilizing adrift:** too spread out to reach each other in six seconds, the downed rolled it out — **Tor (?) nat-20 death save, auto-stabilized**; **Quinton rolled 18** for his third success (at Berrian's +5 aura), stable.
- **Zanim is alive** (Jon, jokes aside): not here, but alive, and could return someday — "high-level druids are honestly way more powerful than sorcerers, FYI." While the party is in the Astral Plane, though, there's no swapping characters (?).
- **Session ended early:** Jon had nothing prepped for the Astral Plane ("I need to do a fuck-ton of preparation… that was a hell of a move"). **Quinton and Vane rolled Arcana** for astral-plane knowledge; Jon will send Quinton information. Party intentions given to Jon: explore, get their bearings, learn what the Zevan Dynasty wants with the material world — likely by **posing as acolytes of the Hive** using what they learned in the Vault (Berrian's plan).
