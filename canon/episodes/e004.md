# E4 — The Carstone Jobs

## Summary
News confirmed the royal wedding: Princess Anabel of Gidia (the king's daughter) would marry Prince Thomas of Carasia. For the bandit job the party received 50 gold and guaranteed passage up the river with Leon. They bought rations at Jemis General Store, then split up. Noctis and Zook visited the viscount, Jon-Pierre — a tall half-elf with long blonde hair and rich clothes — and haggled to 100 gold to find whoever was interfering with his wine delivery, taking an unopened letter to bring along. Quinton and Zanim called on the widow whose merchant husband Jon had gone missing a day past due. The party followed the road north and found Jon surrounded by gnolls, which they killed; Jon still had his letter and gold, though his horses were dead — and the letter turned out to be a personal one from a lady friend. The party stayed the night with a couple, got free breakfast, and met Leon Steadyfoot (7'8", 430 lbs) and the tiny jacked halfling Rolin Blaze at the boat bearing a chisel-and-saw carpentry sigil.
