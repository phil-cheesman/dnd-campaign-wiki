## Summary
The party looted the beholder's treasure alcoves in the Vault of Izdar — gems, plates and vases, an evocation rod with a knife on the end, an abjuration blue sapphire ring from the throne room, four apple-sized rubies, and the spellbook of Comara Blackfire. Quinton kept the mace, Benjamin's dead body held the ruby ring, and Noctis took bracers and a quiver of rotting arrows found with a bunch of paintings. Moving to the fourth floor's main area, Berrian went through the mirror room and Noctis entered a cell room, and the party met Jameti Costco. Downstairs, a chest, an Indiana Jones-style boulder, and an acid pit hid a key. Berrian was teleported into a maze, where he grabbed a golden key, while the rest fought bone minotaurs — Beevus cast Wish to kill six of the nine. In the Gruumsh room of 64 clay orc warriors, the party cast Silence, teleported to the sarcophagus, and opened it, finding a massive mummified, bound, single-eyed orc along with gold, moonstones, and a massive axe given to Cruucar.
