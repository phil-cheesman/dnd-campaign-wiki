# E108 — Potentium Some Information

## Summary
In the Vault of Izdar, the party fought eight ogre zombies and a one-eyed mask creature, with Cruucar taking heavy damage. In Vecna's room ("Never reveal all that you know"), an amulet found inside a hand was given to Berrian, and bowing to the faceless statue sent the zombies back into the cauldron. In a water-font room, an image showed a hulking patchwork flesh golem patrolling, and Benjamin found himself looking through the creature's eye before something exploded out in front of the party. Having now visited the Lolth, Tiamat, Zehir, Vecna, and Bane rooms, they entered a study where a dead robed figure stirred: Withers, also called Gourah, a guard of the Vault from before "the time of corruption," now serving Izzdar the Lith. He dissolved a Texair coin, calling it "weak, diluted," then attacked; the party killed him and he turned to dust. His desk and moldy journal yielded Comara Blackfire's spellbook and lore on the Order of Torag, the Hive, the bad gods, and Potentium — a self-sustaining arcane energy harnessing all seven arcane arts.
