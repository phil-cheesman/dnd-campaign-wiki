# Ring of X-ray Vision

A ring first catalogued in the inventory of Arcane Infinity, the Kierkenwall magic shop staffed by Vaxoldon Vortily Simulacrums, alongside the Rod of Lordly Might, Rope of ArchMagi, Efreeti Chain, Sword of Answering, two wands, and the Ring of Evasion (E101); the party bought from that stock (per glossary). Its fate became a running Gloomville joke: separated from the party in the Shadowfell gang city, Cruucar paid the Ring of X-ray Vision to the front-gate guards to get inside (E123). When the rest of the party — Vane, Berrian, Valinor, Torgoth, and Quinton — arrived at the bridge, Quinton enthralled the guard Crass with his lute, spun a story about children's diseases and a charity, and got the group through by promising to pay the front gate double what Cruucar had paid — i.e., double the X-ray ring (E123, E124). The party then reached the Desperado and gambled their way into two rooms while Cruucar settled into life as a kingpin Clary dealer.
