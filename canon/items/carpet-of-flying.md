# Carpet of Flying

The party's flying carpet, looted from Marquise Avesy's vault in Goldcrest alongside the Portable Hole and other treasures (E58) — though Vane later sweet-talked Elizabeth Janis onto it claiming it was "taken from an evil warlock" (E90). It is the party's workhorse mobility item. They battled the harpy-worshipped Chimera from it (E64), began the three-day crossing of the Grasping Moore by carpet (E67), and flew an invisible Evac to scout Atwell's armored wagon train (E74). Max dove 40 feet off the magic carpet to tackle a guard at the Spires of the Kraken (E84). The party rode it to Lord Blackwood's dinner after taking Viola's head (E85); Berrian and Vane floated invisible on it above Blackwood's Manor, and Vane caught the escaping party on it during the horseback pursuit (E87). It was nearly lost in the black water of the mind-fog marsh but was saved along with the giant ape (E122). During Zax's pursuit at the reef, it ferried crew from the sinking Wrecked at the Reef to the high cliffs alongside lifeboats and Gregory 2.0 (E147).

## Relationships / whereabouts
