# Pierre Beaubois's Small Leather Pocket Book

A small leather pocket book recovered from the eldest Beaubois brother, Pierre Beaubois, and sorted with the rest of the party's haul from the Beaubois Chalet alongside his ruby-eyed lion talisman, the ornately carved winged dagger, and Jeanette Beaubois's jewelry (E24). The book is partly covered in blood, burned and slashed, so only some of its entries remain legible; what survives is a ledger of Pierre's dealings on behalf of the Auumshai, recording contacts, meeting dates, shipment quantities, and coin in and out (E24). The surviving entries tie Pierre's network to the Vontrice family, the texair-shipper Lorenzo Stansport, General Zax of the Widowmakers, and the witch Viola — all later confirmed as Aum Shai threads in play (E24). The intelligence drawn from the book (along with the recovered letters) was passed by Ralph Ironfist and Ottam to King Felix, to be relayed to Queen Beatrice (E24).

## Legible entries

- **Vontrice** — a meeting dated 19 Pelius 499.
- **Lorenzo** — records of shipments coming in by quantity and coin going out, spanning dates from 22 Morane 497 to 29 Avandi 499; a note that "the Baron is the keeper of texair," and that there had been trouble for the last several years but it has been better since Lorenzo took over.
- **Zax** — information on troop numbers, location, cost, and speed of deployment; a meeting dated 4 Avandi 499.
- **Violia** (Viola) — an order placed 42 Avandi, sent via rider on agreeable terms, to be available for drop at a location on 2 Pelius 499.
