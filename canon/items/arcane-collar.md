# Arcane Collar

A long blueish-gold chain kept in Mallie Grisham's collector's cabin — the device that once contained the Kraken. Mallie explained that the Kraken was tamed long ago by a pirate mage named Maggie, a Calypso-like figure with an empathetic connection to the beast, using this arcane collar; control afterwards runs through a scepter Mallie holds, and one must speak the right language and perform the right magic (E153). The plan: wrap the chain around the Kraken's tail or neck and clasp it. In the Kraken's lair 60,000 feet down, Noctis tied the magic rope to the chain and teleported in to collar the beast but was caught at the last second (E154). In the finished fight, the Siren of the Sea necklace produced three Noctises; the last connected the chain across to the other side, and the orb on the chain changed colors and morphed into a working chain. Noctis was swallowed but held on — critical to success (E155). The Kraken was taken alive, the chain collar secured around its neck, and controlled via Mallie's scepter; Mallie then delivered Kraken control (scepter authority) to the Pirate Trinity at Krakens Reef, fulfilling the deal (E156).
