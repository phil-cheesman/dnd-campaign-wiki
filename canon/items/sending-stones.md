# Sending Stones

Communication stones that have kept the party tethered to Maximilian "Max" Taldross and the Knights of Gidia across the campaign. Max gave Evac the first sending stone before the Lorenzo texair job, warning he did not trust Lorenzo and promising hidden backup (E27). Max used it to report from his special mission in Luzonovia — masses of armed men on the borders and coasts, the entire Knights of Gidia deployed, and instructions not to assume the worst if he went silent (E32). During the Eloise Vontrice investigation he advised by sending stone to investigate Eloise first (E40). Ahead of the royal wedding, the party acquired five additional Sending Stones worn in their ears from the Dark Flame, owing 490 gold once the contract completed (E45). Much later, Noctis felt a buzz from the sending stones when Max called to summon the party to Iron Forge, beginning the rogue "Fist" arc (E84).

## Relationships / whereabouts
At least one stone paired with Max (now dead, E136); five ear-worn stones distributed across the party. Cruucar gave his earpiece to Berrian before the Kraken dive (E154).
