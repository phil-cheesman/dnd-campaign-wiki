# Soul Leech Dagger

A demon-possessed, life-stealing dagger that has shadowed the party since the Goldcrest era. Noctis put it in the (abjuration) cauldron alongside the ship-disguise loot (E61). Identify revealed it is very clearly possessed by something — probably a demon — and requires a pact between the dagger and its wielder, which the dagger will extend if need be (E67).
