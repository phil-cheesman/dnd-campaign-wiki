# Mallory's Magic Mirror

A full-body mirror portal found in the lair of Mallory, the Baron of Evershire's blood-cleric Chief Advisor, alongside a blood magic book and a Dragon Lore & History book (E88). Cruucar saw a different room in it and his hand passed through: it opens into the upper chambers of the great hall of Blackwood's Manor — the way in the party had been looking for (E88, episode title "Mallory's Magic Mirror"). Through the mirror the party explored Blackwood's Manor, finding Viola's troop ledgers, a flying-dragon-sealed letter, and a secret room behind the Bandersnatch Mountains mural; at the hallway's end a glowing magical wardrobe held a second magic mirror opening into a massive cavern of acid pools and a huge gold pile, with Elizabeth Janis caged and the black dragon Valadon sleeping inside (E89) — the route to the dragon fight that killed it. Afterward, Tristan and Blackwood's remaining six men were pushed back through the mirror, and Noctis recovered the party's gear (E90). The mirror network thus linked Mallory's lair, Blackwoods Manor, and the dragon cavern.
