# Tuning Forks

Five planar-travel forks recovered from the Nightmare King's tower at Iron Forge. After the Nightmare King teleported away taunting "Follow to the infernal ringing," Cruucar broke open the third-floor desk and found a metal pole — a tuning fork. On a bad roll, Cruucar vanished, teleported to a rocky wasteland under a dark red sky with no sun: endless plains and glowing flammable gas (E93). Cruucar then found four more tuning forks, bringing the total to five; with no spell slots left, no one could cast Detect Magic on them, and Vane botched and lost both magic scrolls. When Adune teleported to the party and sent them to hell, Vane held three empty tuning forks and one attuned to the Material Plane (E94) — the party's eventual ticket home from Avernus, where the Gatekeeper's "soul for a soul" toll governed any exit. The forks are the campaign's established mechanism for travel between planes.

## Relationships / whereabouts
