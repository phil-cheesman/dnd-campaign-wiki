# Rod of Attenuation

A Zeben relic taken from the Vault of Izdar, carried at different times by Cruucar and Vane. In the Dark Glen of the Shadowfell, Vane brought out the rod and it rotated rapidly between purple and green — indicating Shadowfell versus Material Plane — pulsing rhythmically like the throbbing in the party's ears (E130). When the party confronted Nardif Darksi at the Shadowmoon Tower, he questioned them pointedly about their artifacts, including the rod from the Vault of Izdar and the Rod of Attenuation, and offered a trade for a meeting (E135). The truth landed at the Ethereum: the rod Cruucar carries is the 4th of the six gate keys — the Zeeven artifacts that combined can open the gate to bring back the Zeeven (E136). Adune (Morgenrath Tifar), who already held three relics, took it, bringing his count to four (E139); by E144 the Aum Shai possessed five of six, "the Vault rod among them."

## Relationships / whereabouts
Lost to Adune/Aum Shai after the Ethereum betrayal (E136). Previously held by Cruucar (per glossary, also associated with Vane, who used it in the Shadowfell).
