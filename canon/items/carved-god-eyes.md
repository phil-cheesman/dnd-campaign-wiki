# Carved God-Eyes (Obsidian / Amber / Jade Orbs)

Three baseball-sized carved eye orbs — obsidian, amber, and jade — recovered from the pirate ship chest, all with the same look and feel and radiating intense abjuration magic (E35). They were split: obsidian to Zanim, jade to Evac, amber kept in the cauldron. Zanim's obsidian orb vanished overnight on watch (E35). It revealed itself etched with a large eye; something dwells within it, and the words "Consume" and "Return" sounded in the mind. It memorized Evac and, pressed hard against his chest, disappeared into him; diving into the water, Evac saw a great shadow figure saying "Return," and afterward could cast Control Water on command — but only on the sea (E36). Evac later dreamed of the black orb pulling him to a sunken temple where an eye in a black orb-door said "Return" (E40). In a later dream a massive single-slit snake's eye said "Return, Return," then "Disavow," before tendrils grabbed him; he woke vomiting seawater and the orb (E74). The amber orb speaks to Cruucar in Zaxx's voice (per glossary); Cruucar called it out and slept with it (E40).

## Relationships / whereabouts
Obsidian: merged into Evac, expelled in E74; presumed lost with Evac's death (E75). Amber: with Cruucar. Jade: was Evac's.
