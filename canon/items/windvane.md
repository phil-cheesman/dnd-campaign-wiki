# Windvane

A jeweled scepter ending in a double-bladed spear, first seen in one of four relic alcoves in Avernus (E98). Benjamin escaped Avernus through the teleportation circle carrying the scepter spear — a weapon roughly twice his size (E99). At the Arcane Infinity magic shop it was identified as Windvane: a +2 spear dealing 1d6 extra lightning damage on hit, granting advantage on initiative and a flying speed equal to walking speed when taking the Dash action, with 1d4 charges per day (1 charge casts Lightning Bolt, DEX save DC 17) — and a flaw: the wielder becomes mercurial and unreliable ("I break my vows and plans. Duty and honor mean nothing to me.") (E101). After Benjamin died in the beholder fight (E110, per glossary), the weapon passed into the party's hands; Torgoth threw the Windvane during the battle at Rasheed Soltar's building in Mandalonia while healing Quinton with a 6th-level Healing Word (E143).
