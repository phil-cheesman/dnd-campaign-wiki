# Potentium

A cluster of blue-white crystal of unlimited energy, not of this elemental plane, with "some semblance of sentience" (E92). First surfaced as lore during the dragon Valadon's fall (E89), then as the "Titan Killer": the Luzonovians, working with the Archivists, sought it, and the Nightmare King — a Luzonovian spy scientist — slaughtered the guards; Adune's man White Lantern ("The Sparrow Sent You") hid it in the Iron Forge caverns (E91–E92). Lore calls it unlimited power to do anything (E93). Adune explained that texair pushed to full potential becomes volatile and can transform and imbue energy; myth says pushing hard enough creates an ever-growing energy source (E102). Withers's moldy journal described it as self-sustaining arcane energy harnessing all seven arcane arts (E108). The Archivists' High Architect revealed the deeper truth: Potentium is raw divine power — "the Gods are Potentium" — and the "power source" is actually the sentience of Omago, chief architect of the Hive; the desire for it caused the Sundering, and a fragment of Omago now empowers Adune/Morgenrath (E139). Max's team retrieved the crystalline power source from Avernus; Sir Trent Hightower guarded it for the Knights (E132) until Adune absorbed the energy source at the Ethereum, his eyes glowing blue (E136 per arc).
