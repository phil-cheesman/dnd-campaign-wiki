# Forged Amber

Torgoth's signature artifact, received among the gifts given to the party after the Moonbright/Shadowfell saga — when the party learned they had been gone sixty days and returned during the mid-winter festival (E131). The Forged Amber's recorded properties: it is a live lightning bolt — usable as a 10d8 lightning bolt (per glossary) — or, alternatively, it can shock a friend back to life. Torgoth's player-character entry lists wielding the Forged Amber (alongside the Windvane) as one of his defining traits, and he is the party's established reviver, having brought Vane back after his E128 death.
