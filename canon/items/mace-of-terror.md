# Mace of Terror

Quinton Shackleford's signature weapon, recovered deep in the Vault of Izdar. At the bottom of a pit beyond a chamber of carved birds and an adamantine propeller, the party found a crystal sarcophagus with three keyholes containing a mummified skull in the shape of the Bane logo and a body wrapped in chains of solid gold, holding a mace. They shattered the glass case; Cruucar grabbed the mace and then smashed the Bane-skull with it, and later used it to destroy the attacking floating humanoid skull (E106). When the Vault's magic items were sorted, the mace went to Quinton (E108-dup2 distribution: Berrian sapphire ring and amulet, Benny ruby ring, Quinton mace). Quinton kept the mace through the beholder-treasure looting on the Vault's fourth floor (E111), and it was formally listed as the "Mace of Terror — Quinton" in the Tarlif-era inventory (E115). The glossary records it as a vault mace held by Quinton, and his player-character entry names carrying the Mace of Terror among his defining traits.
