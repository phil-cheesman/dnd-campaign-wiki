# Zeben Artifacts (the Six Gate Keys)

Six ancient Zeeven relics that, combined, can open a massive arcane gate to bring back the Zeeven — the pre-Sundering drow dynasty — and restore the bad gods to power (E139, E144). Adune (Morgenrath Gafar, a surviving Zeeven lich whose wife and sons died in the Sundering) has hunted them for years. The party unknowingly carried one: at the Ethereum betrayal, Cruucar's Rod of Attenuation from the Vault of Izdar was revealed as the 4th of the six gate keys (E136). The Archivists' High Architect confirmed Adune had three artifacts and now has four, including the Rod (E139). By E144 the Aum Shai possessed five of six — the Vault rod among them, plus the artifact stolen from Hell, which Adune cracked open and absorbed, visibly transforming. The sixth was already gone when the Aum Shai raided its vault; it was identified as the Cloak of Displacement (E139) and traced to pirate hands — Mallie Grisham, who retrieved it from the Temple of Fenreal and traded it away (E144, E153, E155). It now lies deep in the Jazacha jungle with the Yuan-Ti of the city Oojezz (E156).

## Relationships / whereabouts
Five with Adune/Aum Shai; the sixth (Cloak of Displacement) with the Yuan-Ti in Oojezz, Jazacha — the party's current objective.
