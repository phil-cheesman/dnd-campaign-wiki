# Texair

Texair is the transmutation-magic spice/powder currency underpinning Alambor's economy (1 texair ≈ platinum), made and distributed by the Archivists, keepers of the Arbor Alma tree, since the Sundering (E00). The Archivists allot factions a fixed amount; the rich get the extra at year's end (E07). Carasia's hard times traced to poor texair flow from Alborama (E39). Texair drove early arcs: the Beaubois family's fall began over denied texair (E08, E09), and Lorenzo Stansport's chronically raided texair shipments — four chests of coins and seventeen thousand pounds of powder — ended in his Oni reveal and ambush (E27–E29). The party stored a mine's worth of texair in the Portable Hole; burnt texair turns into very small rocks (E66). Berrian's sick mother needed great quantities of it (E66–E67), and Nardif Darksi and the Gloomville leaders used her to smuggle it (E129, E132); Yenneth's crew skimmed texair through an in with the Archivists (E126, E129). Withers dissolved a texair coin, calling it "weak, diluted" (E108). Refining requires presses, heat, water, magical energy, and an unknown arcane requirement (E149). Pushed to full potential it becomes volatile — the bridge to Potentium (E102, E139); a corrupted form was poured into sewers (per glossary).

## Relationships / whereabouts
The party's texair hoard sits in the Portable Hole. Torgoth spent 8 texair powering Control Water (E148); Berrian gave Noctis 8 texair coins (E149).
