# The Sundering (Book)

An old coded book — roughly 500 years old, in an unknown language, not trapped and not magical — grabbed by Evac in Pierre Beaubois's final room, its code resembling the letters from Pierre's workshop (E19). Evac determined the script was Drow, the same hand as the party's relic inscriptions (Zarryne, Izzdar, Felynriel, Ninath), and estimated four days to a week to work a cipher (E25). He was three of four hours deep into decoding when the white stag hunt interrupted (E30). The code was cracked aboard the Tides Breath: 33 pages, each taking about an hour to read (E34). The party then began a tradition of reading the Sundering book at the start of each episode, opening with the first page, "The Spark," which spoke of the Dawn Gods — Good, Evil, Law, Chaos, Light, and Dark (E55). The book is the party's primary primary-source text on the Sundering, the event 499 years before the campaign when the Zeeven Dynasty defied the gods and the world was destroyed and reshaped.
