# Luzonovia

Inward-looking, closed-off Germanic nationalist empire — formally the **Luzonovian Sovereignty** ("Luzonovia") — with vassals Treaborg and the Vithian Alliance; capital Rheinsteig (played as "Rynestag"), set atop bluffs amid crow-darkened woodlands. Its people are tall, broad, thick-boned, bearded with short-cropped hair; its rangers and spies show great arcane power, fighting skill, and organization. Luzonovia is resourceful and always shows up for texair distributions. It was behind the plot to assassinate Princess Anabel (with the Beaubois family in league), offered the party 20,000 platinum to stop the wedding without deaths, massed troops at Feldbrook, violated a 200-year treaty at the Straits of Rhys, clashed with the Widowmakers in Jazacha, and raced Gidia for the alien temple find there. Its agents built a "rifle" in a huge underground bunker and pursued the "Titan Killer"; the Nightmare King (Nardif Darksi) was a Luzonovian spy scientist working with the Archivists. After the prince's murder, Queen Beatrice declared war and the Carasian–Luzonovian war culminated in the carnage of Bootenhill and the Airhark Lake trap. The party later exposed Gidia's manipulation: Luzonovia and Carasia made peace and now look to fight the Gidians together, with the King of Luzonovia and Queen Beatrice jointly tasking the party to gather background on Adune.

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Capital Rheinsteig (played as "Rynestag"), 50 miles from the Bootenhill battlefield; Reinhard Heimlick is its Lozernovian advisor to the Archivists.
- Formerly the party's chief antagonist nation; now effectively a co-belligerent against Adune's Gidia.
- The party posed as "Luzonovian insurgents" as cover in Mandalonia (E145–E146).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Founding & character.** The Luzonovian Sovereignty was founded in 17 P.T. at the Council of Reckoning, after Alon the Wise — the great mage and first Archivist — brought the Trine together into their tenuous alliance (which has lasted 482 annum as of the doc's writing). Previously a people known as **The Luxen**, they are calculated and fastidious by nature, guarding themselves and their ways from outsiders. Their identity is driven by strong nationalism and a desire to discover and create things of utility, value, and power. Somewhat removed from Carasa and Gidia, they tend to be inward-looking and cut off from the rest of the Trine, though they are good businessmen and trade often. At the first Council of Reckoning, **Lazlo the Tactician** (first monarch) represented the Luxen and negotiated their eastern territories; though that land was not as immediately valuable as some, its strategic positioning (few enemy borders, natural fortifications, and neighbors that later became vassal states) added to the Sovereignty's great wealth, earning him his title.

**People & culture.** Almost exclusively human (other races are very rarely seen). Noble and commoner alike carry themselves well, calculated and fastidious bordering on cold, but fiercely nationalistic and generous to fellow Luzonovians. German-flavored accents. They speak **Zemnian**, the ancient tongue of the Luxen, throughout the Sovereignty, though their heavy study means common and many other languages are known and used when necessary (they prefer Zemnian). Study and learning are second only to productivity; the population is well versed in the arcane arts (for those apt to it) and in scientific and historical study, taught through both the religious institution of Erathis and the scriptoriums. Cuisine and drink are hearty and German-flavored: beer, wheat, cheese, pork.

**Religion.** **Erathis** is the only god allowed temples or visible practice in the Sovereignty; her temples are many and beautifully built. Almost as prominent are the great **scriptoriums** — state-funded temples of knowledge filled with books, scrolls, and the study of the arcane — found in every city, town, and village and funded directly by the crown as essential to furthering Luzonovian society (some claim the scriptoriums are the true object of Luzonovian worship). Festivals: **Midsommer** (first day of the summer sextet, longest days, recast as a celebration of Erathis through communal creation/construction and the reading of Erathis's lore); **Feast of Vita Messis** (varies yearly; the fall of the Texerine, signaled by Archavisian beacons, marking the coming winter — a time of reflection, meager food, prayer, and the mage-blessing of fields). **Midwinter is outlawed in Luzonovia under pain of imprisonment**, as it honors the Raven Queen and the gods that passed in the Sundering.

**Government.** A monarchy with the ranks: King/Queen (leader of the Luzonovians) > Duke/Duchess (the leader's sons/daughters) > Grand Master (provincial leader, residing in the provincial capital) > Burgomaster (village leader) > Lord (vassal-state representative). Currency is standard coin but stamped with the Luzonovian crest (each faction stamps its own coin), which can make it hard to trade or barter in other lands and can draw attention to a foreign-stamped purse.

**Military.** Most likely the most militarily powerful member of the Trine. A structured, disciplined force balancing melee (knights) and arcane use, distinguished by leaning on its allies — goliaths and Aasimar of the Vithian Alliance to the south, and goliaths and half-orcs of Treaborg to the north — and its assassins. Cold and calculated, they take no unnecessary casualties and prefer to prevent fighting by any other means available. All cities and towns are fortified, and they rely on military installations and the natural landscape for further defense. They also maintain the **Volstrucker** ("Scourgers"), an elite arcane assassin/enforcer guild answering through their own Grand Master to the King; their motto is "Kill one, Save thousands," and to most citizens they are little more than a bogeyman / ghost story. *(Players have plausibly encountered Luzonovian spies and assassins by current play — e.g. the Nightmare King, Nardif Darksi, was a Luzonovian spy scientist. The deeper operational secrets of the Volstrucker are deliberately omitted here.)*

**Geography & demographics.** Total population ~35,000; the capital holds ~20,000. Three provinces:
- **Freienstein** — the capital and largest province, in the center of the Sovereignty, with mountains to the west, rolling hills, fields, and industry to the north, and forest and mountains to the south. Its capital city is **Rheinsteig** ("Jewel on the Hill"), built on a large lake at the base of the mining hills. Mining is the chief industry — precious gems and stones from the eastern mountains and rich mineral/iron-ore deposits from the northern hills — and the northern ironworks and forges are legendary. The eastern mountain fortress and passes are a feat of Trine engineering and seemingly impenetrable; forts and outposts ring the Kingdom of Treaborg to the north, and the southern mountains form the natural barrier with the Vithian Alliance. Exports: mining, farming, the arcane arts, craftsmanship, and the sciences.
- **Waldemar** — borders the Archavisian Order, all moors and swamps in the valley between the Archavisian Mountains and the capital's mountain forts, plus the mountain fortresses. Its hardy people build villages on top of the moors with floating gardens and fishing, and elaborate roadways and **floating villages that can be quickly reconfigured for defense**. Their primary purpose is defending the eastern border and the safe passage of texere into the kingdom; swamp monsters still dwell in the uninhabitable stretches. Major town **Feldbruck** (~3,000). Their notable export is **refined peat turned into a coal-like substance** — a compact, long-burning fuel distributed throughout the nation and the Trine.
- **Vittensten** — the most western province, heavily forested with rolling hills; plains turn to rolling hills with lakes and trees and then dense forest. The forest city **Lieszell** (~5,000) is a wonder of Trine engineering, sited on an inland lake and **built into the massive trees, carved out of their branches, limbs, and trunks**, positioned to protect the capital from the monsters of the harsher southern forests. Exports: pelts, craftsmanship, woodworking, and — most valuably — rare herbs, potions, and natural magical artifacts gleaned from the wild forest.

**Vassal states.** The **Kingdom of Treaborg** (Russian-flavored half-orcs; occupied by Luzonovia in 127 P.T. when the Sovereignty needed resources and Treaborg would not yield, now ruled by a puppet "Alliance of Three" oligarchs over an exploited serf population; exports lumber, wheat, cotton, and farmland) and the **Vithian Alliance** (Viking-flavored seafaring raiders; became a vassal in 268 P.T. when the Tempast Court was told by Kord directly to submit, after a devastating raid on the Carasian capital brought the whole Carasian Kingdom to their door; they must trade first with Luzonovia and field ships and warriors year-round).

**Lineage (selected).** Jon's house tree runs from Lazlo down to the present, including: **Lazlo the Tactician** (founding king, already old at founding); **Detert the Warrior** (constant war with neighbors and monsters, nearly destroyed but founded the capital city); **Friedrich the Merciless** (took revenge on and pacified the people of Treaborg, ruled with an iron fist); **Christoffer the Diplomat** (smoothed over the Vithian raids and folded the Vithians into the Sovereignty peacefully); **Gottfried the Tactless** (arrogant, pressed Luzonovian dominance over the other Trine and founded a secret police); and current **King Ruprecht the Young**, who took the throne at 14 and currently defers heavily to advisors (some worry they control him). Ruprecht's father, **Otten the Charismatic**, was working to reestablish Trine relationships when he was killed in an accidental riding accident; as a result Ruprecht refuses to ride or go near horses, traveling only by teleportation circle or gliding.
