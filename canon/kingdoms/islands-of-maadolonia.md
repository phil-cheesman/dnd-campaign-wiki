# Islands of Maadolonia

Vassal island state of the Gidian Empire where the campaign began — all the original PCs arrived at the isles by trade, slave, or passenger boat. A hardy, Irish-accented, Kord-worshipping seafaring people, the Maadolonians supply manual labor and, above all, sailors and ships for the empire (slavery is outlawed in Gidia proper, but an under-the-books slave trade runs through Maadolonia because the Gidian port garrisons turn a blind eye for the right compensation), and the island capital ports the Gidian Navy. The Harper Kieran's group was sent here to investigate the slave trade, vanished, and left the name McKay as a lead; Noctis later spied here on Rasheed Soltar and the Gidian Navy as a member of the Last Shadow (E53). During the wedding crisis the supreme commander took the navy to the isles to head off full-scale war, and the Vithians later assaulted areas of Maadolonia. Aum Shai relocated the slave master Rasheed here after Tarlif; Trent Hightower, Zax, and the Marquee were due to meet him at the docks. The party struck first — posing as Gidian Navy sent by Captain McKay, killing Rasheed, rescuing the tortured Kieran, escaping through the storm drains with Mr. Whiskers the rat as guide, then storming the harbor to seize a ship and sail out with thirty freed slaves (E144–E146 arc, with the capital revisited in E137). The Battle of Dorcha Cliffs — where Leon burned Mandalonian ships and earned a medal — is named after one of the six islands (E132).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Overview.** The Islands of Maadolonia are a group of six island nation-states currently under the sovereignty of the Gidian Empire, **taken by force in 327 P.T.** They are a hardy seafaring people, still uneasy about Gidian rule though not in open rebellion — many meetings and street/town-hall speeches mourn their lost freedom. The first dwellers were humans, but trade has made it a very diverse and outsider-welcoming place, albeit one with fierce patriotism (locals are "rough around the edges" and expect you to prove yourself). Little magic, study, or lore happens here — the people do more manual labor — but there are **no better sailors in the world**, and the Gidian Empire relies on them heavily for men and ships in its navy. **Kord** is the primary god, though all are welcomed except the Betrayer Gods. Major exports are trade, fishing, and shipbuilding. Approximate population ~6,000 (a town of ~3,000 plus many small coastal villages).

**Government.** A bureaucratic government of six tribes, one from each island, led by **Prime Tara O'Mannin**, with secondary leaders **Lyla O'Kane**, **Aiden Donlon**, **Cian Lane**, **Eoin Henly**, and **Theo Collins**.

**The freedom rebellion.** About 22 years ago (477 P.G.) a major uprising led by the local hero and freedom fighter **Tiarnan the Bold** made a valiant effort to free the isles but failed; he now sits in a Gidian prison.

**Major settlement.** Jon's two docs disagree (logged as a conflict): the kingdom-detail doc names the major settlement **Mag Stoirmeach ("The Stormy Cliff")**, while the player-info doc names **"Kierkewall Fortress — The Jewell on the Rocks."** This page uses Mag Stoirmeach, consistent with the per-island breakdown below.

### The six islands

- **Tír Ársa ("The Ancient Land")** — capital city **Mag Stoirmeach ("The Stormy Cliff")**; leader Prime Tara O'Mannin (a hardy "man's world" woman with the sea in her bones). Feature: the **Ceòthach Mountains**, snow-tipped peaks that split the stormy cliff down the center.
- **Tír an Ghealach ("Land of the Moon")** — town **Duncaster**; leader Lyla O'Kane (kindhearted but fiercely protective of her people and land). Feature: the **Gàirelands ("smiling lands")**, mostly untouched country, beautiful, with mountains on either side and woodlands in the middle, tall green hills springing up with livestock grazing.
- **Réimse Ceilte ("The Hidden Realm")** — town **Aisteach ("The Bizarre Land")**; leader Aiden Donlon (a young man, "one of the touched," reverent toward the land and woods). Feature: the **Feidh Woods**, another way into the **Feywild** — a mystical forest of druid magic and strange wonderful creatures; people who go there come back changed but don't know why. (Likely the "Airsteach / Aisteach" the party visited in E148–E150, where leader "Lila O'cane" hates the empire and Mano represents a giant-and-people union — appears to map to this island (?).)
- **Tír Fuar ("The Cold Land")** — town **Neo-Thorrach ("Barren Place")**; leader Cian Lane (older, very hardy, short-tempered). Feature: the **Dorcha Cliffs**, barren cliffs swept by gale-like winds that protect the inner islands from the wind's blast; land and people alike are hardy and barren. (Namesake of the **Battle of Dorcha Cliffs**, E132.)
- **Mag Mhín ("The Gentle Plain")** — town **Cnoc Oir ("Golden Hill")**; leader Eoin Henly (a ruddy, heavyset man with a warm hearth and heart). Quaint family farms cover the whole island, producing more food than needed to serve the collective of islands. Feature: the **Torrach**.
- **Domhan Gruama ("The Grim World")** — town **Caer Yaris**; leader Theo Collins (battle-hardened, strong-jawed, quick to laugh, equally at home on a boat, in a keep, or on a horse). Feature: the **Gaoth Hills**, rich with minerals they mine; an outpost serving as the **first line of defense** for the islands and taking the brunt of the winds.

### The Trine Islands (surrounding waters)

- **Scargate Atoll** — larger islands of varied tropical jungle-mountain topography; ancient history, with the demigod **Ukatoah** having a major presence. Small settlements of pioneers live here at peace with — even intermarried into — the local pirates. Pirate presence: **Fortune's Favor** berths from **Seabreeze Harbor** and a cluster of homes.
- **Kraken Reef** — no permanent settlement; a shallow reef that can barely hold a dozen ships. Site of the annual **pirate council** between the Trinity — a safe space where pirate-vs-pirate killing and any bloodshed is forbidden (offenders are "fed to the depths"). Mostly rogue pirate waters.
- **Anchors Rest** — tiny tropical islands full of strange wonderful creatures; the famed treasure of the **Grim** is split up and hidden here, all of it heavily trapped and hexed. Pirate presence: the **Pirates Grim** berth here to store loot, carouse, and conduct crew initiation and punishment; the climate is mild enough that no permanent settlement is needed.
- **Squall Cliffs** — home base of the **Damned Rose** pirate Trinity; the small town of **Hurricane Wharf** houses the families of the Damned Rose. A hard life under brutal weather, but a beautiful land of mountains rising from the water and cliffs of massive trees over a small island chain.
- **Summershall Cay** — beautiful island homes of the rich and wealthy, brimming with island pleasantries; well guarded by both the Gidian and Carasian navies.
- **Riah Islands** — no major presence except the **Tower of Riah**, a lookout toward the Parathian inlands; all slave trade in and out passes under the strict governance of **the Keeper**, and the tax must be paid.
- **Gilset Key** — named but undescribed in the source.

### Trade routes

Major routes touching or anchored on Maadolonia:
- **Trine Shipping Route** (low danger) — the water highway of the Trine; all major nations trade along it, making Vallon the wealthiest, biggest city in Alambor. Highly trafficked and protected by proximity to the Trine.
- **Iron Route** (high danger) — primarily Gidian vessels, navy-escorted only; crosses pirate waters into somewhat hostile Vithian areas, trading forged weapons to the Vithians and the other Luzonovian vassals for goods in return.
- **Southern Trading Route** (medium danger) — Gidia and Carasia trading with Parathia: spices, silk, magic, horses in exchange for armor, building tools, food, wine. Threatened by rogue pirates, sea creatures, and sudden weather.
- **Nicholas Seaway** (low danger) — the main Gidian trading vein (also used by Carasia), bringing goods to Maadolonia and carrying wool, minerals, and soldiers to and from Gidia.
- **Fordling Passage** (medium danger) — internal to Luzonovia; furs and Vithian warriors plus Luzonovian goods and magic, also a middleman route to Carasia and Gidia; skirts pirate-controlled waters.
- **New World Shipping Route** (very high danger, minor route) — for those who dare brave it into the poorly charted, reef-choked waters of **Jazaaka**; pirate-infested with minimal navy support, but the rewards are adventure, gold, and an unexplored land of hidden wonders.

### The Pirate Trinity

The **Trinity** (a deliberate mockery of the Trine) is the three established pirate lords who set the pirate code and impose a rough "law and order" over pirate-controlled waters, alongside many lesser pirates and privateers:
- **The Damned Rose** — led by Cranston "Rage" Oakley; businesslike privateers-turned-pirate looking to make a living and live somewhat normally in port, but swift and brutal if crossed. Ships/captains: Loyal Plunderer — Harrington "Iron Chest" Merton; Angry Demon — Cranston "Rage" Oakley; Oceans Terror — Preston "The Unseen" Reyson.
- **The Pirates Grim** — led by Oakes "Grim Reaper" Abram; the most bloodthirsty, taking no prisoners. Ships/captains: The Executioner — Oakes "Grim Reaper" Abram; The Night Grail — Wilton "Raider" Charlton; The Bloody Cutlass — Grimsley "Lost Soul" Rave.
- **Fortune's Favor** — led by Mallie "The Sparrow" Gresham (the campaign NPC Mallie Grisham); chiefly after loot, treasure, and adventure, trading a life or a plank-walk for the next bit of loot. Ships/captains: Fortunes Return — Mallie "The Sparrow" Gresham; Jewel of the Seas — Bennie "Golden-Eye" Clark; Neptune's Delivery — Thurston "Intrepid" Keic.
