# Tarlif

City of tall towers in Paratha, reached by the party out of the Great Rift's canyon (E113). A mageocracy of pre-Sundering elves ruled by a panel of six leaders (Vorace, Inobune, Abarian, Uvuor, Amari, Azal); the Sextet runs the Septum. It prizes wisdom and knowledge, practices common corporal punishment, runs an elf-controlled slave trade of tieflings and tabaxi, worships the Demon Queen and Arethus, and exports spices, horses, magic, and education. The Great Rift lies east of it; Moonbright (Berrian's hometown) and the Vermalock lie north. Key sites: the Flying Carpet inn (Siad), the Jade Oricle enhancement shop in the South Sun Ward, the library (Prof Tritus), the Dustfall slums hiding the Harpers' hideout under Shaahim, the Whispering Stacks, and the six-level obsidian Shadowmoon Tower. Tarlif became the Aum Shai arc's stage: dock master Rasheed Soltar and Tinkertop "the Clockmaker" were exposed as Vipers, Nardif Darksi sat the Mageocracy as the Arch Dragon, and Jameti Costco was blackmailed into hunting the artifact. Torgoth joined the crew here (E111 era), and Quinton's band Squinton and the Scorpions played a banger show. The party vowed to return to end Aum Shai and the slave trade; by E137 Tarlif was under threat from the Gidian conquest.

## Relationships / whereabouts

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

In Jon's worldbuilding prose the city is spelled **Tarlif** (also **Tarilif**) and nicknamed **"The Rise and Fall."** It is the capital and only true city of the kingdom of **Parathia**, sitting in the province of **Sarirdir**, and is the only major trading port in an otherwise-landlocked Parathia (the rest of Parathia's settlements have no sea or river access). Population ~8,000 (with Parathia as a whole ~12,000 counting roving caravan bands and scattered desert villages). It sits on the great river **Merithion**.

**Economy / exports.** Slaves are the primary export of Tarlif, alongside spices, magic artifacts, and silk. (The kingdom-level Parathia overview lists the realm's broader exports as spices, horses, magic, and knowledge.) Tieflings are held as indentured servants/slaves; elves are the primary race, with the city built around the elven mageocracy.

**Districts and sites.** Notable areas of the city include: the **Sun Palace** (grand palace where all important functions are held); the **Chaos Well**, a religious monument to Erathis (Erathas) built as a multi-tiered bath house, well, and garden "like a kaleidoscope"; the **Palace Living Quarters** (where Voras lives), the **Palace Reservoir**, and the **Endless Waters** (a spring-fed cascading fountain); the elite **South Sun Ward** and **North Sun Ward**; the **Grand Bazar**; **Hesh Market** (middle-class food market); **Legion Post Lowtown**; **Traders Run** (a shop district, home of the clockmaker); the **House of Commerce** (run by the dockmaster — "a den of thieves and cutthroats"); **Harbor Holdings**, the docks where slaves are ferried; **Nefet Port**; the **Drench** and **Dustfall** slums; the **South Breach** and **North Breach**; **Ankot Market** (middle-class goods market); and **the Dead Sheneth**, a dried-up river bed.

**Establishments.** The **Flying Carpet** tavern, run by Saiyid (canon "Siad"); the **Arabian Nights** brothel, run by Madame Fat'hiyaa el-Saleh; the **Rising Steel** weaponsmith (Amar); **Sachem's Store** general store (Sachem); **Uzazel's Emporium** magic shop (Uzazel); the **Library of Tarlif** (Hakeem); the **Eastern Academia** university (Rahman); the **Raven Queen's Temple**; the **Sun Guard** royal barracks (Bakaar); and the **Moon Guard** city barracks (Jawaad).

**Leadership — the Sestet (mageocracy).** Six mage-leaders of equal authority, each holding a portfolio: **Voras** (M, Law — de-facto leader, "the Voice of Reason"; canon "Vorace"); **Innubine** (M, History/Language — "the Thinker"; canon "Inobune"); **Ibarin** (M, the Planes — "the rebel"; canon "Abarian," and the same figure recorded in play as Nardif Darksi/Nardif Darksky); **Uvior** (F, War — "the Terse"; canon "Uvuor"); **Amari** (F, Nature/crops — "the Concerned"); and **Azahl** (F, the People — "the Passionate"; canon "Azal").

**The Harpers — the "Hot Stone" cell.** Tarlif hosts a Harper cell codenamed **Hot Stone**, tasked with freeing the tieflings enslaved by the Parathians, gathering information on the ruling mages, and investigating the growing occurrence of creatures emerging from the Rift. Its leader (Wise Owl) is the shifter **Sahim** (canon "Shaahim"), the area leader the party dealt with in the **Dustfall** slums. *(The cell's named associate roster and its specific base layout are unrevealed DM prep — kept in quarantine, not surfaced here.)* Harpers are taught thieves' cant, often bear thin rings branded or tattooed on ankles/wrists to signify (former) bondage, are masters of espionage and covert communication via dropped messages, signs and markers, and rely on a network of beggars and the poor kept loyal through shared goals and payment.

**Regional context.** Parathia is a vassal state of the Carasian Kingdom (since 321 P.T., after Carasian knights aided Parathia in the War with Gusaash). Its elves were once followers of Corellon in the eledine forests; after Corellon was killed in the war of the betrayer gods and that land destroyed, they became nomads who remember the past and now worship death (the Raven Queen). Sarirdir province also contains the Garden of Kathir (a fertile-crescent paradise of wealthy plantations) and an entrance to the Rift; the slot canyons behind Tarlif run back into that Rift.
