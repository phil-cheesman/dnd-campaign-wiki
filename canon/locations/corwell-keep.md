# Corwell Keep

Hilltop keep of Baron Alexander Janis in Evershire, near Banderhop (the Bandersnatch Mountains' largest peak); travelers bound for Airdale must pass it. The party was introduced at court by Ford, finding the sickly Baron dining with his court of eight and his wife Elizabeth — with detection revealing darkness around chief advisor Mallory, book keeper Leofrene, and coin keeper Atwell. Local history from barkeep George: the Baron once loved a woman driven out to the swamp (the Mother/Viola), and a curse may lie on the land. The keep was the site of the "brain-block" confrontation: Blackwood and Mallory met the party with a dozen guards, something pressed into their minds like a brick wall, and Noctis, Berrian, and Cruucar — the only three who entered — were later gripped by a deathly cold enchantment worked by the Mother through Mallory. Blackwood's rebellion logs counted 15 of his names posted here. After turning into the dragon Valadon, Blackwood attacked the keep with green luminous flames — "the revenge he started 10 years ago" — leaving it blasted, acid-burned, and boggy. The party found twelve prisoners, Able Foster, and the dying Baron with LJ; the Baron died, leaving LJ (Leon) in control. LJ later held court here with Valadon's head on a wagon.
