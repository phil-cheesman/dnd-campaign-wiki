# Avernus

Hell plane of blood rivers and treeless wastes — only blood-red moss grows on the rocks — ruled by Zariel, Arch Duchess of Avernus, whose chief advisor General Bell (a 35–40-foot, CR 27 pit fiend and former ruler of the plane) plots to overthrow her (other notes say he seeks to overthrow "Cario" as Duke). Adune teleported the party here after the tuning-fork mishap. The Gatekeeper of Avernus imposed the Pact — "a soul for a soul": a toll of one soul plus interest of one soul per person per day, collected by vanquishing natives and learning their true names; Vane, Cruucar, Noctis, and the companion Colossus were in debt. The party witnessed devil-vs-demon battle columns, met Harden's crew from the Swords Coast (who later sold them out to Bell), learned Tiamat dwells on the plane, and crawled up a waste shaft into Bell's Forge — the Temple of Blood Sacrifice atop a 400–500-foot spiked red stone tower in the clouds — where Bell beheaded Colossus with his flaming sword. Cruucar paid all four souls back to the Gatekeeper. Known exits: Fort Knucklebow, the Bronze Citadel's teleportation circle, and the Pillar of Skulls. Max's team had earlier retrieved the sentient crystalline power source from Avernus — the "weapon of mass destruction" the Gauntlet sought, later guarded by Trent Hightower (E132 recap).
