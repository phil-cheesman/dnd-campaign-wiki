# Evershire

The oldest Gideon settlement and the last bastion Gidia established in Dalacia — a beautiful lake valley on the Gidia/Dalacia border, decimated some time ago by the "Black Dread" (long debated: dragon or plague). The dragon's acid attack killed Baron Alexander Janis's twin sons (Kance and Clarence), orcs and goblins then devastated the land, and Lord Blackwood arrived as Special Regent, "cleaning up" while taxing it under martial law as a slumlord — secretly allied with the very raiders (the Canick Tribe) he claimed to remove, with the witch Viola also in league. The region holds Corwell Keep, the towns Airdale, New Langdale, Tamworth, Carningsby, and Squamish, the Shimmering Grotto mine, the Crag fortress, Blackwood Manor, and the Bandersnatch Mountains. A 50-strong rebellion (Able Foster, Thomas Davis, Betty) harassed Blackwood's supply lines; the party joined it, took the Crag, sabotaged the mine, and finally slew Blackwood — revealed as the black dragon Valadon — earning the titles Saviors of Evershire (locally) and Proctors of Evershire (from King Felix). The party came seeking purple heart wood for Cruucar's glaive and dragon lore. LJ, the Baron's curse-freed son, now rules Evershire, with Able Foster as Chief Guard and Daniel Bradston "Man of the People."
