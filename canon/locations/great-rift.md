# The Great Rift

Vast canyon expanse east of the city of Tarlif, formed when the gods Carinian and Torog died fighting each other in a battle close to the actual Sundering fight to the death; it runs thousands of feet deep, with supernaturally carved spires rising 10,000 feet from its floor. Ella dine trees now grow along it, the way into the Feywild is "mixed" along it, and the monsters of its caves have started to venture out — Paratha's capital, sited down from the Rift, suffers strange incursions and supernatural beasts, with hushed talk of an "Alliance of the 5 Armies" (E10). Below the Rift lies "the Deep" — darkness and terrors linked to the Shadowfell — and the Vault of Izdar, the evil temple complex of the Hive. Guided by Wahle, the party descended into the Rift (E104): two uneventful days, the canyon floor reached on day three, a giant intelligent snake fight at a ruined ancient temple, and Wahle's departure with a hand-drawn return map. Jameti Costco's search maps for the Vault left the area north of the Vermalock and the Rift uncrossed (E115), and Prof Tritus's library lore (E114) tied the Rift, the party's artifacts, and the Vault of Izdar together, noting planar movement happens via the Astral Sea.
