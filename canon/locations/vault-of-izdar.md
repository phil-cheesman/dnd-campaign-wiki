# Vault of Izdar

Evil underground temple complex beneath the Great Rift, dwelling place of Izzdar the Undying ("the Lith"). Its obelisk reads: "I Izzdar the undying, member of the hive, dwell below... Time shall come when the Hive shall rule over all, restoring power and glory to the ancient ones until we walk together unshackled." A chained book lists nine bad gods, and the Vault's rooms carry their logos: Zehir (snake), Lolth (compass — she dwells in Inverness), Torog (shackles), Asmodeus (energy sword), Gruumsh (tri-cross bones), Tharizdun (spiral), Bane (talons), Vecna (skull), Tiamat (pinwheel). The guard Withers (Gourah), of the Order of Torag from before "the time of corruption," welcomed then attacked the party and turned to dust; his moldy journal describes Mana, the Dynasty, the Vault Elders, the Hive, and a "Large Servant," and notes three other such locations may exist. A water room held six paintings of the Hive archmages — Ninath, Faldryal, Zarryien, Ilharius, Velonim, and a tall bald drow — paired with six weapons, and the beholder Beltraz the Unseen laired in the eye-mirror chamber. The party looted heavily (Rod of Attenuation, Nine Lives Stealer, the amethyst orb, an adamantium scepter) before exiting to Tarlif. The Vault has a connection to the Zeevens, and Omago — chief architect of the Hive, one of nine drow leaders from the Vault — is the sentience inside the Potentium power source now empowering Adune.
