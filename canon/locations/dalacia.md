# Dalacia (Free States of Dalacia)

A free-state confederacy of self-governing towns — farmers, primarily half-elves (Jon's worldbuilding doc lists the population as primarily half-elves, then humans, halflings and travelers; the earlier play-notes mention of tieflings is not corroborated there). Per Jon's authoritative lore Dalacia is a vassal state of the **Gidian Empire** rather than an independent world power — it accepted Gidian rule (see below); the "world power established at the Sundering settlement" framing reflects the party's earlier in-play impression. Its capital is Aberdeen, hometown of Ford the gunslinger. Evershire, the oldest Gideon settlement, was the last bastion Gidia established in Dalacia, sitting on the Gidia/Dalacia border; the purple heart wood for Cruucar's glaive grows there near Corwell Keep. Dalacia is troubled land: a dragon (the Black Dread, later revealed as Valadon) attacked roughly seven years before the party's arrival, killed the lord's sons, and a plague followed. Zanim dreamed of hulking creatures twice the height of men fighting Aberdeen's defenders while Aberdeen burned — and the dream proved prophetic. Ford's account (E132): he fought in Dalacia and Evershire repelling orc invasions after the party slew Valadon, and at the Battle of Aberdeen the defenders repelled the Luzonovians and hailed a new leader — "Zanim the Bold," the party's fallen druid. Adune later reported Aberdeen won, safe, and recovering (E91). By the grim war status of E137, however, Dalacia was occupied by the conquering Gidians.

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Status & governance.** The Free States of Dalacia are a vassal state of the Gidian Empire, governed as a confederacy: each individual city or town governs itself, but all contribute to a league/federation that promotes (at least in theory) the common good of all member states. Each town has an appointed **Marshal** to run it; the **Chief Marshal** over the capital is **Walter "Truth" Fulton**. The Gidian Empire generally stays out of Dalacian affairs, sending emissaries only when absolutely necessary or when called upon by the Marshals. Approximate population ~8,000 — the capital town of ~2,000 plus many villages and settlements throughout. The accent/flavor is American Southwest.

**People & faith.** Primarily half-elves, then humans, halflings and travelers. Their primary god is **Pelor** (canonically logged in this wiki as "Paylor"); all other gods except the betrayer gods are accepted. They are described as hardworking, welcoming, and entrepreneurial — a people "looking to live out the 'American Dream.'" Generally peaceful so long as their freedoms aren't interfered with.

**Economy.** Major exports are farming and craftsmanship, with **wheat and cotton** the primary goods, alongside beautifully crafted furniture. Wagon trains constantly flow back and forth between the prairie settlements and farms, carrying goods out; flowing in are guarded wagonloads of **texere**, doled out to the farmers in huge markets located in local towns and villages. This peaceful trade has gone on for hundreds of years.

**Geography.** The land is very much the American Southwest: flanked by mountains that house roving bands of goblins and kobolds, and hills/plateaus that hold all manner of dangerous beasts and gnolls. Within the fork of these dangers lies a large prairie of rolling hills and small rivers.

**History.** The prairielands were settled around **100 A.S.** by **Elijah the Tall**, who ventured into the vast prairie to battle off the roving bands of gnolls, beasts and goblins and begin settling the land. Dalacia became a vassal state of the Gidian Empire in **102 P.T.**, when — on the brink of starvation due to the loss of transmutation magic and the new course the weather was taking — its people gladly became Gidian subjects to preserve their way of life. (The capital is **Aberdeen**, styled "The Prosperous.")
