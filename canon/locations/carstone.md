# Carstone

Carstone is a small, quaint farming village in the southern Carasian Kingdom, set on a slow-flowing river roughly 60 miles inland from the shore where the party shipwrecked, and about 80–90 miles from the port town of Surtree (E02, E03). On their act-one overland journey the party passed through Carstone (E03, E04): the road runs north and east from the wreck, reaching a fork after about 40 miles, from which a road heads roughly 20 miles north to the village (E03). The approach drops from dusty high grasses and scattered trees down to the river, then follows it past poplars and great oaks until the river widens and reveals fields of crops and the modest, well-kept village beyond (E03). The viscount of the town is the half-elf Jean-Pierre Bassoet, who owns the local vineyard and hired the party over a wine-delivery problem (E03, E04). The party bought rations at the village general store (E04), and the merchant Jon — a wine merchant whose delivery had been delayed — was rescued from gnolls on the north road during this leg (E04).

Carstone is a small, quaint, quiet farming village on a slow river in the southern Carasian Kingdom. Its economy centers on fields of crops and a vineyard owned by the viscount, with produce and wine shipped by barge along the river. The wharf moves goods up and down the river between Surtree and the "Parthians" downstream, making the village a modest river-trade waypoint.
