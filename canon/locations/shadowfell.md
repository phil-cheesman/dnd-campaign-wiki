# Shadowfell

Dark, evil mirror plane of the Material Plane (its counterpart is the hyper-emotional Shea; its opposite is the Feywild). Foul magic and diseases pervade it — the longer one stays, the more the mind is lost — and shadow dragons dwell there. The landscape the party entered held no trees, only burned stumps to the horizon, with no sun and no moon. The Nine — the Knights of Theydune, pre-Sundering dark paladins (Shadow Captain, Chief Keeper, Betrayer, Dwemer Lake, Tatanin, Knight of Umber, Dark Marshal, Undying, one unnamed) — were sent here as punishment; Jameti Costco found them and led both the Arch Dragon and Berrian's father Bo to them. The wedding assassins' portal may have led here (E51). The party entered by swimming through Vane's scepter pool at Moonbright's waterfall (the Pool of Death, where the planes are thinnest), and the whole Gloomville arc — Grimsburg, Gloomville, the Tower of Illmore, Sorrowshade, Withervale — played out on this plane, where Yenneth had been exiled by Nardif Darksi after murdering a family in Tarlif. Berrian's mother, "the bridge" between the Knights and Aum Shai, was found here, and his parents were saved from the Shadowfell and are still recovering. A shimmering wall splits a river between Shadowfell and Material Plane; the Rod of Attenuation pulses purple/green between the two.
