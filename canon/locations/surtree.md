# Surtree

Port city and the party's first home base, led by the shrewd high elf Viscountess Avesia ("do not lie to her") with High Recter Ventius and Captain Mckinnon. The dying Weatherface tasked the shipwrecked party with delivering a letter to the Last Drop inn here, which led them to Maximilian Taldross and the Knights of Gidia. Surtree was home of the once-wealthy Beaubois family, who fell from grace around 480 annum, turned to opiates, black magic, and family sacrifice, and were driven to a chalet on the bluffs 15 miles N-NE. Pierre Beaubois's revenge played out across the city: his guard assaulted it, Spawn of Kyuss (worm-plague undead) attacked the dignitaries' play with a disembodied voice threatening Avesia, and corrupted texair flowed in the sewers via refinery piping from Blackfinger Tower toward the fields north of town. Local fixtures: the Last Drop, Leon's Iron Chisel (built Zanim's prosthetic leg), Iron Works with the dragonborn armorer Broomhida, the university (Professors Williams and Willis), and the Embassy guest quarters. After defeating the Beaubois plot the party was honored as Defenders of Sutray and granted the 170-acre Beaubois Chalet. King Felix later styled them "Defenders of Sutrey" among their formal honors (E100).

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Ruled by Viscountess Avesia (family of the Queen of Carasia), so the city is Carasian-aligned.
- The party's chalet lands outside town were later seized under their Carasian banishment.
- The Spawn of Kyuss seen here reappeared in the Shadowfell at Withervale Estate (E127).

**Geography & approach.** Surtree is a large walled Carasian river city on the river Charmé. The waterway widens and slows on the approach, lined with small farms; barges and boats crowd the channel and barge operators call to one another in nautical terms. The city spans both banks. The smaller **right bank** sits *outside* the main wall: a single gate into a small walled area with barge docks, surrounded by modest buildings scattered amongst trees and fields, with wagons and people moving along the lanes. The **left bank** holds the main town, at least ten times the size of the right side, enclosed by a wall with three large gates. Inside, a cobblestone riverbank runs with walkways and greenways where well-dressed townsfolk stroll. Midway into the town a **forked canal** holds small gondola-type pleasure boats (water ferries / pleasure craft). Several grand buildings rise above the rest with ornate structures and roofs — some religious, others of learning and government. At the back middle of the town a **second tier** stands behind an inner wall, raised above the rest on a hill. A well-made **bridge** spans the river, tall enough for vessels to pass beneath. On high, tree-lined banks near the bridge stands a once-grand estate now crumbling and ruined — it was burned down.

**The Old Beaubois Family Ruin.** The burned-out estate on the high banks is the Old Beaubois Family Ruin. No one lives there now; the old mansion, a church, and the crypts are all that remain, mostly ruined.
