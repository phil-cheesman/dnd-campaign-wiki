# Feldbruk

Feldbruk is a marsh city of roughly 3,000 people in Waldemar province, on the eastern frontier of the Luzonovian Sovereignty, set among the moors and swamps of the valley between the Archavisian Mountains and the capital province's mountain forts. Built across waterlogged terrain, the city sprawls over stilted timber houses, floating platforms, and a maze of winding reed-lined waterways; wooden causeways and raised pathways link its districts, and boats — rowboats, dugout canoes, and gondolas — replace horses and wagons for transport. Like all Luzonovians, its people have turned a difficult land to advantage, building floating gardens and fisheries to sustain themselves and elaborate, reconfigurable roadways and floating villages that can be rearranged quickly as a defensive measure. The province exists chiefly to defend the Sovereignty's eastern border and to guard the safe passage of texere into the main parts of the kingdom; the surrounding swamps remain dangerous, holding their share of swamp monsters in the stretches too difficult even for the Luzonovians to inhabit. In play, the party knew the place as "Feldbrook": Luzonovian troops massed there over several months during the run-up to war (E33), Max was assigned to fight near it (E52) before being recalled to Kingswatch (E67), and the swamps were described as horrible and held back by dykes and walls (E67). En route to the Archivists, the party was flown there in Cruucar's airship and dropped off with the Archivist advisor Reinhard Heimlick (E137).
