# The Trene

The "realm of realms" — the collective order of nations spanning Alambor, whose peace the Archivists keep. Teleportation between Trenes runs only through guarded citadels: Salem, high priest of Paylor, led the party to the teleportation citadel in Grellier — effectively an embassy and "a magic death box in a sense," the only teleportation from one Trene to another (E26). High Archivist Zinoff the Righteous convened a summit of the Trene's leaders in Vallon ahead of the royal wedding (E33). The Vontrice family is described as the wealthiest, most connected family in the Trene (E40). At the king's war council the party infiltrated under Waldo Taldross's identity intending to expose Sir Trent as a betrayer of the Trine — and one council note claims it was exposed that the Carasians were "the rightful Trine overlords" (E135–E136). Zildran's paladin warriors are now employed by the Trine, and attacked the party on the aerials during the ship action (E151). The Pirate Trinity — a coalition of three pirate leaders with three ships each — is an explicit mockery of the Trine (E147).

## Relationships / whereabouts
