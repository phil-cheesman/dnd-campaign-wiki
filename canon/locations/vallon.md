# Vallon

Capital of the Carasian Kingdom and venue of the royal wedding of Princess Anabel and Prince Thomas, held at the Halls of Wonder — the palace whose blueprints and guard lists the conspirators sought to trade to the Arch Dragon. Early notes name the wedding capital "Balom," later consistently Vallon/Vilan. The party sailed here with Adune for High Archivist Zinoff's summit of the Trene's leaders, were secretly hired by Queen Beatrice as Prince Thomas's protectors, and ran the wedding-arc investigation from the Slayer's Take inn in a rough part of town: the Temple of Erathis hilltop church (blue-robed monks; Milo Zulezi, Wise Owl of the Harpers in Vallon), the Falcon Citadel dungeons holding the doppelganger Ors, the Moon Hand Bastille prison (the party broke out), the Vital Market, the Revana District with its Raven Queen temple, and the House of Midnight brothel. The wedding day featured joint Gidian–Carasian guard command with Jon Louie, a planned four-hour carriage parade on a secret route, and a magic-canceling cone over the venue — but Justin stabbed the prince at the ceremony. The party's ship Wrecked at the Reef was papered as built in Vallon in 478. By E137, Vallon and Grellier were the only Carasian cities left in the war.

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

Vallon is the capital city of the Carasian Kingdom (population ~25,000) and the seat of Champlet, the capital province — a northwestern province on the coast of the Bay of Resplendence and the Trine Waters. Its nickname is "saphir sur la mer" ("sapphire on the sea"). The Carasians boast it is the greatest city in Alambor and one of the wonders of the Trine: situated where the Bay of Resplendence meets the Trine Waters, it is to many the center of the world for trade, variety, and finding anything that exists. All trade through Gidia flows through this space and up the canal, and Vallon is the primary way into the heart of the Trine (at least for Gidia and Carasa). Champlet province around it is an idyllic landscape of hills, fields, and forests holding some of the largest vineyards — producing what is held to be the best wine in all the Trine — with the coasts lined by fishing towns. Major exports/commerce: wine, food, cotton, knowledge, and "any artifact or craft that can be dreamt up," found in Vallon's trading markets.

Geography: the city sprawls across many cliff-tiered islands around one large central island, all set above the surrounding bay, linked by great arched bridges that tower over 100 ft above the water so ships can pass beneath. The only ship entrance is a great defended interior bay — a massive bowing ring, part manmade and part leftover cliff — sealed by two bridges holding wood-and-iron portcullises that can be dropped, with guns and ballista lining and hidden through the cliffs. The inner ring is paved with docks extending far out into the water to receive hundreds of incoming ships. The Carasian Kingdom keeps no large navy; the Grand Canal that feeds the entire inner Trine runs through Carasa and straight through Vallon, and the ballista-lined walls are "a constant reminder who really owns the waterways."

Landmarks named in Jon's notes: the gilded towers of the six deities dominate the skyline, alongside the Colisée de Juli (the fighting arena, also written as building L1), the Halls of Wonder (the High Queen's high court / royal palace, L3), and the Université de sept ("university of seven," I1). Religious buildings include temples to Avandra, Erathis, Kord, Moradin (rendered "Mordin" in the notes), Pelor, and the Raven Queen. Barracks/military: Moonhand Bastille (J1) and the Falcon Citadel (J2). Libraries: the Royal Athenaeum (H1) and the Grand Archive (H2). Named taverns include the Three Crowns ("les trois couronnes"; owners Louys Paquin, a fire genasi, and Everad, a female minotaur), the Slayer's Take, and the Cloven Hoof (a centaur). Named brothels include the Crowing Cock, the Queen's Robes, and the House of Midnight ("la maison de minuit"). Other establishments: weaponsmiths Steel Wings and the Eternal Flame; general stores Goody's Convenience, the Vital Mart, and the Little Bird; and magic shops the Dark Flame (Vax'ill Dan, a half-elven man) and Angels and Demons (Azazel, a Deva).

Leadership: Vallon is the seat of Queen Beatris Lionsblood (the eldest heir always succeeds, regardless of gender). Her partner is Volo; her son and heir is Prince Thomas; she also has four half-princess daughters — Ada, Adelina, Annais, and Elizabet. The kingdom's ruling council is the Mantle, which includes Lady Penelope Vontrice. Her archivist advisor is High Rector Lucia. (Jon's prose spells the queen "Beatris Lionsblood" and the prince "Thommas"; the play-notes canonical spellings "Queen Beatrice" / "Prince Thomas" are retained per the glossary, with Jon's spellings as aliases. The notes also list council roles "Chief Advisor" and "Leader of the Queen's Guard" without names.)

The Harpers maintain their main cell in Vallon, called "the Hive" (building L2), led by Wise Owl Milo Zulezi (who in play is met at the Temple of Erathis). Per Jon's Carasian-Kingdom notes, the Harpers are a decentralized, secretive freedom-fighter network ("Down with tyranny. Fairness and equality for all.") with cells and lone operatives across Alambor, focused on rooting out corruption, dismantling the slave trade, and freeing the enslaved; the Vallon cell's task is to run operations and distribute information to other cells and to work with Carasian leadership on what needs the Harpers' attention. Its agents (e.g. Ilario Masino, Adelardo Scaramella, Fazio Pellino, Isotta Gucciardo, Fiorella Pompei, Elvira Mellone) hide "in plain sight," typically in the poorer and run-down areas, behind doors, passages, and traps meant to mislead infiltrators. (Note: this Harper "Hive" is a distinct entity from "the Hive" of Izzdar and Omago — the drow Zeeven group below the Vault — which shares only the name.)

Carasian-Kingdom context: Carasa was founded in 17 P.T. after Alon the Wise, the first Archivist, brought the Trine together in the Council of Reckoning; its first monarch was Anestasia the Bold. Champlet holds the majority of the Carasian coastline and has a more Mediterranean feel approaching Vallon. The capital and its great structures were built up over generations of the royal line — notably Alote the Prosperous (built the first canal allowing ships to pass from the Bay of Resplendence into the Trine Waters) and Julien the Builder, who "built the capital city Vallon into what it is today" and expanded the canal into its current grand stone form. The Carasian culture is French-flavored and life-loving, prizing food, wine, the arts, the arcane, and festivals; the great combat-and-games festival "Festival de Jeux" (25th of Tempastum) culminates in the Chief Games held in the Colisée de Juli, with winners crowned by the High Queen and immortalized on the "wall of stories" in the capital.
