# Blackwood Manor

Lord Blackwood's fortified seat near the moores of Evershire: a walled, many-building hilltop complex beside a large still lake, with a moat, two stories, a large roofed rear building, and a two-story keep with wrap-around ramparts — moderately fortified, less so than the Crag (his logs counted 22 names here). The party came for a dinner "reservation" in a great double-storied hall reminiscent of Edoras, where Blackwood unveiled a vintage wine with Elias's head floating in it (Leofrene's too, per the recap), confirmed Viola's Aum Shai membership, and declared his aim to take over the world using texair and Aum Shai. Battle erupted; Blackwood shrugged off the party with Legendary Resistance, jailed Benjamin, Cruucar, and Noctis, then transformed into the dragon Valadon and flew off to burn Corwell Keep. Exploration via Mallory's magic mirror revealed the study's troop ledgers tied to "the Mother," a flying-dragon-sealed letter matching Pierre Beaubois's dagger, a Bandersnatch Mountains mural concealing a secret room (Rope of Entanglement, the Mode of Elemental Energy diamond), and a wardrobe-mirror portal into the dragon's gold-piled lair cavern where Elizabeth Janis was caged. After Valadon's death the Manor was awarded to the party — and that night Tristan ran through it shouting "Intruder! Intruder! Intruder!" (the intruder: Adune).
