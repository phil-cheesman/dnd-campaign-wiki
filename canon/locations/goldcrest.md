# Goldcrest

Rich Gidian harbor city — "the New York to Washington DC" — built like Venice along the water on three rivers, with cliffs into a harbor. Founded in 168 by Erik the Shipbuilder right after the Carasians completed the famous passage; Rise the Bold used it as a launch point (his statue stands atop Nicholas the Merchant's, the city's merchant king). Sofia Rose Hand made it the capital of Gidia for 30–40 anum after a civil war over her queenship, creating the lasting rivalry with Kierkenwall; after she died the capital went back, but Goldcrest remains the center of commerce and power. Districts: Endless Falls (religious, First), the Garrison (Second), Hightower (Third — nobles, Shawn Williams's home), and Eastown (Fourth — business, the old market, three academies); the Library there is a major Knights of Gidia center. The party delivered Rudar's cargo here, lodged at Le Kraken, and was hired by Shawn Williams to steal Marquise Avesy's ledger from his manor vault for 22,000 gold — then to raid Marc Carivessee's vault, after which Quinton, Zanim, and Cruucar became wanted men with faces posted across the city. Benjamin, the party's Robin Hood cleric, was from Goldcrest, kicked out for trying to steal from Avesy. The Marque later hung Shawn Williams's people here.

Goldcrest is the major port city (population ~8,000) of the Gidian Empire, and is the seat of the empire's coastal **Strathmore province** (which borders Archivisian territory). The surrounding land teems with lakes and rivers and has two long coastlines; the people are so reliant on the water that "you would think it was a chain of islands," boats are more common than horses, and locals say every man, woman and child will swim more miles than they will walk. The city itself is built into the water rather than merely on the coast — Venice-like, across the coast and the river mouths. It boasts the largest port in all of Alambor and is the wealthiest city in Gidia, "much to the consternation of the people of Kierkenwall," and is a favored haunt of high-ranking officials and nobles (who linger there for its entertainments). Locals call the city **"Our Fair Lady"** — after both the goddess Erathis and Queen Sophia Rosehand.

**Founding & growth.** Goldcrest was founded in 168 by King **Eric Shipbuilder**, who — after seeing the Carasians complete their canal — wanted to turn Gidia into a seafaring nation and commissioned hundreds of ships, birthing the Gidians as a sea people. It began as a small shipbuilding town. King **Rhys the Bold** (r. 185–209) took a shine to it as a launch point to explore the rest of Gidia and venture out into the Bay of Resplendence (Alote) and the trine waters; under his reign it grew in wealth and power. His successor **Nicolas the Merchant** (r. 209–241) explored less but grew commerce immensely. The city fell into some disrepair over the following years until it backed **Sophia Rosehand** (r. 269–301) in her contest for the throne; she won and made Goldcrest the new capital during her reign. A wise and strong ruler and a devout follower of **Erathis**, she made Goldcrest not only the merchant capital of Gidia but also its most learned and beautiful city. Moving the seat of power away from Kierkenwall — and the city's devout Erathis worship — set Kierkenwall lastingly at odds with Goldcrest; but Goldcrest had become too wealthy and powerful for Kierkenwall to do anything but wait until the capital eventually moved back. As a result, Goldcresters tend to think themselves superior to the rest of Gidia, especially Kierkenwall.

**Economy.** Trade is Strathmore's primary commerce: ships from all over Alambor arrive to deliver goods, taxes and royalties from the vassal states, and Goldcrest is the dock for the Empire's Navy (so sailors and soldiers are constantly in and out). Inland, fishing and craftsmanship — specifically water-vessel construction — are major exports. The texere (the empire's lifeblood agricultural magic) flows through Goldcrest before being distributed.

**Religion.** Goldcrest's patron god is **Erathis**, goddess of civilization, invention and law. Her white cathedral crowns the cliffs, and her **High Priestess** is the city's chief religious figure — very prosperous (drawing much money from the populace) and putting it toward the poor, the sick, the arts, and religious building.

**Knights of Gidia — "the Library."** Goldcrest hosts the Knights of Gidia cell known as **the Library**, the empire's primary place of learning, study and religion, housing a huge library and extensive grounds and buildings for study. Its commanding **Vindicator is Matthias**, an older paladin (monk-like, age ~79) and one of the oldest Knights of Gidia. The cell's seat is **the Three Academies** in Eastown.

**The four districts:**
- **Endless Falls** (District 1, the religious quarter and part of the older city): dominated by a stone bastion above the cliffs from which the **Endless Falls** stream — an impossible, never-ending cascade from a ground well that bubbles up out of the cliffs. The statue of **Rhys the Bold** stands at the bastion's furthest extent; below sits the temple of "the Lady" (Erathis) with its gardens and waterways, the Archivist Advisor's living space, and the High Priestess of Erathis's manor; steps lead down past the statue of **Nicolas the Merchant** and a Row of Mausoleums to the lower quarters.
- **The Garrison** (District 2, the military quarter): a walled fortress of tiered cliffs and locked, gated waterways leading to the **Chateau**, seat of the military leader; includes the military dockyard and sheltered military docks. Led by **Admiral West**, a former leader of the Navy forced to step down about a decade ago and bored, irritable and quick to act ever since.
- **Hightower** (District 3, the posh district): home to most nobles and dignitaries. Contains **Denth Palace** (the Duke's seat), lycees, libraries, theaters, the Royal Market, Botanical Gardens and the Royal Dragoons.
- **Eastown** (District 4, the commoner quarter): crowded and dense, where the bulk of the city lives and "the real work gets done." The **three arenas** dominate it (the Old Arena, the King's Arena, and the Lady's Arena), along with the Three Academies, the **Salt & Steel Company** (always smoking from its processing), the tavern **Le Kraken** (run by Kayden Clark), workshops, markets, and **Shipyard Isle**.
