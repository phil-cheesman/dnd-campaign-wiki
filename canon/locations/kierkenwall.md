# Kierkenwall

Capital of the Gidia empire and the city where the campaign's opening boat departed. Founding lore: three brothers settled a green-algae estuary and founded Gilda, which became Kierkenwall ("three rivers"); six knights each founded a rival sextet of the city, plus the older Olde Town (High Gilda is the central area). Landmarks include the statue of Gidia the Tall, the Crucible arena, the Kingfisher tavern with legal basement fights, the Nessel Nook Inn, the high-end Lavish Chateu, the magic shop Arcane Infinity, Kierkenwall University (Gregor is lead scientist), and the lighthouse at the bay's entrance holding the great Blue Sapphire. It is a huge, heavily fortified city with a mile-wide river and a massive port; the Knights of Gidia stronghold hosts the only teleportation circle the party first arrived by. The capital moved to Goldcrest under Sofia Rose Hand for 30–40 anum, then returned — the two cities still dislike each other. The party met King Felix's full council here (E27), ran the texair wagon job on the Kings Road, was honored before huge crowds after slaying Valadon (E100), and finally was smuggled in by Shaahim and Aldo Taldross for the war council at which Adune killed King Felix and framed them (E134–E136).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Setting & nickname.** Kierkenwall is the capital city of the Gidian Empire (population ~11,000) and the dominating feature of Langdale, the Empire's capital province. It is nicknamed "Three Rivers" for the point where three rivers — the Gilda, the Lucian and the Declan — meet, flowing from the different corners of Gidia and swelling into the great river that winds its way into the Bay of Resplendence. (The played notes' "huge city with a mile-wide river" reads as the river at the city; Jon's prose fixes the city's own population at ~11,000 — the largest single settlement of an Empire whose total population is given as ~29,000.)

**Geography & fortifications.** The city lands on the banks of the huge Gilda River where it meets the Lucian and the Declan, resting on the bend as the rivers combine and head off to the Bay of Resplendence. The river is so wide here, and whips up into small squalls at a moment's notice, that breakwater sea-walls ("the break") were built on outcropping islands to break the water and protect ships inland while forming a further line of defense. A moat rings the entire perimeter, broken only by the stream that splits the city, flows through it, and exits into the lake. A high grey wall of seemingly very old construction encloses it, studded with square squat towers at regular intervals. Six fortresses form additional bastions around the perimeter and make up the districts — guard liveries and many buildings are colored to match their fortress. There is a well-known rivalry between the districts that plays out during games held in the Arena of Cassius (possibly the same venue play-notes call the Crucible (?)). Several raised areas rise within the city, and the original quarter is called Oldtown (play-notes: "Olde Town").

**Districts.** Jon's notes lay out colored districts tied to the six bastion fortresses: the Purple District (the Gleaming Heights), the inner ring built on a raised rampart of stone and earth, with the gleaming white walls and domed towers of the Ivory Citadel shining over the rest of the city; the Green District (the Greenway, split by the High Street into Upper and Lower) housing the nicer homes of wealthy merchants and businesspeople; the Red District (Merchants Mile), the noisy, less glamorous industrial and trades quarter where the City Market is found; the Blue District (the Talons), the roughest working-class dock area run largely by the wharf masters like territorial gangs; plus an Emerald District and a Copper District (detail not given). (The Ivory Citadel / "Sapphire Court" / "Duke holds court" labels in this district sheet appear to be an earlier draft layer that does not match the played King-Felix court below — noted, not asserted as current (?).)

**Economy.** Because natural waterways span the province, most commerce flows up and down the rivers in huge barge trains: grain, cotton, and all manner of food going out to the capital and distributed throughout the Empire, with goods coming in from the mountains, the spoils of the coast, and wagons from Dalacia — making the city a center for commerce. Surrounding Langdale is the Empire's breadbasket of rolling hills and sprawling fields dotted with prosperous, humble villages and towns, where the trading and distribution of texere is essential and the Texere Markets are a focal point of every town. To the north along the Dalacian border, several fort chains and communication posts help defend the province from raiding bands out of the hills and mountains.

**Royal court.** Kierkenwall is the full seat of the Gidian royal court under King Felix the Cold — a tall, broad-shouldered, grey-streaked man who carries himself as if under a great weight, with cold, discerning grey eyes. His court includes: Chief Steward Percival de Graves, an old family friend the King has known his whole life; the court's Archivist advisor — the chief archivist (play-notes "Hyrector Revaneous"), an albino half-elf recognizable by his telltale purple-stained arms; Foreign and Domestic Affairs minister High Councilor Adun, a brilliant, well-traveled young man (mid-30s) who grew up in the northern mountains outside Ironforge; Peacekeeper Robert Kaveny ("the Watchman"), a thin man who came up through the streets of Kierkenwall and is one of the few commoners in court; Supreme Commander James Royce ("Master of the Deck"), a decorated naval officer in red Gidian uniform; Righteous Hand Ser Trent Hightower of the Knights of Gidia; and Master of Commerce Count Aldo Taldross. (These map to the council the party met in E27, here with Jon's canonical spellings: Adun = "Adune"; Kaveny = "Robert Kavney"; de Graves = "Percival Degraves".)

**The Fist.** Kierkenwall hosts the Knights of Gidia "Fist" cell, tasked with protecting the royal family and the Crown's interests within the city, rooting out troublemakers and dissent, and relaying supplies, armor, arcane knowledge, training and instructions to the rest of the "Fingers." Its Vindicator is Ser Kern. (The party knows the Fist as the unit Max led (E54+); Ser Kern is a Kierkenwall-cell officer not yet encountered in play (?).)
