# Grillers

Grillers is one of the largest cities of the Carasian Kingdom and the provincial capital of its central breadbasket province, a city the party has heard of and skirted since early in the campaign (E02, E14, E15). The Harper Wise Owl Milo Zulezi operated out of "Grier" (E26, E39), and the city was later listed among the Carasian cities still in play (E137). Earlier play-notes reach it under heavily corrupted spellings — Greer/Grier/Gruier — and capture fragments that Jon's worldbuilding docs now confirm and correct: a stickler outsider Duke who is "cousin to the King" (play-notes E25, "Duke Fenenis Qiparious"; Jon: Duke Fenian Caipeiros, the Queen's cousin), the Blushing Monk tavern (E25), the Temple of Paylor/Pelor citadel in the city (E26, E102), and a meeting of city leaders (E26) whose attendees map onto Jon's roster below.

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Provincial capital of Vierteaux, the central province of the Carasian Kingdom, bordering the Morian Kingdom to the north.
- Set on the shore of Lake Blanchet; merfolk hold the lake as their own nation and have a representative in the city's leadership.
- Hosts a Knights of Gidia outpost ("The Den"), a Gidian embassy presence inside a Carasian city, and a Harpers cell.

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Setting & role.** Grillers (population ~9,000) is the provincial capital of Vierteaux, the central province and "breadbasket of the Carasian Kingdom." It sits in a heartland of gentle rolling hills, wooded areas, lakes and rivers — a near-paradise of huge wealthy farmland, private vineyards and breweries interspersed with villages and towns, from which wagon trains flow off to the capital and the other major towns of the realm. Vierteaux borders the Morian (dwarven) Kingdom to the north and the two opposing provinces of Carasa; its northern reaches butt up against the Morian mountains, where logging, forestry and wild pelts are major exports and where orcs and goblins occasionally raid down from the mountains (usually kept busy by the dwarves before they get far). The southern forests remain untamed, drawing the occasional trophy-hunting adventurer.

**Economy.** The city's massive granaries and market are a sight to behold, their structures dominating the landscape, the marketplace deafening with the trade of animals, food and all manner of goods; wagon trains stretch for miles in every direction. Major exports are food, cotton and lumber. Additionally, trade with the Morian Kingdom yields armor and weapons of expert craft, transported both south to the outposts and west to the capital.

**Geography.** Tall yellowed-limestone walls rise out of the surrounding fields; cone-capped towers fly the Carasian banner and the blue Blanchet banner (a knight on a griffin slaying a serpent). The dense thickets around the cerulean Lake Blanchet partly shield a busy port of fishing boats and lake/river-runner vessels. A river flows in from the north, meandering through the countryside and beneath one of the great towers into the city.

**Districts.** Four districts: (1) **The Gleaming Heights** — the inner ring, home to the **Ivory Citadel** from which the **Sapphire Court** presides; set on a raised rampart of stone and earth, its gleaming white walls and golden-domed octagonal keep shine over the city, and the Duke holds court here with the other nobles. (2) **The Greenway** — split by the High Street into Upper and Lower districts, housing most of the nicer homes and the wealthy merchants and businesspeople. (3) **Merchants Mile** — the less glamorous, perpetually noisy industrial and trades quarter where most of the workers, trade businesses and the City Market are found. (4) **The Talons** — the working-class docks and roughest part of town, run largely by the wharf masters who operate like territorial gangs.

**Feature — the Sapphire Caves.** Beneath the city, diving ~300 feet down from the glass lagoon, expansive underwater caves open up, full of stalagmites and stalactites crystallized by the lake-bottom mineral deposits so that they gleam like sapphires when light catches them. The caves run all the way under the city and exit in the middle of the harbor.

**Leadership.** The city is governed by the **Sapphire Court** under **Duke Fenian Caipeiros** (high elf), a mature elf raised in the capital and cousin to the Queen, assigned the post ~15 years ago; mild-tempered and capable but overshadowed by his older cousin and quietly afraid of upsetting her or the people, feeling somewhat like an imposter. Other figures of the court and city:
- **Cerwyn the Ruddy** (human), High Priest of Pelor — a former farmer, wise and deep-spoken but sparing with words, preferring to listen and judge; a practical, beloved religious leader.
- **Joan Revere** (human), the people's leader — a young, fiery woman (mid-20s) pushing for everything to be shared equally and stirring traders and farmers toward talk of strikes and riots if the texere flow doesn't increase. (Play-notes "Joahne Revare," a House of Commons member at the Grier leaders' meeting, E26.)
- **Aru Vam** (merfolk), rule of law — a middle-aged merfolk placed to bridge the gap between the merfolk who hold the lake as their own nation and the people of Grillers; fair and just, protective of her people's rights to the fishery and waterway. (Play-notes "Aurovan," the water person at the meeting, E26.)
- **Guillaume Lacan** (elf), Master at Arms — a young (for an elf), lithe and quick master of his craft who waits for the Duke's direction, fiercely protective of the Duke and the people, and a romantic about knighthood. (Play-notes "Giom Lecom," E26.)
- **Otten the Stern** (half-elf), Deliberator — councilor and court member, a near-permanent resident who leaves only when called back to Alambor Alma; owns a large house/monastery where Archivists stay when traveling Vierteaux; stern, with a tall widow's peak, cold and calculated, currently far more concerned about the stores of texere than other affairs. (Play-notes "Otto the Stern / Ottam," the Deliberator who left for King Felix, E24, E26.)
- **Ozros the Learned** (tiefling), professor/mage at the university — a former slave of a Parathian mage (Nadif Darksky of the Mageocracy of Parathia) who was freed to continue his study in the Carasian capital, focusing on the histories of antiquity, astral cycles and languages.
- **Tanrick "Stonewall" Tornatin** (gnome), Guildmaster of Shipping and Trade — a gruff gnome wholly dedicated to getting the best outcome for those he represents, dismissive of the Duke and often rude. (Play-notes "Tannerack Tantan," guildmaster at the meeting, E26.)
- **Ralph Ironfist** (human), Vindicator of the Knights of Gidia at the Gidian outpost — a large, imposing, clean-shaven man with a strong jaw and cleft chin; a zealous Knight who follows orders without fail and enjoys authority over others. (Same Ralph Ironfist the party knew as a Kierkenwall-side Vindicator, E20–E52; here stationed at the Grillers outpost.)
- **Jonas Liggit**, Commander at Arms of the City Barracks; **Joan Revere** also keeps the Guild Hall.

**Establishments.** Taverns: the **Blushing Monk** (keeper Prior, "Cynthia Knucklebrand"), the **Swarthy Sailor** (Odo Fletcher), and the **Lavish Chateau** (Cyril Prussett). The brothel **Sapphire's Secret** (Marco Silvertongue); the weaponsmith **Ironforged** (Zula Steadyhand); the cobbler **Shoe and Shod** (Patrice Lesney); the general store **Grillers Goods** (Huet Demaret); the magic shop **Arcane Infinity** (Vaxildan Vortelli — see below); the library **Scriptorium** (Tiberius Stormwind); a university building under the tiefling **Ozros the Learned**; the **Temple of Erathis** (High Priest Rhudar Longstride, a centaur) and the **Temple of Pelor** (High Priest Cerwyn the Ruddy); plus the Royal Barracks, City Barracks, Guild Hall, Town Hall and Fisherman's Wharf. (Note: the Kierkenwall city doc reuses this same establishment list as a drafting template; these establishments belong to Grillers.)

**Arcane Infinity.** The city's magic shop, run by **Vaxildan Vortelli** — the same Arcane Infinity proprietor the party knew as **Vaxoldon Vortily**, the man with five simulacrums of himself (E100–E102). Its detailed stock has included a Vicious Maul (360 gp) and Vicious Halberd (370 gp), a Scimitar of Sharpness (1,730 gp), a Rope of Entanglement (4,000 gp), potions of healing up to Supreme Healing (1,350 gp), an Ioun Stone of Intellect (3,000 gp), Boots of the Winterlands, +1/+2 weapons, a wide range of spell scrolls, and a fully-charged **Nine Lives Stealer Rapier** (8,030 gp).

**Factions.** A **Gidian outpost** known as the Embassy, "**The Den**," maintains the relationship with the Carasians, assists with local troubles and larger incursions from the Horde Alliance, and stations men at Draçon when necessary; its Vindicator is Ralph Ironfist. A **Harpers** cell works out of the **Blushing Monk** (tavern/brothel) under **Wise Owl Jacopo**, a merchant within the city — work similar to the Harpers' efforts in the capital, fitting for Grillers' role as the trade hub of the breadbasket where many wealthy merchants are based. (Jacopo as Grillers Wise Owl sits alongside Milo Zulezi, whom play-notes name as the "Grier" Wise Owl, E26, E39; whether both held the post or this is a spelling/role overlap is unresolved (?).)
