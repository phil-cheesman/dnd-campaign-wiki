# The Kings (Gloomville gang)

A Gloomville (Shadowfell) gang led by Skoony, a red tiefling. Etak's lay of the land named the city's three gangs: Sane's Night Reapers, the very violent Vault of Shadows, and the Kings — the rival of Sane's crew, contracted to secure a specific item from a specific place (E124). Sane's job offers to the party included gutting the Kings: kill most (not all) of the gang, with the strict condition that Skoony must not be killed — with his people dead, Skoony would need to ask for higher help, fall into debt, and Sane would step in (E124). The glossary marks Skoony "do not kill" (E124–E126). The unidentified item the Kings were contracted to secure was a live thread through the Gloomville arc and presumably relates to the contested artifact business that ran through Withervale and the Tower of Illmore.
