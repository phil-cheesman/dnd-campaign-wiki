# Widowmakers

Cruucar's former masked mercenary group, led by his arch nemesis General Zax. A herald at King Felix's council reported the Widowmakers over Disacka were struggling in the wet season and needed a relief party (E27). Luzonovian forces clashed with the Widowmakers in Jazacha during the pre-war escalation (E33). The pirate ship Black Widow's crew had worked with both the Widowmakers and Zax on the slave routes around south Jazacha and Freetown (E35). By the Shimmering mine episode the Widowmakers had fallen out of favor with Gidia — General Barnabas's explicit warning: do not wear the mask — making Cruucar's Widowmaker past a liability (E66). The Widowmakers, probably Zax's, swept through the Evershire valley after the dragon's arrival (E71). Most recently, a group posing as merchants ambushed High Harper Koru after he retrieved the relic at the Labyrinth of Ninath — the Widowmakers, with General Zax and a stranger called the Marquee, who took the relic (E139). Zax now hunts the party with six main leaders and roughly thirty hired mercenaries aboard the Black Widow (E147, per the Pirate Trinity arc).
