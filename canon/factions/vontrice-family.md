# Vontrice Family

The wealthiest, most connected family of the Trene, with large land and farming holdings and three villas in Vallon; their signet is two black wings with a ruby (E39). The name "Vontirece" appeared in ledger entries from Pierre Beaubois's effects (E24), and Penelope Vontrice — the matriarch, a member of the Mantel advising Queen Beatrice — was named in Pierre's journal as possibly behind the assassination plot (E24, E37). A disguised meeting with a Carasian dealer left behind the Vontrice signet ring, tying the family to the plot against the party (E39). High Rector Lucia's rundown (E40): brother Robert handles the finances from 100 miles away; the Baron was politely removed from the city for selling slaves; Julia is a by-the-book diplomat in Paratha; Lucas is a playboy with a House of Midnight tab and a penthouse; and Penelope's "adopted niece" Eloise is a reclusive former slave girl with a villa in the Revana District. The grim payoff: the real Eloise Vontrice had been dead at least a month — throat slit, found in a Vontrice sarcophagus among six at the farmhouse — impersonated by the succubus/devil-creature of the Shadowed organization plotting the princess's assassination (E45, E46).
