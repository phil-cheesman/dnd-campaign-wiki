# Shadowed Moon

The supreme rank and figure of Aum Shai, and the name the cult itself first traveled under. A letter read by Max referenced a Shadowed Moon meeting that granted Pierre Beaubois's pet project (pitched by "Viper Beaubois"), set for after the Festival of Amentes (E24). Viola declared the Shadowed Moon himself appointed her (E31). After the wedding assassination the party learned "Aum Shai" is how the Shadowed Moon organization is known — embedded in multiple organizations (E51). Adune briefed that Aum Shai is led by the Shadowed Moon and transitioned to a new leader roughly ten years ago (E52). Crates bearing the sign of the Shadowed Moon, alongside Pierre's ceremonial dagger, were found in the Shimmering mine (E66). The Shadowed Moon gave Nardif Darksi a direct task to retrieve the artifact, impatient that "everything is almost in place" (E115). In the Aum Shai hierarchy it sits at the very top: Fangs → Wolf → Viper → Arch Dragon → Shadowed Moon — "the Shadowed Moon is a him" (E134). At the Shadowmoon Tower the party was told Sir Trent Hightower, the Righteous Hand, is the Shadowed Moon (E134) — but the true reveal came at the Ethereum: Adune was evil the whole time, the Shadowed Moon and leader of the Remenant, the surviving Zeeven Morgenrath Tifar (E136).
