# Aum Shai

The campaign's central conspiracy. After the wedding assassination the party learned "Aum Shai" is the name by which the Shadowed Moon organization is known — embedded in multiple organizations, with arcane effects marking members (E51). Adune briefed them that it is uninfiltratable, led by the Shadowed Moon (a new leader took over roughly ten years prior), and that every member carries a special token and a constantly changing code phrase — Pierre's token was his flying-dragon dagger (E52). Its rank structure: Fangs → Wolf (captain) → Viper → Arch Dragon → Shadowed Moon (E134). Blackwood/Valadon held the Arch Dragon seat and confirmed Viola as a member (E87, E89); later Nardif Darksi served as Arch Dragon, with Rasheed Soltar and Tinkertop as Vipers in Tarlif (E116, E134). Aum Shai killed Quinton's family (E102), ran the slave trade, and gathered the ancient Zeeven artifacts — holding five of the six relics that combined can open a massive arcane gate to bring the Zeeven back (E144). The sixth was already gone when they raided its vault. Adune — the surviving Zeeven lich Morgenrath Tifar — is the true leader; Sir Trent Hightower is his second-in-command (E135, E136, E144).
