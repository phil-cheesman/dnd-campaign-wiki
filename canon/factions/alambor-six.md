# The Alambor Six (the party)

The party's public identity and accumulating titles. Quinton's song "The Alambor Five" (G, D, Em, C) formally named the original party — Noctis, Quinton, Zanim, and Zook (E03). They were sworn in as Defenders of Godo and honorary knights of Gidia (E08), and infiltrated the Beaubois party under the band name "The Magnificent Four" (E10). As Defenders of Sutray they were granted the 170-acre Beaubois Chalet estate — five farmsteads, a granary and mill, and timberworks (E24). High Rector Lucia was furious when they blew up the Vallon bazaar and gave the guards the Alambor Five name (E40); a Goldcrest delivery letter was addressed for delivery "by the Alambor Five" (E55). After lancing the dragon Valadon they were hailed Saviors of Evershire (E90), and King Felix the Cold publicly honored them as Defenders of Sutrey, Proctors of Evershire, and Slayers of Valadon the Black Dread, with 12,000–15,000+ platinum, honorary membership in the Counsel of Felix, and medals granting teleportation-circle access (E100). Max's speech dubbed them "Saviors of Alambor" (E135 notes). After King Felix's murder they were framed as his assassins (E135); two months later Queen Beatrice and King Rupert the Young received the audience of the Alambor Five in pin-drop silence (E137).

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Currently fugitives from Gidia, sponsored by Carasia, Luzonovia, and the Archivists; hunted by Trent Hightower, Zax, and the Merchant.
- Assets: the Beaubois Chalet estate, ships (Wrecked at the Reef), Yankee Williams's crew.
