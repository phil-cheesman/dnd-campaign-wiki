# Vithian Alliance

A theocracy under one religious leader worshipping Cord, established at session zero as a vassal of the Luzonovia empire alongside Treaborg (E00); the Tempest Court is part of the alliance's theocratic structure (E00). The Vithians are fishing-and-raiding pirates. Their coast figured in the artifact hunt: Captain Vex Totem and Zax found a key on the Vithian coast at heavy cost — half the crew lost getting it — before the pair returned from an inland temple covered in blood (E35). During the pre-war escalation, the Vithians (as a Luzonovian vassal state) assaulted areas of Mandalonia, an action connected somehow to King Felix's late wife (E33, noted as unclear). In Ford's account of the turning tide of the war, the Vithians were scattered and pushed off the coast during the battles around Battalonia (E132).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

A Viking-flavored seafaring and adventuring people, the Vithian Alliance is a group of roughly defined regions each ruled by a member of the Tempast Court. Its rulers are three: **Rök** ("Doom"), **Akkeri** ("Anchor"), and **Dagan** ("Daybreak"). Their primary god is Kord ("Tempast"), the storm-and-war god; the people live life to the fullest, are quick to fight, but hold extreme reverence for the gods and the weather. Primary races are humans, then goliaths and aasimar (with some other races present). Approximate population is ~6,000 — a town of ~1,500 plus many small villages along the coasts — and their major settlement is **Drangar** ("Rising Tide").

**Economy and seasonal rhythm.** Their major exports are fishing (primary) and raiding (secondary), the latter bringing back gold, gems, and rare and wondrous items. They raid through the summers, sailing far and wide — chiefly into Jazaaka, but also into the Trine and its vassal states — and in winter they hole up in their towns and villages to eat, drink, and sing of the previous year's tales. The exceptions are those who can't get enough fighting and wander the lands as mercenaries for hire. They have regulated trade with other nations and must trade first through the Luzonovian Sovereignty; they look down on the Luzonovians as stuck-up, cautious, boring city-folk, useful mainly for trading warm clothes, weapons, and grain when needed, and for smoothing over the international disputes their raiding causes.

**Vassalage to Luzonovia.** They became a vassal state of the Luzonovian Sovereignty in **268 P.T.** After a particularly horrible raid on the Carasian capital destroyed much of it and brought the entire Carasian Kingdom to their doorstep, the Tempast Court was told by Kord directly that they must submit to Luzonovian rule — a vision understood as the cooler, wiser heads of the Court keeping the Vithian people from destroying themselves by their own nature (?). As a vassal they are required to field a fleet of ships and warriors year-round and to keep armies that can be called on at a moment's notice to fight for the Sovereignty (when they aren't off raiding, and if the war is interesting enough to them). To be among the people, however, you would never know they are under the Sovereignty's protection, as they treat it as an afterthought.
