# Knights of Gidia

Gidia's knightly order with arcane and holy abilities, the party's longest institutional ally. The party was sworn in as honorary knights of Gidia (and Defenders of Godo) at breakfast in Sultray (E08). Rank order: Righteous Hand, Vindicator, Red Hawk — Maximilian Taldross's rank; Ralph Ironfist, a power-hungry Vindicator, outranks Max (E20, E24). Every member carries a secret encoding object — once obtained, a simple Detect Magic can read all encoded letters (E20). The order maintains a heavily fortified stronghold in Kierkenwall, posts in Grellier (E25, E26), and a base at the Library in Goldcrest, the school Marquise Avesy attended (E54, E56). Max gave the party a Blunder Buster flare device the Knights had been developing (E10). The entire order was deployed during the Luzonovian escalation (E32); Max led "the Fist," a hand-picked subset of ~12 fighters thwarting the Luzonovian invasion (E86, E91). The dark turn: a mole inside the Knights/the Gauntlet worked for Aum Shai (E132), revealed as the order's own leader, Righteous Hand Sir Trent Hightower — the conspiracy's second-in-command (E135, E144). Max was frozen out, then died saving the party at the Ethereum (E136). The party later posed as Knights "sent by Sir Trent" to stall Mandalonian guards (E145).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

The Knights of Gidia (also "the Order of Gidia") are an elite guild of paladins and warrior-mages who serve the Gidian crown, motto **"When enemies strike, we strike back."** They are the empire's primary source of learning and study — a population otherwise not much given to the arcane arts, scholarship, or religious fervor — pairing weapons-craft and scholarship as equally important, and studying their enemies through history and arcane magic so as to pre-empt threats before evil can befall the empire. When any enemy of Gidia breaks laws or agreements or threatens the empire, the Order strikes hard and fast without waiting for the blessings of distant temples or the permission of rulers. They are a tightly knit, like-minded group driven by righteous zeal and a finely honed sense of justice, who take personal responsibility to keep the empire's leadership informed of the machinations of the outside world; the trust and bond they share is held to be infallible, above even their service to the empire's leadership. Their stated goal is to stay armed, vigilant, and ready to smite the empire's enemies, enforce justice through rule of law and knowledge, and enact retribution when necessary — identifying threats (secretive power groups, threatening nation-states, dangerous creatures), watching over them, and being ready to attack.

**Recognition phrase.** The challenge/answer used by the order is **"When enemies strikes, we strike back."** answered with **"Verily watchful we remain."** (?) (verbatim challenge wording from Jon's doc).

**Rank hierarchy (low to high):** Chevall, Marcheon, Redhawk (canon also "Red Hawk"), Vindicator, and the single Righteous Hand at the top. The current Righteous Hand is **Ser Trent Hightower** — in Jon's Kierkenwall notes a broad-chested strong man with jet-black hair and beard just streaked with grey, dark brown eyes, a deep calming voice and a slight Scottish lilt, who grew up outside Ironforge and proved himself as a soldier in the army; he sits on the king's council in Kierkenwall and wears the white cloak and lion of the order. (Canon: Hightower is later revealed as the Aum Shai mole and second-in-command — see his dossier and the narrative above.)

**The trial.** Every knight must pass "the trial" to be inducted: rigorous arcane and (primarily) combat training at The Farm with a chosen weapon; a month surviving alone in the wilderness; a month living as a beggar; and, as proof of commitment to the cause, the interrogation and execution of a traitor to the crown. As knights, all members carry an **arcane focus** that lets them read coded order-only messages — only a holder of the focus can read them.

**Cells / strongholds ("the Fingers," coordinated by the Fist):**
- **The Fist** — Kierkenwall (Kierkenwall). Protects the royal family and the crown's interests in the city, roots out troublemakers and dissent toward the crown, and relays supplies, armor, arcane knowledge, training, and instructions to the rest of the Fingers. Vindicator: **Ser Kern**. (Sources place the Fist's presence at "Grillers" / Grillers Goods within Kierkenwall.)
- **The Library** — Goldcrest. The empire's main center of trade and learning and the primary place of study and religion for both the kingdom and the order, housing a huge library with extensive grounds and study buildings; the texere flows through Goldcrest before distribution. Vindicator: **Matthias**.
- **The Lighthouse** — guards the southern shipping lanes into the Trine waters, protects the order's interests in expanding into Jazaaka, and monitors the vassal state of Maadolonia. Captain: **Bartlett**.
- **The Embassy ("The Den")** — maintains the relationship with the Carasians, assisting them with local troubles and larger incursions from the Horde Alliance; stations men at Dracon when necessary. Vindicator: **Ralph Ironfist**.
- **The Crag** — on the Morian border; defends against the Durgar and other dark creatures that surface from mining, helps keep the peace with the Kingdom of Moran, and learns advanced weapon-making from them. Knight in charge: **Ser Aelshire**.
- **The Farm** — kept away from prying eyes; the order's prison, interrogation, and inquisition arm, where weapons testing, training, and the trial take place. Overseen by **Finnes "The Inquisitor"**.

**Stronghold construction (visual).** All the strongholds are unique but share the same austere feel — simple but very sturdy, meant to withstand a siege. The only ornate or noteworthy features common to all are beautifully carved stone knights and gargoyles tucked into niches and corners throughout each fortress. Knights wear white cloaks and bear the lion of the Knights of Gidia on the breastplate.
