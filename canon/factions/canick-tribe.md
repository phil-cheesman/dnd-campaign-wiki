# Canick Tribe

The red-handprint raider tribe of the Evershire arc — goblins, trolls, ogres, and orcs. Village leader Leonard explained that the Canick Tribe had raided since the Black Dread (the dragon) came about a year earlier, after the dragon killed the guards and the lord's sons and the valley turned bad (E71). The mine (Shimmering Grotto), once family-owned and transferred to Baron Janis, was "destroyed" by the Canick tribe, then taken over and used by Blackwood (E73). Lord Blackwood repeatedly blamed the Canick Tribe for attacks — including the burning of Airdale and Carningsby — that were actually his own cloaked men (E77), and his guard leader Drachen was sent to the Crag "to tell the Canick tribe to chill out" (E73), exposing the tribe as Blackwood's framed-and-paid instrument. Loot from Blackwood's fort included 24 clay jars of severed fingers in vinegar, possibly collected by the Canick Tribe for Viola — later revealed as payment to the guards (E80). The tribe's core dwelt in a cave at a massive rock wall near the Shimmering Grotto: dozens of goblins, trolls, and ogres, 15–20 orcs, and a general with his private guard, plus sapphires and gems; two orc guards there were dragging out a captive — Benjamin, whom the party freed (E75, E77).
