# Volstruckers

Secretive, highly dangerous protectors of the realm of Luzonovia — even Domitus's sources know very little about them (E14). They bookend the campaign's opening: Chias, the figure in sapphire blue silk robes who showed the party around the boat out of Kierkenwall, was a Volstrucker, entrusting the party with a delivery for "the man with the sigil" in Sultray along with the recognition phrases "Verily watchful we remain" and "When enemies strike we strike back" (E01). Their symbol was later identified during the Sultray investigations (E14). Martinet Ludinus Dealeth, the tall council speaker who invited the party to the Ethenium, was flagged as potentially the leader of the Volstruckers (E37). During the wedding-eve investigation a contact declared, "If we are working with you we are the Volstruckers" — the group that revealed the real Eloise Vontrice had been dead for months and that the demon lady was an imposter (E45).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

Per Jon's authoritative worldbuilding, the Volstruckers (colloquially "Scourgers"; motto "Kill one, save thousands") are the elite assassin guild of the Luzonovian Sovereignty — a clandestine organization of arcane assassins, hidden spies, and executioners. They are regarded by most ordinary Luzonovian citizens as ghost stories and urban legends, told to children as bogeymen to warn against disobeying parents or staying out too late; only the Sovereignty's elite are aware that the Scourgers truly exist, and even the Archavisian Order has difficulty verifying the truth about them. The Volstruckers are counted among Luzonovia's most devastating military assets, contributing to the Sovereignty being arguably the most militarily powerful member of the Trine.

The Volstruckers answer directly to their Grand Master, who answers only to the king himself. Every few years the Grand Master hand-picks promising candidates from the Academies throughout the Sovereignty; thanks to the Sovereignty's rigorous recordkeeping and the Grand Master's complete authority and access to all of its records, he is able to keep watch for prospects. Training — in the arcane arts, in assassination, and in the process of transformation — is conducted at a location known only to high-ranking members of the Volstruckers, not even to the king. That transformation, which is called "awakening," involves injections made partly of texere to strengthen the students' transmutation magic; the injections leave noticeable scars across the arms. The arms of the Volstruckers are also covered with black maze-like tattoos.

Volstrucker agents can communicate with one another across long distances by writing a message in arcane ink (the same kind wizards use for transcribing spells), addressing it to any other member, and tossing the letter into a fire; the letter then appears on the person of the agent to whom it is addressed.

**Beliefs:**
- The shadows are your friend.
- Power is meant to be hidden until needed, and then unleashed without pity or remorse.
- Know thy enemy better than they know themselves — and then strike.

**Goals:** Seek out threats to the Luzonovian Sovereignty and destroy them with impunity, regardless of cost or consequence to others; constantly move the Sovereignty toward a position of power, holding that only its leadership can be trusted to rule justly.

**Hierarchy** (ascending): Viper → Shadow Stalker → Reaper → Black Dragon → Grand Master.
