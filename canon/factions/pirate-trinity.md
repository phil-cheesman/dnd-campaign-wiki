# Pirate Trinity

A coalition of pirate captains and their fleets — "a mockery of the Trine" — with three leaders running three ships each: Scar Atol (Pirates Grim), Oaks (Grim Reaper, Executioner), Damned Rose (Scargate Atoll), and Cranson Oakly, a refined business type (Oceans Terror) — the notes list four names against three seats (E147). (DM-confirmed: the **Damned Rose** is a *ship*, not a person or a 4th leader — which resolves the four-names-for-three-seats puzzle.) Every five years the pirates gather at Krakens Reef for a council (E147). A massive weather-controlling sea creature — the Kraken — was plaguing the Trinity; Mallie Grisham had promised to take care of it, which let him lead the pirates of Anchors Rest, and he set the party's price for the final artifact's location: capture the creature and deliver it to the Trinity (E152). Mallie confirmed the Trinity's sea monster is real and that the Kraken was once tamed by the pirate mage Maggie via an arcane collar (E153). After the capture, Mallie traveled alone to the Trinity meeting at Krakens Reef and delivered the Kraken's control (scepter authority), fulfilling the deal — but revealed that Zax, the Merchant, and possibly Sir Trent Hightower had already contacted the Trinity, and that he had shared the party's destination, Jazacha, with them: a confirmed information leak (E156).
