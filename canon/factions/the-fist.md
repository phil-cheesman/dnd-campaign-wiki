# The Fist

Max Taldross's hand-picked strike group within the Knights of Gidia. Six weeks before the Iron Forge events, Adune and the Righteous Hand approached Max and a group of fighters to learn what the Luzonovians were truly up to — by any means necessary; the hand-picked group of about twelve was nicknamed "the Fist" (E84). By the Blackwood Manor arc, Max belonged to "the Fists," a Knights of Gidia subset tasked with thwarting the Luzonovian invasion from Wintervail (E86). The Goldcrest notes compare the Library — the Knights' center of knowledge and "badass warriors" — to "the Fist in Kierkenwall," suggesting it as the order's elite Kierkenwall arm (E54). The Fist's defining operation was at Iron Forge in Wintervail: the Luzonovians, teaming with the Archivists in search of technological inventions and a source of ultimate power, flipped a leading chemist in the Depths and assaulted the Spires of the Kraken; Max (going rogue) pulled the party in, retook a Spire, and freed his captured scouts Roland Blaze and Everdak (E84, E91 — "The Fist Enters the Cavern"). Max's team later retrieved the sentient crystalline power source from Avernus, which Sir Trent Hightower took over guarding while Max was frozen out (E132).
