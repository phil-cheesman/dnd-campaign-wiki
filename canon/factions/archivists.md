# Archivists (Archevesian Order)

A ~10,000-strong order of advisors (flowing lime-green robes/cloaks, purple-stained hands and forearms) and full-plate warriors keeping peace across the Trine. They are keepers of the Arbor Alma — the tree placed by the gods at the end of the Sundering — and makers and equal distributors of texere, the transmutation-magic currency underpinning the economy (E00, E07): the Archivists give each faction a set amount, with the rich getting the surplus at year's end. Founding lore: after the Sundering, three primitive nation states fought over the Tree until the first Archivist, Alon the Wise ("Alan the Wise" in play notes), took control of it ~480 years ago to distribute texere equally and keep the peace (E33). All Archivists can practice transmutation without texere (E139). Zeenoth the Righteous ("Zinoff"/"Zenith" in play notes), the High Architect, conducted the royal wedding and called the summit of the leaders of the Trine (E31, E50). During the Iron Forge arc the Luzonovians were reported teaming with the Archivists hunting technological inventions and a source of ultimate power (E84, E91). Their citadel is a Minas Tirith-like fortress at Donum Vite, a city grown from living trees (~2,000–3,000 people), where the High Architect revealed Adune's true identity; Berrian filled an empty council seat and the Arbor Alma greeted him: "Welcome back, Berrian" (E139). Nardif Darksi was brought into Aum Shai specifically to destroy the Archivists and the old order (E134, E135). Post-Ethereum, the Carasians and Luzonovians met with the Archivists to unite against Gidia (E136, E137).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

**Identity and motto.** The Archevesian Order (the "Archavists") are the keepers and defenders of "The Gift" — the transmutation tree Arbor Alma — and serve as diplomats and sages to the great nations of Alambor. Their motto is "Life above all." The order was founded **17 years after the Sundering** by the great mage **Alon the Wise**, at a time when the world was tearing itself and the Tree apart, harvesting texerine and warring over control of it; he created the order to protect the Tree, distribute its product justly, and keep its own members (all of whom can perform transmutation themselves) from succumbing to its temptations. The order is drawn from all races, nationalities, genders and creeds; they worship none of the living gods but pay respects to the fallen gods in remembrance of their sacrifice. (Note: Jon's prose dates Alon's founding to 17 years after the Sundering, whereas the played-out E33 lore says ~480 years ago and uses "Alan"; the discrepancy is noted (?) and the prose spelling "Alon" is treated as canonical, with "Alan the Wise" retained as a play-notes variant.)

**Recruitment and the two tracks.** Members are identified by their innate transmutation magic, which usually makes itself known before age three; a sect of the order finds and recruits them as monks. Trainees take one of two paths: **the way of the Warrior** or **the way of the Advisor**. Training is held at the temple **Donum Vite** ("Gift of Life," also written "Donum Vitae" in Jon's docs — a self-conflict logged below), where most of the Archevesians live, though many are spread throughout the Trine kingdoms serving as advisors to the ruling groups or wandering as surveyors who report the state of the world back to the high council of elders.

**The harvest and the staining.** An Archivist is recognized by their lime-green cloak and purple-stained arms and forearms. Once a year, as the leaves fall and signal the coming of winter, all able Archivists return to the Tree to harvest the texerine and turn it into texere — the processing that stains their hands purple. The processing is done at the grove of the Tree, after which the texere is doled out to the Trine accordingly.

**Beliefs.** All nations and peoples are your responsibility, act accordingly; respect the balance of life which flows through Arbor Alma; be ever vigilant for those who wish to upset the balance; use your gift of transmutation magic wisely and only for good; preserve the sanctity of the Order, seeking out corruption and temptation and snuffing it out; balance in the realm can take a winding road — look to the destination and not the path.

**Goals.** Protect Arbor Alma, preserve the stability of the gift of texere, and steer the Trine down a righteous path.

**Hierarchy.** Roughly half warriors and half advisors, the order is topped by a single **High Architect**, **Zeenoth the Righteous**. The warrior track is led by the **Masters of the Wild** (one per Trine, each running one of the three citadels); the advisor track is led by the **High Rectors** (one per Trine). The **Deliberator** rank sits just below High Rector on the advisor track (Otto the Stern is a Deliberator). The serving Carasia High Rector is **Lucia**, a long-running party contact.

**Holdings.**
- **Donum Vite** — the temple-city and seat of the High Architect, Zeenoth.
- The grove of the Arbor Alma, where texere is processed. The party saw a white wolf and a unicorn coexisting in the grove (E139); the unicorn is the only one known in Alambor and appears on the order's sigil.
- Three citadels straddle the Trine, each run by a Master of the Wild. The party passed through one of them, **Arcis Sept**, whose Master **Vel Elerius** is plausibly the **Master Vel Alerias** met in E139 (?). The other two citadels and their Masters have not been encountered.

**Festivals and the Order's role in the calendar.** The Archivists are the realm's seasonal heralds. The **Feast of Vita Messis** is a floating fall holiday — the fall of the texerine, announced by the Archevesian order lighting beacons throughout the land to warn that the long winter is coming; a time of reflection, meager food and drink, prayer, and the blessing of fields (which mages must prepare to receive the texere). **Carnival Amantes** (held on the first day of Avandi) is likewise triggered by the order: it is marked by the first bud on the Arbor Alma, communicated to the entire realm by the Archevesians lighting purple flames around the nations, stemming from Donum Vite.

**Texere / the Gift in the wider world.** Transmutation is rare and naturally occurring in only very rare individuals; the gods bestowed Arbor Alma ("The Gift") after the Sundering so that humans who could not generate the magic could still survive the wild, feast/famine weather (a consequence of the storm god Kord being captured and tortured rather than killed). Texere coins, pressed from refined texerine leaves, are spent to cast transmutation spells, with cost scaling by spell level and rarity. The Tree only grows in its one location; attempts by science-focused kingdoms to transplant it failed. The order's distribution of texere is what holds the peaceful sharing of the resource among the three nation states together.
