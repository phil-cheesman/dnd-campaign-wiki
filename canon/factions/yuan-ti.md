# Yuan-Ti

Mythical, reclusive snake-people living deep in the Jazacha jungle — holders of the final Zeben artifact and the focus of the current arc (E156). Mallie Grisham learned the artifact he once traded away now lies with the Yuan-Ti of the city Oojezz, a people obsessive about powerful artifacts; Jameti Costco had been his guide there, "but something went real wrong" (E155). They love magical artifacts: recovering the relic will require a fight or a trade of something extremely valuable (E158). They are more intelligent than the lizard folk and control the jungle's animals via enchanted stones on necklaces; Yuan-Ti are active in daytime while the lizard folk patrol day and night (E158). A typical patrol: a huge-jawed crocodilian tracker, six 8–9-foot lizard folk with glowing chains, and a Yuan-Ti riding a lizard creature (E158). Oojezz sits in a hot, humid bowl-shaped valley between two fang-like mountains, waterfalls spilling down, jungle and buildings interwoven, built in three rings with a great temple at the center (E158). The party has fought an Aboleth with two Yuan-Ti and their lizard mounts (E159, taking four very fine daggers from Yuan-Ti bodies — E160), and infiltrated a five-floor structure disguised as medusas and high-ranking snakes, battling a Hydra, two abominations, and two wizard Yuan-Ti (E161). On **floor three, the Bloodmult Crucible** (an alchemical molting chamber), the party fought ~12 Yuan-Ti — broodguards, malisons, pit masters, an eight-headed Anathema, a Moltborn Brute, and an allied Spirit Naga (E162). On **floor four, the abyssal pit chamber**, a far larger host gathered: a serpent-queen **Marilith** (seven sword arms, parry reaction), **multiple Anathemas** (Entangle, poison/constriction), hooded **Nightmare Speakers** (Hunger of Hadar, Witch Bolt, magical darkness), **Pit Masters** (Eldritch Blast, Counterspell), and **animated serpent statues** (E163). Their floor-four murals depict Yuan-Ti history, **Chizaka**, and the subjugation of the lizard folk. Mallie Grisham broke the fight by Mass-Suggesting most of the host into the central pit, after which the party finished the survivors.
