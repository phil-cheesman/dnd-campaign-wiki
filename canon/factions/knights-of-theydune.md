# Knights of Theydune (the Nine White Knights)

Nine pre-Sundering dark paladins sent to the Shadowfell as punishment. The named members: Shadow Captain, Chief Keeper, Betrayer, Dwemer Lake (the Dwemerlake), Tatanin, Knight of Umber, Dark Marshal, and Undying — one unnamed (E116, E124). Jameti Costco found them in the Shadowfell and led both the Arch Dragon (Nardif Darksi) and Berrian's father Bo to them; Bo was taken, and Berrian's mother presumably followed attempting a rescue (E116, E126). Two cloaked White Knights, seemingly Parathean from the south, spoke to Berrian's mother at Moonbright — she got happy, then left with them (E118). The Nine are HQ'd in the gothic obsidian Tower of Illmore in the Shadowfell, ruled by the nine-foot undead Dwemerlake, guarded by 7.5-foot celestial-looking black-plate soldiers (E126). They seek texair, which enables transmutation — possibly to build a portal back to the surface world (E126). Casamir pitched selling texair directly to the Nine, cutting out middlemen like Yenneth and Elville, who had been skimming (E126). Berrian's father was held caged in the tower with other near-dead men; the party rescued him. Valinor and Bo fought four White Knights very unsuccessfully — each fights with physical weapons plus special spell capabilities (E126). Berrian destroyed the fear-spreading Knight of Umber with a massive 260-damage smite (E126).

## Relationships / whereabouts
