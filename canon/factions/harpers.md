# Harpers

A do-gooder spy network; Noctis is a member, first introduced to them by the girl Kieran (E53). Milo Zulezi, "the Wise Owl" of the Harpers in Vallon, met the party at the Temple of Erathis during the wedding arc (E39). High Harper Koru held Noctis to a promise to find Kieran, missing after being sent to Mandalonia to investigate an under-the-books slave trade (E53). In the Tarlif arc the Harpers operated from a hideout in the Dustfall slums under Shaahim, the area leader — stirring the pot locally as rebels and messing with people at the docks (E131, E132); Shaahim told Noctis "the beginning of my beginning begins with Rasheem" (E115). Harpers watched the Rasheed Soltar house infiltration from outside (E115), and Shaahim's men escorted Jameti Costco's wife and kids by ship to Kierkenwall (E116). Harpers rode hidden with the party in the cart assault on the Shadowmoon Tower (E132); Shaahim later teleported and disguised the party into Kierkenwall (E134) and died at the Ethereum (E136). The Harpers have been tracking relic obsessions underground (Rasheed among them); Koru retrieved the relic at the Labyrinth of Ninath but was ambushed by the Widowmakers posing as merchants, who took it (E139). The Verdant Brotherhood was noted as a similar organization (E102).

## From Jon's worldbuilding docs

The Harpers, also called the **Freedom Fighters**, march under the motto **"Down with tyranny. Fairness and equality for all."** They are an old organization that has risen, been shattered, and risen again several times; their longevity and resilience come from their decentralized, grassroots, secretive nature and the near-autonomy of their members. They keep "cells" and lone operatives scattered throughout Alambor, sharing information with one another only as needs warrant. Their stated ideology is noble and incorruptible — they seek no power or glory, only fair and equal treatment for all, with a particular focus on disbanding the slave trade and freeing those caught in it. Their core beliefs: one can never have too much information; too much power leads to corruption; no one should be powerless; slavery is an abomination and all should be free to make their own choices. They aim to gather information across every region, promote fairness by covert means, thwart tyrants and any leader/government/group that grows too powerful, and aid the weak, the poor, and the oppressed — acting openly only as a last resort. Typical operations include freeing slaves, recovering an artifact that would upset the balance of power in a region, gathering intelligence on a powerful individual or organization, or determining the true intentions of an ambitious political figure or evil spellcaster.

Harper agents are trained to act alone and rely on their own resources; when they get into scrapes they generally cannot count on rescue, though small sects of Harpers of similar race or shared goals do band together, and friendships between Harpers are described as nigh unbreakable. They are masterful spies and infiltrators, using guises and secret identities to build relationships, cultivate informant networks, and manipulate others. All Harpers are taught **thieves' cant** (near-impossible to decipher unless taught), and most bear a thin ring branded or tattooed on their wrists or ankles to symbolize bondage/slavery. They are masters of covert communication via dropped messages, signs, and markers, and they maintain a loyal **network of beggars and the poor** throughout Carasia and most of the other slaving towns, kept loyal through shared goals and payment.

**Hierarchy (rank, ascending):** Watcher → Harpshadow → Nightwalker → Wise Owl → High Harper. Approximate standing strength given by Jon: ~81 Watchers, ~36 Harpshadows, ~18 Nightwalkers, 6 Wise Owls, and 1 High Harper. (One of Jon's docs, the Carasian Kingdom faction note, instead lists the third rank as "Brightcandle" rather than "Nightwalker" — conflict logged below.)

**Cells / bases of operation** (each a distinct hideout, hidden in plain sight in the poorer quarters):
- **The Hive** — in **Vallon**, the Carasian capital. Runs overall operations, distributes intelligence to the other cells, roots out corruption in the city, and coordinates with Carasian leadership on what does and does not warrant Harper attention. Wise Owl: **Milo Zulezi** (per the main overview; the "For Elliot" overview leaves this cell's Wise Owl unnamed — see conflict). *Note: this Harper cell "the Hive" is unrelated to the drow "Hive" of Izzdar/Omago the party has encountered (E104+); same name, different group.*
- **The Monk** (the Blushing Monk) — in **Grellier(s)** ("Grillers"), Carasia's trade capital and breadbasket hub. Work parallels the capital's, focused on the city's wealthy merchants. Wise Owl: **Jacopo**, a merchant within the city.
- **Autumnfall** — near the town of **Poulon**; a smaller cell focused on arcane threats in the surrounding forest (goblin raiding parties, ill-intentioned magic users). Wise Owl: **Sinhe "Sunshine,"** a golden tabaxi.
- **The Docks** — operating around **Maadolonia** / the Trine waters. Gathers intelligence on shipping and the slave trade, infiltrates slaving vessels, and disrupts that traffic. Wise Owl: **"The Protector" Adrir**.
- **Hot Stone** — in **Tarlif**, Parathia. Works to free the tieflings enslaved by the Parathians, gathers intelligence on the mage-council ("Sestet") running the nation, and investigates creatures increasingly emerging from the rift. Wise Owl: a shifter named **Sahim** (canon **Shaahim**), the area leader the party dealt with in the Dustfall slums. *(The cell's named associate roster and its specific base layout are unrevealed DM prep — held in quarantine, not surfaced here.)*
- **Crow's Nest** — based at **Freetown**; a pirate cell that robs the rich to give to the poor and hunts down slave-trade vessels to free those aboard. Wise Owl: **Captain Avantica**, a water genasi pirate-ship captain.

The Harpers operate within the **Carasian Kingdom** ("Carasa"), the wealthiest member of the Trine — a diverse, inclusive collection of nation-states with a wide distribution of wealth (some very rich, some impoverished), which gives the Harpers' anti-slavery, pro-poor mission its footing. The slaving towns they work against extend south into Parathia (e.g. Tarlif, the only major trading port of Parathia, where slaves are the primary export alongside spices, magic artifacts, and silk).
