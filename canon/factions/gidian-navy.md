# Gidian Navy

Gidia's navy, controlling the ports as Adune's and Trent Hightower's war of conquest expanded. The party made the Mandalonia island capital just as the Gidian Navy made port, with word of the dead King Felix spreading fast (E137). Captain McKay of the Gidian Navy was named by Kieran as possibly behind the under-the-books slave trade (E53) and was listed among the expected attendees of Rasheed Soltar's secret Aum Shai meeting, alongside Sir Trent Hightower, Zax, the Goldcrest Merchant, the Easterner (Marquise Avesy), and Rasheed (E142). The party exploited the navy's authority for their infiltration: traveling to Mandalonia by ship, they used Seeming to disguise themselves as Gidian naval forces "sent by Captain McKay" to enter Rasheed's building ahead of the meeting (E142, E143). After Rasheed's death, soldiers mobilized on the Mandalonia docks hunting the "Luzonovian spies"; three ships sat in the harbor including a Man o' War, and the party assaulted a guarded ship of twenty, cut loose, and escaped the harbor with thirty freed slaves plus Kieran (E146). Notes also flag an unresolved situation between the navy and Wrecked at the Reef (E140).
