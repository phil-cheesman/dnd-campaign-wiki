# Beaubois Family

A fallen wealthy Sultray family and the campaign's first major villains. Once personal friends of the royal family, they failed around annum 480 when the crown — having spent its wealth helping the surrounding people — could not help them; the father fell ill and died, and Pierre's wife died, leaving Pierre hating the crown most of all (E09). They sold opiates, dabbled in black magic and family sacrifice, and were driven by townsmen to a chalet 15 miles N-NE of Sultray on the bluffs (E08, E09); at trial Queen Beatrice pardoned them. The household: Pierre, his sister Jeanette, her husband Claude, and their son Patrice, with 20–40 guards (E09). Per the Taldross letter they were in league with the Luzonovians in the plot to kill Princess Anabel (E08, E09); the party infiltrated their party as the band "The Magnificent Four" (E10). Pierre recreated the Spawn of Kyuss and planned a zombie plague on Alambor (E15, E19); Claude and Jeanette poured corrupted texair barrels into Sultray's sewers while Pierre announced "phase 3" and that the Arch Dragon was in place (E23). Beaubois guards assaulted Sultray (E20), and the party fought worms and undead in the chalet catacombs (E22). Their 170-acre chalet estate was granted to the party as Defenders of Sutray (E24).
