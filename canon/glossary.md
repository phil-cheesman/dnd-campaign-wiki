- **The Hollow Years** — the unrecorded era **between the Sundering and the start of Alambor's calendar**, named for the party on a set of history checks in E167 (18/18/19). Everything said of it is that it was **"chaotic, horrible — which is why there are no records"**, and **nobody knows how long it lasted**. Aliases: the hollow years, hollow years. Episodes: E167.

- **The Anchor (astral navigation)** — *"a thought known absolutely to belong to oneself"* — a sequence, memory, poem, prayer, face or melody, familiar enough to reconstruct effortlessly and personal enough that psychic influence cannot imitate its emotional texture. Every Ziven navigator develops one. *"When something in the darkness speaks using your own voice, return to the Anchor."* Then re-establish: **Where am I? Where do I intend to be? Who is doing the intending?** The standing instruction to travellers leaving Ilharess: *"Know where you are. Know where you intend to be. Know who you are. Lose any one of these, and the Sea will decide the others for you."* Aliases: anchor thought, Shared Plane. Episodes: E167.
- **Astral Currents / Astral Winds / Psychic Storms** — the three navigational features of the Endless Between. **Currents** are not substances but *"directional tendencies within planar space"* — a road on which distance itself is easier to cross; the competent navigator asks not *"which destination is closest"* but *"which destination is most connected to where I presently am."* **Winds** are events, not features: regions abruptly acquiring momentum; treat one as **temporary geography** and let it carry the first leg. **Psychic Storms** are *"contamination of perception"* — false landmarks, dead captains' ships, absent companions' voices, **false currents and apparent Color Pools**; during a severe storm, *"assume none are"* real and navigate only on information recorded before entry. Aliases: astral current, astral wind, psychic storm, the Endless Between. Episodes: E166, E167.
