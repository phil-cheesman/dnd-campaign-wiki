# Rolin Blaze

A tiny, jacked halfling first seen on [Leon Steadyfoot](leon-steadyfoot.md)'s riverboat — the boat bearing a chisel-and-saw carpentry sigil — when the party met its crew (E04). He resurfaced years later as a high-level magic user serving [Max Taldross](max-taldross.md): one of Max's hand-picked scouts (one of "the Fist"(?)) who had been ambushed and captured during the Luzonovian assault on the Spires of the Kraken. The party retook the Spire and freed him along with Everdak, and the thankful Roland healed the party up afterward (E84). Max can call or control his agents via a badge.
