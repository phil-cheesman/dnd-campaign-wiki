# Maximilian "Max" Taldross

The party's longest-running ally. A Boromir-looking Knight of Gidia (Red Hawk rank) who first approached Noctis in Sultray (E08), Max recruited the party against the Beaubois plot, revealed the Luzonian-backed assassination plot against Princess Anabel, recounted the Beaubois family's fall from grace, and handed out a sending stone, a Blunder Buster flare device, and escape plans. He was impersonated by the doppelganger assassin Ors (E38–E40). Six weeks before E84 he went rogue: he and the Righteous Hand's hand-picked group of ~12, "the Fist," were tasked to learn what the Luzonovians were truly up to. He could call or control agents (Oran, Roland Blaze) via a badge, teleported the party to Iron Forge, and dove 40 feet off a magic carpet to tackle a guard. Adune taught him to speak in Cruucar's head. Later frozen out of the Knights while Sir Trent Hightower guarded the crystalline power source from Avernus; details of his past had been altered and King Felix kept distant from him. His whole family burned to ash in an attack; his father Aldo/Waldo smuggled the fugitive party into the capital to expose Trent at the war council. Max retrieved his grandfather Sir Edward Taldross's sword from the crypt, gave the "Saviors of Alambor" speech, and died saving the party at the Ethereum (E136).
