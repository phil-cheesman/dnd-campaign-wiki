# Jameti Costco

A tomb-robber ranger and man of mystery — scruffy, lean, older-looking, sunken eyes, single braid, satchel of scrolls and notebooks (including a Tarlif map with surrounding locations crossed off, possibly a Vault search) — a mage/sorcerer and Harper-adjacent figure who can travel between planes. Valinor identified him as the man Berrian's father Borrus "Bo" went to meet: Jameti told Berrian's mother that Bo could be brought back if only she helped, found the White Knights (the Knights of Theydune) in the Shadowfell, and led both the Arch Dragon and Berrian's father to them. The party observed him in Tarlif, met him on the fourth floor of a tower, and overheard the conspiracy at Hotstone: Nardif Darksi had killed Beevus and Keevus over the fake artifact, and Jameti — who knew where the real artifact was but couldn't get in, his family threatened — had two full moons to retrieve it or die with his family. Found in a back room with Rasheed Soltar and Tinkertop, he later came clean, naming Rasheed and Tinkertop as Aum Shai Vipers and Nardif as the Arch Dragon on the counsel. Noctis arranged for his wife and two kids to sail to Kierkenwall under Taldross and Adune's protection. Jameti went to Gidia, met Adune, and Adune sent allies; he emerged from the shadows with Ford, Leon, and Max as one of four allies. He was once Mallie Grisham's jungle guide — "but something went real wrong."
