# Valinor

A tall armored man in a long green flowing cloak, first appearing in Berrian's ominous dream before a tall black spire — alongside nine knights in white cutting down Berrian's father. He found Berrian in the flesh while Berrian was enriching Airdale's crops, bearing news of his father, and later sought him out at the Lavish Chateu, where he shared that Jameti Costco — a man who can travel between planes — was who Borrus "Bo" (Faldorn) went to meet, and that Costco would be in Paratha in roughly 20 days. He summoned the party south to Tarlif (19 days by horseback) and became a traveling companion through the Moonbright and Gloomville arcs: he hunted (finding nothing), stood rear-guard with the party, entered Gloomville's gate to the Desperado, stayed clear-headed through the sleep madness, and in the Tower of Illmore said he believed Berrian's father was somewhere inside. Valinor died and came back resurrected — and dying seemed to cure whatever the mind fog was. He can turn into a giant eagle, flying Cruucar, Noctis, and Vane to the Dark Glen in Sorrowshade. News later came that Valinor and Bo fought four of the White Knights — each using physical weapons plus special spell capabilities — and were very unsuccessful.
