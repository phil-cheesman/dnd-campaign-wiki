# Nardif Darksi, the Nightmare King

A fey drow / dark elf — few are left — described by Adune (who admitted past work with him) as very dangerous and capable of interplanar travel. First met as "the Nightmare King" in the Iron Forge mines: a humanoid with exaggerated features and spectacles floating on a rock ("Welcome to play in my little pond"), a Luzonovian spy scientist who slaughtered all the guards around the Titan Killer research; when his Fire Giant died he teleported away taunting "Follow to the infernal ringing." Under the alias "Iberrian" he sat among the six leaders of Tarlif's Mageocracy, offering Yenneth's people life in exchange for Shadowfell exile and posing as the "savior" of Berrian's mother — whom he and the Gloomville leaders used to smuggle Texair after promising Bo could be brought back. As Aum Shai's Arch Dragon on the counsel he killed Beevus and Keevus over the fake artifact and gave Jameti Costco two full moons to retrieve the real one or die with his family; the Shadowed Moon gave him direct tasks. He admitted he was brought into Aum Shai's fold and seeks to destroy the Archivists and the old order. Confronted atop his tower (a tall, dark, handsome turbaned man with a curled black beard), he questioned the party about their artifacts — and escaped yet again, surviving the Ethereum alongside Sir Trent.
