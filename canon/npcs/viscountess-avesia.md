# Avesia, Viscountess of Sutrey

Shrewd high elf leader of [Surtree](../locations/surtree.md), ruling from the Palace while High Recter Ventius runs the Capital Building. Family of [Queen Beatrice](queen-beatrice.md) of [the Carasian Kingdom](../kingdoms/carasian-kingdom.md), she is gracious and soft-spoken but hard when needed — and Max Taldross warned the party "do not lie to her" (E13). She hosted the party among the city's dignitaries at the dignitaries' play, where a disembodied voice threatened her directly before [Pierre Beaubois](pierre-beaubois.md)'s Spawn of Kyuss attacked (E14). After the party destroyed the creatures she thanked them, asked Max to summon the Knights of Gidia, and granted them palace access and Embassy lodging (E15). She met with the party again following the Beaubois plot's defeat, and Pierre's winged golden dagger reminded her of "The Remnant" (E24/E136). As war loomed, she pleaded with the party to sail to Vallon for High Archivist Zeenoth's summit of the Trene's leaders (E33).

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Kin of Queen Beatrice; her rule keeps Surtree Carasian-aligned.

- Last seen pleading for the party to reach Vallon ahead of the gathering war (E33).
