# Viola, "the Mother"

A tall, cloaked, jet-white-haired swamp deal-maker whom desperate people seek out — Ford called her an experimenter and "sage woman" rather than a witch, capable of brewing exceedingly rare potions. First surfaced as "Viper Viola" in Lorenzo's letters and Pierre Beaubois's shipment book; an Aum Shai member ("Viper"), appointed, by her own claim, by the Shadowed Moon himself: "Payment is always received no matter the cost." She blames the Archivists for her parents' deaths. Long ago she was the woman Baron Janis loved; his father banished her, the child never born — and she cursed LJ into a flightless bird. She haunted Quinton's dreams, standing over his begging mother in Norbury and telling his sister his mother's time was up ("I'm running out of time"), and held the party, LJ, and Evershire under a creeping cold-curse "grip," worked through the Baron's advisor Mallory. In league with Blackwood's faction. The party tracked her east of the marsh as her voice taunted them; Benjamin killed her with a critical javelin throw (E87), and Cruucar took her head in his backpack (later tossed out at Blackwood's). Her 10x10 hut yielded poison and soul-removal books, a Vampyric Potion, the Galas Var walking cauldron, clay dolls, a shapeshifting potion, and a magic-immune poison.
