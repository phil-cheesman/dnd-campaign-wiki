# Queen Beatrice

Queen of Carasia, "the Lion's Blood." A Sutrey play told her origin: her father Raymond the Kind was killed in a Horde ambush south of Dracone, after which Beatrice slaughtered the whole Horde. She pardoned the Beaubois family at trial, and was for a time fighting the tribes to the south. At the summit over the Anabel assassination plot she attended with Penelope Vontrice, chief advisor Autumn (a role similar to Adune's), the Queen's Guard leader, High Rector Lucia, and her son Prince Thomas — the royal-wedding groom — alongside her unmarried life partner Volo, a proven soldier. She asked the party to serve as Thomas's secret personal protectors throughout the wedding, officially with "no dealings" with the queen. After the wedding assassination she addressed the people while the party was taken into a side room; forged letters (placed in her hands via Robert Kavney) damned the party, and Ralph Ironfist and Ottam carried intelligence from King Felix to her. She banished the party until the prince was avenged. Later, with King Rupert the Young and an Archivist advisor, she received the Alambor Five in a pin-drop-silent audience, declared war on Luzonovia, and — jointly with the King of Luzonovia, after the factions stopped fighting — tasked the party to gather everything on Adune. Her 1,000-ship armada sails for Rynestag, Luzonovia's capital, on the 25th of Rovana.
