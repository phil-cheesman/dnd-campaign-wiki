# Leon Steadyfoot

A 7'8", ~430 lb shaved-head, tattooed goliath craftsman and long-running party ally. **Not to be confused with [Leon Janice "LJ"](leon-janice-lj.md)** — a different person entirely; the bare name "Leon" in the notes can refer to either, so resolve by context (goliath carpenter = Steadyfoot; cursed bird / Baron's son = LJ).

He first appears early as the boatman who ferried the party up the river (E04–E05), with a girl back home named Thary. He ran the **Iron Chisel**, a workshop in Surtree, where he built **Zanim's prosthetic leg** after Zanim lost his foot to hobgoblins; the party paid him 100 platinum and a large gem for work there (E13–E15). The tiny jacked halfling Rolin Blaze was first seen on Leon's boat (E04).

Later he served as the party ship's **bosun and carpenter** (E54, E61), retiring when Roland took over the business, then rejoining the crew and keeping its detailed records. At the **Battle of Dorcha Cliffs** he lit the torches that burned the Mandalonian fleet on the wide river "called the sea," earning a medal, after which Max recruited him for his team (E132). He resurfaced at Tarlif as one of four allies — alongside Ford, Max, and Jameti Costco — who emerged from the shadows for the reunion before the Shadowmoon Tower assault (E132).

He died a hero at the **Ethereum** (E136): smuggled into the war council, he burst into the room with Ford and Max right before the blast that killed the king, and was among those who fell saving the party (with Max Taldross and Shaahim). His body was recovered.
