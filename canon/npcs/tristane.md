# Tristane

An elf or half-elf associated with the [Archivists](../factions/archivists.md), the order that makes and distributes [texair](../items/texair.md). The DM's reference portrait shows him in green-and-gold Archivist robes holding a book and a sprig of herbs, with violet-stained hands — an Archivist herbalist (?). He gave the party "the rundown" on a texair theft, leading them to kill the bandits who stole it and return it for 150 gp on their way to [Surtree](../locations/surtree.md) (E07–E08). At Prince Thomas's wedding in Vallon, a Tristane — together with the best man Justin — helped women into the prince's chambers through the front door (E44); whether this is the same man is unconfirmed (?).
