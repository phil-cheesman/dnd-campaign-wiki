# Zeenoth the Righteous

High Architect of [the Archivists](../factions/archivists.md), the order's single topmost authority — an old man in simple purple and green robes with long white hair and a braided beard (E139). He conducted the royal wedding of Princess Anabel and Prince Thomas in the Halls of Wonder (the invitation named him "the high architect of the archivists Zinoff the Righteous," E20), and when the best man Justin stabbed the prince and threw down a portal, Zeenoth — his magic first crippled by a restricting band Justin had placed on him — closed the portal as fire-horse riders and green worm creatures poured through (E50, E51). The party learned the high archivist trusts them (E51). He summoned a summit of the leaders of the Trene at [Vallon](../locations/vallon.md) (E33, E37). In the aftermath he debriefed the party: the letters Queen Beatrice read were forged at an extremely high level, he shared the lore of the bound sea demigod Uquatoa and the Cloven crystals, examined the cursed LJ (a True Polymorph + Wish only the original caster can undo), and invited them to the Archivist lands at Callum Heights (E52). Later, as the elderly High Architect "Zenith," keeper of the Donum Vite and the Grove, he received the party at Loflorien and revealed [Adune](adune.md)'s true identity as the lich Morgenrath Tifar (E139).

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Seated at Loflorien in Donum Vite, the Archivist temple-city; keeper of the Arbor Alma Grove (E139).
- Long-standing ally of the party; trusts them and has invited them into the order's lands (E51, E52, E139).
- Works alongside [Adune](adune.md) (a fellow Archivist) early on, though by E139 he names Adune the enemy Morgenrath Tifar and regrets not acting against him sooner.
- One step above Master Vel Alerias of Arcicept in the order's hierarchy (E139).
