# Lord Blackwood / Valadon the Black Dread

Special Regent of Evershire, appointed by Baron Alexander Janis after the "Black Dread" — a black dragon breathing acid — killed the Baron's twin sons and orcs and goblins devastated the land ten years before. Blackwood (6'6", slicked-back hair, sunken jawline, curved bony nose, piercing dark eyes with a hint of yellow-green) "cleaned things up" but proved a slumlord taxing the land, suspected of alliance with the very raiders he claimed to remove, with Viola in league. Divine Sense read pure black evil off him. He held the Arch Dragon seat of Aum Shai, confirmed Viola's membership through a contact, and aimed to take over the world using texair and Aum Shai; he ran the Shimmering mine, kept severed heads (Leofrene's, Elias's) floating in vintage wine, and froze Cruucar and Berrian with Mallory's power. After hosting the party for dinner at his moated manor, he declared he would "complete the revenge he started 10 years ago," transformed into the dragon Valadon (80-foot wingspan), and took the Baroness to his lair in the Bandersnatch Mountains. The party fought him there; Able Foster threw LJ, who lanced him twice, killing him (E90). King Felix honored the party as Slayers of Valadon the Black Dread.
