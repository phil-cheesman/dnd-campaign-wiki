# Pierre Beaubois

Eldest of the Beaubois siblings (likened to Lucius Malfoy) — the sober one, embittered by his wife's death and hating the crown most of all after his father fell ill and died. Head of the chalet household with sister Jeanette, her husband Claude, nephew Patrice, and 20–40 guards. The party infiltrated the chalet to search his second-floor room/study; his coded letters required an arcane cipher key carried on his person, and Evac found crates marked with the sign of the Shadowed Moon plus Pierre's ceremonial flying-dragon dagger — his Aum Shai token. The coded book "the Sundering" (~500 years old) used the same cipher as his workshop letters. Pierre recreated the Spawn of Kyuss — a worm-plague undead created in ancient times by Kyuss, high priest of Orcus — and unleashed them at the Sutrey theater, where worm-covered creatures tore the queen actress apart on stage as Pierre laughed, a disembodied voice threatening Avesia and the city with vengeance for his family. The Shadowed Moon meeting granted his pet project (pitched by "Viper Beaubois"); he announced phase 3 with the Arch Dragon in place, needing an in-person trial to test potency. His shipment book named Viola; his journal named Penelope Vontrice as possibly behind the assassination plot. Queen Beatrice pardoned the Beaubois at trial. His matching dagger later appeared on Drachen, leader of Blackwood's guard.
