# Sir Trent Hightower

The Righteous Hand and leader of the Knights of Gidia — a tall silver fox in plate armor and cape, the only armed man at King Felix's council, expert swordsman (E27). Persuaded by Adune, he sent Max and "the Fist" on missions including one to kill or capture "Berrian" of the Tarlif Mageocracy; he later requested Max personally guard the king, then froze Max out of the Knights while personally guarding the sentient crystalline power source retrieved from Avernus. The bombshell: the Seal of Aum Shai revealed Sir Trent as the mole — the Shadowed Moon, Adune's second-in-command and leader of the Aum Shai conspiracy. The party infiltrated the war council under Waldo Taldross's identity to expose him, right before the massacre that killed King Felix. He survived the Ethereum and slipped away unblamed with the Nightmare King. Since then he has used the chaos to expand Gidian control, combed the seas and discovered the Labyrinth of Ninath, saw Rasheed's dead body (calling off the Aum Shai docks meeting with Captain McKay, Zax, and the Merchant), and — at the Atium antechamber — a 7'5" Sir Trent strode out of a mirage and beat the party so badly they should have died. Per Mallie, he has possibly contacted the Pirate Trinity.
