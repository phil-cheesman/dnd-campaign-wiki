# Rasheed Soltar

Dock master of Tarlif ("Have not forgotten") and an Aum Shai Viper — Jameti Costco named him and Tinkertop as Vipers, with Tinkertop the mastermind. He offered to help Quinton's band get popular if they played for him for free, and his muscle lost 150 gold to the party at dice in the Flying Carpet. Rasheed ferries slaves out of the city to Mandalonia and everywhere, smuggles items, and was building something; he relocated to Mandalonia and ran the Whispering Stacks, his main base of operations. He declared Jameti would die within a week. He captured Kieran — the Harper spy inside his slave-trade organization who had discovered information about the final artifact — and interrogated/tortured her ahead of a planned docks meeting with Sir Trent Hightower, Captain McKay, Zax, and the Merchant (with the Easterner/Marquise Avesy also listed). Disguised as Gidian Navy sent by Captain McKay, the party infiltrated his building; a failed domination attempt exposed them, Noctis killed his primary lieutenant, and a full battle in Rasheed's chambers ended with Rasheed defeated, killed, reanimated, and interrogated — revealing the sparrow calling card of Mallie Grisham's Fortune's Favor. With guards mobilizing outside, the party stalled by posing as Knights of Gidia claiming Rasheed had fled under suspicious circumstances. Sir Trent saw the dead body and the Aum Shai meeting was called off. Confirmed dead E145.
