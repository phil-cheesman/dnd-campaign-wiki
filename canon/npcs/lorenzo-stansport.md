# Lorenzo Stansport

A texair shipper from Galsia first named in [Pierre Beaubois](pierre-beaubois.md)'s letters (E24), Lorenzo presented himself as "Sir Lorenzo Stansport," a burly, dark-haired human warrior in a green cloak and brown leather armor who fought with a large two-handed glaive. At King Felix's court he raised his chronic [texair](../items/texair.md)-raiding problem and tied the party to escorting his shipment, meeting north of Kierkenwall (E27). High Chancellor [Adune](adune.md) warned the party against him — for four months his shipments had been raided while his secretive Galsian mercenaries returned unhurt and the Gidian cart crews ended up dead (E28). The escort was a setup: when the party found the texair cart raided, Lorenzo turned on them and revealed his true form as [the Oni](ooni.md), a blue-skinned ogre-mage fiend (E29). Looting his people exposed his frame job — forged letters branding the party as bandits, addressed to spymaster Robert Kavney and the Baron, plus a letter to "Viper [Viola](viola.md)." [Ford](ford.md) the gunslinger arrived to help. Lorenzo escaped behind a concealing cloud and resurfaced in the swamp, shrugging off an arrow and healing (E30). By the time the party divided spoils, [Cruucar](../characters/cruucar.md) claimed Lorenzo's glaive — a heavy, non-magical two-hander (E32).

## Relationships / whereabouts
- Named in Pierre Beaubois's shipment book/letters; Viola also appears there, linking the two (E24, E29).
- Protected by spymaster Robert Kavney, who swapped the party's letters for four forged ones placed before the queen (E27–E29).
- Galsian mercenaries served as his loyal, unhurt escort; expendable Gidian crews were the ones killed (E28).
- Last seen alive in the swamp in his true Oni form, escaping and healing (E30); his glaive ended up in Cruucar's hands by E32.
