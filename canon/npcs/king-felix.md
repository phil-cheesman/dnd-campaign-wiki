# King Felix the Cold

King of Gidia and father of Princess Anabel (he personally stopped Vane's approach to her: "This is my daughter Anabel") and of Duke Arden, his smart, fun-loving second son. His inner circle: right hand Percival Degraves (a childhood friend), chief archivist Hyrector Revaneous, High Chancellor Adune, peacekeeper Robert Kavney, supreme commander James Royce, righteous hand Sir Trent Hightower, and master of commerce Aldo Taldross. Trent noted the King was troubled over his late wife and his threatened daughter; the Vithian assault on Mandalonia connected somehow to his wife (unclear). For unknown reasons Felix was kept distant from Max Taldross, with whom he shared a bitter history. After the party slew Valadon, Felix received them before a huge crowd as Defenders of Sutrey, Proctors of Evershire, and Slayers of Valadon the Black Dread, rewarding them with 12,000–15,000+ platinum, honorary membership in "the Counsel of Felix," and medals granting realm-wide access and teleportation-circle use. At the war council — which the party had infiltrated under Waldo Taldross's identity to expose Trent — a wall of force trapped the party and a devastating spell killed many, including the King: murdered by Adune, with the party framed for the assassination and made enemies of Gidia.
