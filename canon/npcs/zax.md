# Zax (General Zax)

A big black dragonborn — bigger than Cruucar — and Cruucar's arch nemesis and former commanding officer in the Widowmakers/Golden Company, who oddly forced his troop to attack a fortress in Jazacha, a place of lizard people and ancient treasures. His name appeared in the contact ledger alongside Vontrice, Lorenzo, and Viola. With the pirate Vex Totem he hunted the sea-power artifact: a key was found on the Vithian coast at heavy cost, Vex and Zax returned from an inland temple covered in blood, and Zax then snatched the scroll and ran — leaving each side with only half the map. In Evershire lore he was called the dragon Valadon's "dog," at sea but set to return and run Evershire. With the Widowmakers — six main leaders and roughly thirty well-trained hired mercenaries — he ambushed High Harper Koru at the Labyrinth of Ninath (posing as merchants, with the stranger called the Marquee) and took the relic. He was an expected attendee of Rasheed's Aum Shai docks meeting alongside Sir Trent Hightower, Captain McKay, and the Merchant, has contacted the Pirate Trinity, and now actively hunts the party aboard the Black Widow — a skull-flagged ram ship whose crew wear a woman's-face tattoo and work the slave routes around south Jazacha and Freetown. The obsidian orb has spoken to Cruucar in Zax's voice.
