# Leon Janice "LJ"

**Not to be confused with [Leon Steadyfoot](leon-steadyfoot.md)** — a wholly different person (the goliath carpenter who built Zanim's leg). LJ here is the cursed bird-son; the bare name "Leon" in the notes is ambiguous between the two — resolve by context.

Baron Alexander Janis and Baroness Elizabeth's eldest son, a twin. When the Black Dread devastated Evershire, his two siblings died to the dragon, but LJ was never seen falling and could never be found — the Baroness never saw him die. He had in fact been cursed by the witch Viola into a flightless, foul-smelling bird (a combination of True Polymorph and Wish, per Zeenoth the Righteous — only the original caster could undo it; the options were to convince, kill, or force her to turn him back). The party carried him for a long stretch (he was tied to Berrian's elk Gregory as a steed, hid in the long grass, and featured in Quinton's song "Take Him Home, Dragonborn" as part-time captain of Wrecked at the Reef). He suffered the same cold-curse illness as the party. After Viola's death broke the curse and Mallory's death cleared the mind-grip on him and the Baron, the truth emerged: LJ is the Baron and Baroness's lost son. He had once wounded the dragon before it took his mother to the Bandersnatch Mountains. In the lair fight, Able Foster threw him and he lanced Valadon twice, killing it, falling limp beneath it — an earth elemental pulled him out alive. With the Baron dead, Leon took control, held court at Corwell Keep with Valadon's head on a wagon, pardoned Blackwood's men, and granted the party 10% of the treasure plus Blackwood's Manor.
