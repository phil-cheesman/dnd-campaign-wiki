# Mallie "Molly" Grisham, the Sparrow

Pirate captain of Fortune's Favor — sparrow flag, big poofy hat, short, very fast, with high cheekbones, piercing blue eyes, and a scar. Under Divine Sense he reads as inherently good but complicated, darkened by pressures. Confirmed to be the same person as "Shauwn O'Malley," who delivered the royal wedding letter (E20). His sparrow calling card was left where the final Zeben artifact was stolen: drunk Archivists tipped him to a relic beneath the seas, and he navigated the fog-bound Veiled Ocean to the underwater Temple of Fenreal and retrieved it, then traded it away for something very important to him (left blank in the notes) — it now lies in the Jazacha jungles with the artifact-obsessed Yuan-Ti of Oojezz. At the Crow's Ledger in Anchors Rest he set his price: capture the Kraken plaguing the Pirate Trinity. The party did; Mallie controlled it with a scepter and chain, delivered it at Krakens Reef, and so leads the pirates of Anchors Rest. He guided the four-ship armada to Jazacha and the jungle expedition (five of his men died), and burned the Wish spell stored in his bracelet to wish Zuk'taal away, leaving him dejected. He also leaked the party's destination to the Trinity and warned that Zax, the Merchant, and possibly Sir Trent Hightower had contacted them.
