# Ford (Fjord)

A gunslinger from Aberdeen, capital of Dalacia, and Max Taldross's friend — the promised backup who arrived during the Lorenzo/Oni ambush at the texair cart (E29). He investigated Viola for the party, describing her as an experimenter and "sage woman" rather than a witch, and looked into the letter and shipment connecting to her. Zanim left the party to meet Ford under a cover story of going to fight in Aberdeen; Ford later recounted the Battle of Aberdeen, where the Aberdeeners hailed a new leader, "Zanim the Bold." In Evershire he had free reign from guard captain Elias (though Blackwood's people disliked him) and introduced the party at Corwell Keep to the sickly Baron and Elizabeth — the visit where detection revealed darkness around Mallory, Leofrene, and Atwell. He helped repel orc invasions in Dalacia and Evershire after the party slew Valadon, stayed in touch with Max, met up with Wrecked at the Reef, and was permitted to deploy the crew in battles around Battalonia. He rode disguised with Shaahim, Max, and Leon escorting the smuggled party, took part in the planned Shadowmoon Tower assault, and burst into the council room with Max and Leon at the Ethereum massacre (E136). Evac once found traces of three rifle-like weapons that outrange even Ford.
