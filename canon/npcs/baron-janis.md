# Baron Alexander Janis

Baron of Evershire at Corwell Keep — sickly, with a scarred arm, dining with a court of eight at a large dark oak table when Ford introduced the party. His tragedy frames the Evershire arc: ten years earlier the Black Dread's acid attack killed his twin sons Kance and Clarence, while his eldest, Leon Janice, was never seen falling but could never be found. In the aftermath he appointed Lord Blackwood as Special Regent, agreeing since his sons had died — installing the disguised dragon over his own land. Barkeep George's history added the deeper wound: before the baroness, the Baron deeply loved a woman with whom he had a child; his father found out, the mother was banished, and the kid was never born — that woman became the swamp witch Viola, "the Mother." The Baron was mind-controlled through his Chief Advisor Mallory (recommended by Blackwood), a blood-domain cleric through whom the Mother worked her grip magic; councilman Atwell talked shit about him over "illicit affairs." The party found him dying in the prison cells with LJ among twelve prisoners. With Mallory dead, his and LJ's minds fully cleared — and the revelation landed that LJ is his and Elizabeth's lost son — but the Baron died around the dragon's fall, leaving Leon in control.
