# Adune (Morgenrath Gafar)

High Chancellor of Gidia and long-time party patron — a charismatic, handsome young man on King Felix's inner council who briefed the party on Aum Shai, explained Potentium (texair pushed to volatile full potential), revived Zanim's dead body at the royal wedding (E50), gave the party his coin-pin symbol of office, teleported them across the realm, and once sent them to hell. He admitted past work with the Nightmare King and ran his own agents (White Lantern, passphrase "The Sparrow Sent You"). Revealed as the true Big Bad, planned for years: Adune is Morgenrath Gafar, a 1,200-year-old drow lich and surviving Zeeven whose wife and sons died in the Sundering. He hates the gods and the mortal races and aims to bring the Zeeven back by opening a gate to another dimension. He leads Aum Shai / the Remenant (the "puppet master" behind the veil), murdered King Felix at the war council with a devastating spell — framing the party — and cracked open and absorbed the artifact stolen from Hell, his eyes glowing blue: a fragment of Omago, chief architect of the Hive, now lives within and energizes him. He holds four (later five) of the six Zeben relics and was sly, changing his appearance many times.

## Before the Sundering — the record in Ilharess

The party searched his name in the libraries of [Ilharess](#) after E167 and found him in the
archives as **Councilor Morgenrath Tifar of [House Tifar](#)** — *"Councilor of Arcane Law,
jurist, scholar and diplomat,"* **670 PS–?**, *"presumed lost during the Sundering."*

What the Ziven record says about him, in short:

- He wrote the foundational Ziven treatise on the limits of magical power, source of the still-cited
  **Tifar Principle**: *"Power does not confer authority merely because lesser power cannot resist
  it."* He applied it to the gods as readily as to archmages.
- He **personally knew [Erathis](#)** — *"from Erathis perhaps more than any living teacher."*
- He was a **peacemaker**: he argued against the war, mediated between the Ziven leadership and the
  divided gods, advocated for **the [Gilda](#) and other subject peoples**, and **declined a
  promotion** rather than spend three years away from his son's childhood.
- His reputation went **moderate → naïve → disloyal.** *"I stand upon Maena. That should be
  sufficient."*
- His wife **Zarra** and sons **Minonet**, **Valtel** and **Illrin** are all recorded **deceased**
  after violence at **[Phemathis](#)**. **He is never recorded dead.** His Council entries decay
  from *Absent: Diplomatic Assignment* → *Absent: Phemathis Emergency* → *Absent* → nothing. The
  Sundering follows soon afterward.

This corroborates the E139 reveal — *a surviving Ziven whose wife and sons died in the Sundering* —
and sharpens it: **they died at Phemathis, before the Sundering, not in it.** It also explains
Jon's E167 confirmation that **Morgenrath blames and hates all the gods, Betrayers included.**
Full detail and quotations on [House Tifar](#). *(Source: Jon's library-research handout,
17 Aug 2026.)*
