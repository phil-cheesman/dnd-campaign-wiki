# Adune (Morgenrath Gafar)

High Chancellor of Gidia and long-time party patron — a charismatic, handsome young man on King Felix's inner council who briefed the party on Aum Shai, explained Potentium (texair pushed to volatile full potential), revived Zanim's dead body at the royal wedding (E50), gave the party his coin-pin symbol of office, teleported them across the realm, and once sent them to hell. He admitted past work with the Nightmare King and ran his own agents (White Lantern, passphrase "The Sparrow Sent You"). Revealed as the true Big Bad, planned for years: Adune is Morgenrath Gafar, a 1,200-year-old drow lich and surviving Zeeven whose wife and sons died in the Sundering. He hates the gods and the mortal races and aims to bring the Zeeven back by opening a gate to another dimension. He leads Aum Shai / the Remenant (the "puppet master" behind the veil), murdered King Felix at the war council with a devastating spell — framing the party — and cracked open and absorbed the artifact stolen from Hell, his eyes glowing blue: a fragment of Omago, chief architect of the Hive, now lives within and energizes him. He holds four (later five) of the six Zeben relics and was sly, changing his appearance many times.
