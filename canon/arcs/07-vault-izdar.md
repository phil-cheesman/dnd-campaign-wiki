# Arc 07 — The Vault of Izdar (E103–E113)

## Story so far

After an unrecorded session (E103, "Nameless Episode" — no notes), the party descended into the Great Rift, the canyon scar left where the gods Carinian and Torog died fighting. Five days down, at the bottom of a 10,000-foot spire-scape, their guide Wahle delivered them to a ruined ancient temple soaked in evil — the Vault of Izdar, also called the Hive — and promptly left (E104). An obelisk bore the warning inscription of Izzdar the Undying, and a chained book listed nine betrayer gods.

The Vault was a megadungeon keyed to those gods, and the party spent ten sessions stripping it. They matched obsidian symbols to deities — Zehir's snakes, Lolth, Bane, Vecna ("Never reveal all that you know"), Gruumsh, Tiamat, Tharizdun — and survived the rooms behind each: a crystal sarcophagus holding a gold-chained corpse and a mace (E106), flying skulls and winged fiends, blood rooms and zombie vats, an iron-golem chamber they wisely skipped. Casualties of the place's strangeness piled up: Quinton was transformed into a woman (E105), Vane was briefly petrified by a popping eye (E109), and every attempted rest was interrupted by sounds of hammering, chains, and sawing bone.

The Vault also taught. A dead guardian called Withers (Gourah), serving since before "the time of corruption," dissolved a texair coin as "weak, diluted" before attacking (E108) — his journal held lore on the Order of Torag, the Hive, and Potentium, the self-sustaining arcane energy harnessing all seven arcane arts. Paintings named the Hive's archmages — Ninath, Faldryal, Zarryien, Ilharius, Velonim — names that matched the drow relics the party had carried since the Beaubois catacombs. Notes on Omago, arch mage of transmutation, and the Zeeven drow tied the dungeon to the campaign's deepest history.

Collecting ten eyeball orbs opened the inner sanctum: the lair of Beltraz the Unseen, a beholder under a black silk drape (E109). The fight killed Benjamin, but Noctis stunned the creature off a wall-run and Berrian punched through its body (E110). The party looted nine treasure alcoves, met Jameti Costco inside the Vault (E111), beat a maze, bone minotaurs, and 64 terracotta orc warriors, and walked out with a fortune — including Comara Blackfire's spellbook, the Nine Lives Stealer sword, and a simple unadorned adamantium scepter whose significance nobody yet grasped (E113).

They emerged from the canyon at Tarlif, the slave-trading mageocracy of Paratha, at level 12 — carrying, though they didn't know it, one of the six keys to the apocalypse.

## Key outcomes

- Potentium, Omago, the Hive archmages, and the Zeeven's pre-Sundering order connected to the party's relics.
- Jameti Costco met in the flesh.
