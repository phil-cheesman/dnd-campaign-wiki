# Alambor — Campaign Overview

Alambor is a world 499 years after the Sundering, when the drow Zeeven Dynasty defied the gods and half the pantheon died. The economy runs on texair — transmutation-magic currency made by the Archivists from the spice of the Arbor Alma tree — and the great powers (trade-empire Gidia, hard-pressed Carasia, inward Luzonovia, the mageocracy of Paratha, and more) circle each other in a slow slide toward war.

The party — first named the Alambor Five in Quinton's song at Carstone, later the Alambor Six — began as shipwrecked nobodies carrying a dead man's letter to Sultray. That letter pulled them into the Beaubois conspiracy: a disgraced noble family brewing an Orcus-flavored zombie plague and serving a hidden organization, the Shadowed Moon (Aum Shai). Stopping Pierre Beaubois made them knights and landowners; failing to stop the assassination of Prince Thomas at the royal wedding made them exiles, and Queen Beatrice declared war on Luzonovia on forged evidence.

The middle campaign is the fugitive years: a stolen-ledger heist in Goldcrest that put their faces on wanted posters, the liberation of Evershire from "Lord Blackwood" — actually the black dragon Valadon — a detour into Avernus itself to steal a crystal power source (the Potentium, the "Titan Killer") for the Knights of Gidia, and the looting of the Vault of Izdar, a betrayer-gods dungeon at the bottom of the Great Rift that yielded Zeeven relics whose purpose no one yet understood.

Everything converged in Tarlif and the Shadowfell: the party rescued Berrian's parents from the Knights of Theydune, unmasked the Nightmare King as Aum Shai's Arch Dragon, and learned the relics they carried were gate keys. Then the campaign's great betrayal landed — High Chancellor Adune, their patron since Kierkenwall, is the Shadowed Moon: Morgenrath Tifar, a 1,200-year-old Zeeven lich who murdered King Felix, framed the party, killed Max Taldross and his family, absorbed the Avernus power source, and now holds five of the six gate keys he needs to bring back the Zeeven.

The current arc is the race for the sixth relic — through pirate fleets, a captured Kraken, and the Yuan-Ti city of Oojezz in Jazacha — while Adune's Gidia conquers the map. A known handout ("Fallout & Campaign Arch 3 Kickoff") confirms explicit macro-arc breaks; the recaps don't record its exact placement, but the two clearest fault lines are the post-wedding fallout (E51→E52) and the post-betrayal time skip (E136→E137).

Along the way the party buried Zook, Zanim, Evac, Benjamin, and Colossus, and rebuilt around Berrian, Cruucar, Noctis, Quinton, Torgoth, and Vane.
