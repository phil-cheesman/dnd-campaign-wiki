# Arc 05 — Evershire and the Black Dread (E67–E90)

## Story so far

Quinton left to save his mother (E67), and the reshaped party — with Berrian and his fey elk Gregory — crossed the monster-haunted Grasping Moore by magic carpet, killing Grey Renders and a seven-headed mud hydra (E68–E69). They emerged into Evershire, the oldest Gideon settlement, to find a valley dying: a village burning under Canick Tribe raiders. The fight cost dearly — Zanim fell from the sky in eagle form and died (E70).

Evershire's sickness had a name. A year earlier the "Black Dread" — a dragon — killed the Baron's sons; then orcs and goblins ravaged the land; then Lord Blackwood arrived as Special Regent, "fixing" everything while taxing it to death. At Corwell Keep the party found Baron Janis sickly and surrounded by corrupted advisors — Mallory, Leofrene, Atwell (E71–E73). Working with the secret rebellion of Able Foster "the Bear," they intercepted Atwell's treasure wagons (Evac died in that fight, E74), freed the thief Benjamin from the Canick cave (E75) — he joined, alongside the newly arrived Vane — and broke the tribe's stronghold and the Crag (E76–E79). Loot tied everything together: Blackwood's letters to "Mother," jars of severed fingers bound for Viola, and a creeping cold curse cast from the party's own stolen blood.

The screws tightened. Blackwood froze party members with borrowed power in open court while Airdale burned (E81); the resistance hid in a butcher's basement, where the Baroness revealed that LJ — the cursed lion-creature the party had carried since Sultray — was her lost son Leon (E81–E82). Max briefly pulled the party away to Wintervail (E84), retaking the Spires of the Kraken for his rogue Knights cell, "the Fist" — a thread that would own the next arc. Back in the marsh, the party finally killed Viola, Quinton's family tormentor, Benjamin's natural-20 javelin ending her (E85); Cruucar packed her head as a dinner gift.

Dinner at Blackwood Manor (E86–E87) dropped all masks: Blackwood served wine with Elias's head in it, took the party apart, and revealed himself as Valadon the Black Dread — the dragon, and Aum Shai's reigning Arch Dragon. He burned Corwell Keep and carried off the Baroness. Through Mallory's magic mirror the party entered the dragon's lair (E88–E89): the dying Baron passed rule to the restored Leon (LJ), and in the lair battle, Leon — hurled by the Bear — lanced Valadon to death. The party freed Elizabeth Janis, were hailed Saviors of Evershire, and were granted Blackwood's Manor (E90). The celebration ended with a sword-drawn cry of "Intruder!"

## Key outcomes

- Zanim left the party (E70 was his last session) and Evac died (E74); Vane and Benjamin joined the party.
- Viola killed; LJ revealed as Leon Janis and restored to rule Evershire.
- Lord Blackwood unmasked as the dragon Valadon the Black Dread, Arch Dragon of Aum Shai — slain by Leon's lance.
- The party received Blackwood's Manor and the title Saviors of Evershire; lore surfaced of the Potentium, a veiled "puppet master" leading Aum Shai, and the return of the Zeeven.
