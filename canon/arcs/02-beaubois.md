# Arc 02 — The Beaubois Conspiracy (E09–E24)

## Story so far

With the assassination plot in hand, the party dug into the Beaubois — a once-great Sultray family, friends of the crown until the crown let them fall, now dealers in opiates, black magic, and family sacrifice, holed up in a chalet on the bluffs. Posing as the band "The Magnificent Four," the party infiltrated a Beaubois party (E10–E12), performing "Luzonovian Rhapsody" while searching Pierre Beaubois's study. They found tower blueprints annotated into a research facility, a mural of Orcus — the betrayer god of undeath — and overheard Pierre discussing a "refinement" nearing completion before black-cloaked figures grappled out the windows.

Back in Sultray, the conspiracy turned public. At a play for the city's dignitaries, worm-riddled undead tore the lead actress apart on stage while Pierre laughed (E14): Spawn of Kyuss, an ancient worm-plague undead recreated by Pierre. Viscountess Avesia threw the city behind the party. The blueprints identified the lost tower of the dark sorcerer Myrrl Blackfinger in the Madean Delta — now a refinery with underground piping running toward Sultray's fields.

The tower assault (E16–E19) brought a shield guardian, displacer beasts, and two lasting additions: Cruucar, a dragonborn found imprisoned in the dungeon, who joined the party; and the Sundering — a 500-year-old coded book in an unknown language that would take Evac months to crack. Pierre himself slipped away by Dimension Door, boasting that only one piece remained before he could unleash a zombie plague on Alambor.

The endgame ran through the ruined chalet's catacombs (E21–E23): assassins in the streets, graveyard undead, and finally Pierre's secret meeting, where barrels of corrupted texair were being poured into Sultray's sewers. Here the larger shape of the campaign first showed itself — Zeeven Dynasty lore, a recurring crescent-moon-and-stars symbol, a "Shadow King" angry at Pierre, an "Arch Dragon" in place, and the refrain "that which was lost must be returned." The party brought Pierre down (Quinton landing his first-ever melee hit), looted his ruby-eyed lion talisman and coded letters, and learned the sewers could be cleansed with divine energy.

Maximilian's letter analysis named names — Vontrice, Lorenzo, Zax, Viola — every one of which would matter for years. Avesia granted the Beaubois chalet's 170 acres to the newly styled Defenders of Sutray, and the party celebrated at Madam Alex's brothel.

## Key outcomes

- Pierre Beaubois defeated; his zombie-plague operation at Blackfinger's tower and the chalet catacombs dismantled; corrupted texair in Sultray's sewers identified.
- Cruucar joined the party (E18).
- The Shadowed Moon organization surfaced for the first time: its symbol, the "Arch Dragon," the Shadow King, and the phrase "return what is lost."
- The coded Sundering book recovered; the Beaubois chalet lands granted to the party.
- Names planted for later arcs: Viper Viola, Lorenzo, Zax, Vontrice.
