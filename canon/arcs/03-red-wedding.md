# Arc 03 — The Road to the Red Wedding (E25–E51)

## Story so far

The arc opened as a victory lap and ended in catastrophe. The party traveled via Grellier to Kierkenwall (E25–E27), met King Felix's inner circle — including High Chancellor Adune and peacekeeper Robert Kavney — and took a texair escort job for Sir Lorenzo Stansport. Lorenzo betrayed them, revealing his true form as an Oni (E29); his frame-job letters tied to "Viper Viola," the swamp deal-maker from Pierre's books. Hunting her brought Ford the gunslinger into their orbit and surfaced the first hint that Viola is bound to Quinton's own family. War clouds gathered: Luzonovian forces violated 200-year treaties, the Knights of Gidia deployed wholesale, and the party sailed with Adune to Vallon, Carasia's capital, for a summit of the Trene (E33–E36). At sea they acquired the god-eye orbs and lore of the imprisoned sea god Uquatoa — and the obsidian orb sank itself into Evac's chest.

Vallon was a nest of knives. A doppelganger assassin, Ors, impersonated Max and stabbed Noctis with the words "Return what is lost" (E37–E38). Queen Beatrice secretly hired the party to protect Prince Thomas, the groom. Their investigation of the Vontrice family found that "Eloise Vontrice" was a devil — a succubus wearing a dead woman's life — running a Luzonovian-flagged operation with rifles, explosives, and a death pact that briefly bound the party's tongues (E41–E45). They killed the demon, broke the curse, and found the temple blueprints and sniper traces that spelled out exactly how the wedding would die (E46).

It wasn't enough. On the wedding day (E48–E50), the sewer teams died of black wounds, an invisible blade-master guarded a custom long-range rifle in the clock tower — its poisoned ammunition killed Zanim, whom Adune brought back — and at the altar Prince Thomas was stabbed by his own best man, Justin, a black-cloaked Aum Shai operative who shut down the Archivists' magic with a restricting band, opened a portal that disgorged worm-creatures, and escaped. Revivify failed on the prince.

The aftermath (E51) named the enemy: Aum Shai, the organization of the Shadowed Moon, embedded everywhere. Robert Kavney placed four letters in the grieving queen's hands — later revealed as expert forgeries — and Beatrice blamed Luzonovia, burned her son's body on the water, declared war, and banished the Alambor Five from Carasia unless they brought back her son or his killers.

## Key outcomes

- Lorenzo the Oni exposed; Viola's hut raided and her connection to Quinton's mother seeded.
- The Eloise Vontrice succubus plot destroyed — but the wedding assassination succeeded anyway: Prince Thomas died, killed by Aum Shai.
- Carasia declared war on Luzonovia on forged evidence; the party was soft-banished and their chalet seized.
- Aum Shai named; Robert Kavney's role made deeply suspicious.
- Evac absorbed the obsidian god-eye orb ("Return"); Zanim died and was revived by Adune.

## Unresolved threads left behind

- Viola at large; Quinton's family in her shadow.
- Uquatoa, the Cloven crystals, Vex Totem in Freetown, and the remaining god-eye orbs.
- Lorenzo's fate after the swamp; the unread bulk of the Sundering book.
