# Arc 01 — Arrival in Alambor (E00–E08)

## Story so far

Session zero set the table: Alambor, 499 years after the Sundering, when the drow Zeeven Dynasty defied the gods and half the pantheon died. The world's economy runs on texair, transmutation-magic coins made only by the Archivists from the spice of the Arbor Alma tree. The party — Noctis, Quinton, Zanim, and Zook — began aboard a boat out of Kierkenwall bound for the Carasian port of Sultray, where the famous bard Finnad framed the story and Chias of the Volstruckers handed them a delivery, recognition phrases included: "Verily watchful we remain" / "When enemies strike we strike back."

The ship never arrived. Wrecked on the Carasian coastline, the party watched the dying Weatherface press a letter on them, bound for the Last Drop in Sultray. Dead beach bandits bore strange symbols — a U-shape with fingers, a smiling jack-o-lantern brand. Hobgoblins on the inland road cost Zanim a foot. At the village of Carstone they took their first paid work — bandit heads for the wharfmaster — and Quinton's song "The Alambor Five" formally named the party. Word spread of a royal wedding: Princess Anabel of Gidia would marry Prince Thomas of Carasia.

A river journey with the giant boatman Leon Steadyfoot brought lore (the Great Rift, the Hoard Alliance, Paratha's slave economy) and a farmstead terrorized by a metal-plated "creation." Somewhere in the gap left by the lost session E06, the party's path turned through the Mena Isles — and it cost them. Zook died (E07); his replacement was Evac, a Vedalken from the Mena Isles. The party also found Ferant dead in a crumbling manor haunted by a spectral woman, recovered 15 gemstones, and got their first real look at how texair flows from the Archivists to the factions.

The arc closed at the Last Drop in Sultray, where the letter finally reached its man: Maximilian "Max" Taldross of the Knights of Gidia. Under a truth spell he revealed the campaign's first great plot — Luzonovia, in league with the fallen Beaubois family, planned to assassinate Princess Anabel before the wedding at Balom. By breakfast the party were sworn Defenders of Godo and honorary knights of Gidia, with a mansion-fortress chalet to infiltrate.

## Key outcomes

- Party named the Alambor Five; sworn honorary knights of Gidia under Max Taldross.
- Zook died (E07); Evac the Vedalken joined.
- Weatherface's letter delivered; the Anabel assassination plot revealed — Luzonovia and the Beaubois family behind it.
- Setting load-bearing lore established: the Sundering, the Zeeven, texair and the Archivists, the Volstruckers.
