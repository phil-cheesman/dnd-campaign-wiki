# Arc 04 — Fugitives at Sea: The Goldcrest Heist (E52–E66)

## Story so far

Banished but rich, the party reinvented itself as a ship's company. High Rector Lucia bought them the Black Widow for 1,000 platinum; Yankee Williams signed on as first mate and crewed it with Ronkros the aarakocra navigator, Daylor, Taryn, Razortail, and Leon the goliath bosun (E52–E54). A new tradition began: reading the decoded Sundering book one page per session, starting with "The Spark" and the Dawn Gods. Side obligations stacked up — Rudar's cargo contract to Goldcrest, and Noctis's Harper promise to find Kieran, an agent lost investigating a slave trade in Mandalonia.

In Goldcrest, the merchant prince Shawn Williams hired them to steal the private ledger of Marquise Avesy, "the Easterner," the shadow ruler of the city's commerce (E56). The heist (E57) was half-triumph, half-fiasco: they cracked the gem-lock vault and grabbed the ledger and treasures, but a near-invulnerable stone golem and a smashed display case blew their stealth, and Avesy saw Quinton flying out a window. The payout — 22,000 gold laundered through a fake cotton consignment under the alias "James Beach" — arrived alongside wanted posters bearing three of their faces (E58). The escape from the docks (E59) drowned two men and put the whole city on alarm.

The party went underground as shipwrights: repainted and re-papered the vessel as Wrecked at the Reef, forged a deed, adopted cover identities, and sent Yankee's crew off on legitimate trade (E60–E61). At Emerald Lake, the town leader Odo offered to make them legal in exchange for recovering a lost military message in Callum Heights, where something was breaking cross-country trade (E62). The job ran them through a harpy flock and their Chimera god (E63), then deep into a cave where Dragoloth the Silent — a twelve-legged behir who spoke of "the Crescent Moon," a Dark One in the eastern swamps, and a great red devil in the mountains — swallowed Noctis whole before dying (E65).

In the monster's stomach lay the message canister, sealed with the Lion of Gidia, beside crates marked with the Shadowed Moon (E66). They emerged to find Emerald Lake surrounded by 18,000 troops of the North under General Barnabas — the war was no longer somewhere else. The arc closed on a changing of the guard: Berrian arrived, a paladin seeking texair for his gravely sick mother, just as Quinton prepared to leave for his own family.

## Key outcomes

- The party became shipowners and then fugitives: Avesy's ledger stolen, faces on wanted posters, ship re-flagged as Wrecked at the Reef.
- Odo's message recovered; the harpies, Chimera, and the behir Dragoloth killed; party reached level 8.
- The Sundering book readings began; Kieran's disappearance accepted as a standing Harper debt.
- Berrian joined (E66); the Gidian war machine arrived in the interior.

## Unresolved threads left behind

- Marquise Avesy's vendetta and Shawn Williams's exposure in Goldcrest.
- Kieran still missing in the Mandalonia slave trade.
- Dragoloth's words: the Crescent Moon, the Dark One in the east, the red devil in upper Callum Heights.
- Shadowed Moon crates and a mechanical drill in the behir's cave — someone was mining there.
- The coded message itself, still to be decoded and delivered.
