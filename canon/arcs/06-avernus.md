# Arc 06 — The Fist, the Nightmare King, and Avernus (E91–E102)

## Story so far

Adune was the intruder at the manor — and he came with a mission. At the Spires of the Kraken the party joined Max, Sir Gallot, and Sir Trent Hightower to learn of the "Titan Killer": the Luzonovians, working with rogue Archivists, sought a power source of unlimited energy, and the Nightmare King — a Luzonovian spy-scientist — had slaughtered the mine's guards (E91). The party fought through Fire Giant caverns of molten rivers and military barracks (E91–E92), where Adune admitted he had worked with the Nightmare King before: a dangerous fey drow capable of interplanar travel. The hidden prize was a cluster of blue-white crystal of unlimited energy, not of this elemental plane.

The Nightmare King escaped the party's assault with a taunt — "Follow to the infernal ringing" — leaving behind five planar tuning forks (E93). Adune sent the party to hell. At the gates of Avernus, the Gatekeeper exacted a pact: a soul for a soul, plus interest per person per day, payable by vanquishing natives and learning their true names (E94). The party crossed rivers of blood and watched devil-demon battle lines, learning of Bell, chief advisor to Zariel and would-be usurper of Avernus. Cruucar turned soul-collector, killing the liar Olf and nearly turning on the party in a collection rage (E95–E96); the toll was paid back at the Gatekeeper's bridge.

The target was Bell's Forge, a temple of blood sacrifice in the clouds. The party crawled in through the waste shaft — "the ol' Catholic poop hole loop hole" — disguised as devils and slaves (E97–E98). Past a horrifying urn-riddle door, General Bell himself rose from the blood fountain: a 35-foot pit fiend, CR 27, who revealed the hunter Harden had sold them out, and beheaded the hireling Colossus with his flaming sword. Racing rounds of combat to charge a teleportation circle, the party escaped with the crystal — Vane died and was Revivify'd, Max nearly fell — and materialized atop the Spires of the Kraken, weapon in hand, to Adune's open astonishment (E99). They hit level 11.

The reward was the campaign's high-water mark of legitimacy (E100): King Felix honored them as Defenders of Sutrey, Proctors of Evershire, and Slayers of Valadon, with 12,000+ platinum, council membership, and free use of Gidia's teleportation circles. Quinton returned — Aum Shai had murdered his family (E102). Adune showed them "the Farm," where the artifact's Potentium would power constructs and shields for the war. Ominous notes rang underneath: Robert Kavney's aura-less aside about "the other energy source," the king's strangely reverted demeanor, and Valinor's summons — Berrian's father had gone to meet a planes-walker named Jameti Costco, and the trail pointed south to Tarlif.

## Key outcomes

- The crystal power source — the sentient "Titan Killer" — stolen from Bell's Forge in Avernus and handed to Adune and the Knights of Gidia.
- Colossus died in Avernus; Vane died and was revived; party reached level 11.
- Royal honors and riches from King Felix; Quinton rejoined, his family murdered by Aum Shai.
- The Nightmare King escaped, identity unresolved (paid off in Arc 8).
