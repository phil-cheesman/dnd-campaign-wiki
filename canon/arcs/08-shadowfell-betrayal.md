# Arc 08 — Tarlif, the Shadowfell, and the Great Betrayal (E114–E136)

## Story so far

In Tarlif the party gained its final member — Torgoth, an 8.5-foot goliath met in the market (E114–E115) — and uncovered an Aum Shai cell running the city: dock master Rasheed Soltar, the mastermind Tinkertop, and Nardif Darksi, the new Arch Dragon sitting on Tarlif's ruling council. Jameti Costco came clean (E116): the party's relics are gate keys that can open a portal "larger than anyone can" to the Feywild and Shadowfell, and Berrian's father Borrus had followed the White Knights — the Knights of Theydune — into the Shadowfell through a gateway at Moonbright, Berrian's dying hometown.

The party went in after him. Past Berrian's sister Marora, the storm giant Orgmond, and Shadowfell wyverns (E117–E119), they swam through the Pool of Death into a sunless plane of burned stumps and creeping madness — sleep changed people: Noctis attempted suicide, Torgoth attacked the party, Cruucar woke twenty miles away (E120–E123). Through Grimsburg and the gang-run city of Gloomville they worked for the crime boss Sane, which led them — posing as a Texair merchant's bodyguards — into the Tower of Illmore, headquarters of the Knights of Theydune and their undead master, the Dwemerlake (E124–E126). They fought free through constructs, the Knight of Umber, Spawn of Kyuss, and the Tainted (destroyed by a single ~200-damage smite from Berrian); Vane died at the bridge and was revived by Torgoth (E126–E128). The bitter truth: Berrian's mother was the bridge between the Knights and Aum Shai, mind-lost in the Sorrowshade forest — and the party led five Knights straight to her at the Dark Glen (E129–E130). Berrian died there (E131, circumstances unrecorded), but the quest ended with both his parents saved from the Shadowfell, the party gifted and returned — sixty days gone.

The endgame moved fast. Reunited with Ford, Leon, Max, and Jameti (E132), the party climbed the Shadowmoon Tower's trial chambers — Cruucar surrendering his brothers' axes as the price (E133) — and confronted Nardif Darksi, who was also the Nightmare King from the Iron Forge mines, Aum Shai's Arch Dragon (E134). His seal exposed Sir Trent Hightower, the Righteous Hand of Gidia, as the apparent Shadowed Moon. The party raced to Kierkenwall and infiltrated the king's war council to expose him — and walked into the trap of the entire campaign (E135–E136). A wall of force, a devastating spell, King Felix dead, the party framed as his assassins, Max's family burned to ash. Then the true reveal: Adune — patron, chancellor, friend since E27 — was the Shadowed Moon all along: Morgenrath Gafar, a surviving Zeeven, a 1,200-year-old drow lich empowered by a fragment of Omago. He absorbed the Avernus power source before their eyes. Max died saving the party; Leon and Shaahim fell too. Cruucar's Rod of Attenuation was the fourth of six gate keys. The shattered party escaped, leveled to 14, and the world skipped two months into open Gidian conquest.

## Key outcomes

- Torgoth joined; Berrian's parents rescued from the Shadowfell; Berrian and Vane each died and returned.
- Nardif Darksi unmasked as the Nightmare King and Arch Dragon; Sir Trent Hightower exposed as Aum Shai's second.
- Adune revealed as the true Big Bad — the Zeeven lich Morgenrath Gafar — holding the absorbed Potentium and most of the six gate keys.
- King Felix murdered and the party framed; Max Taldross, Leon, and Shaahim dead; the Taldross family destroyed.

## Unresolved threads left behind

- Adune's gate: he holds five keys; the sixth relic is still loose in the world.
- Sir Trent Hightower commanding Gidia's expansion as Adune's enforcer.
- The party's fugitive status — framed regicides in a Gidia-conquered world.
- Tarlif's Aum Shai cell (Rasheed, Tinkertop) and its slave trade; the vow to end both.
- Nardif Darksi's fate after the tower, and whatever it is he fears.
