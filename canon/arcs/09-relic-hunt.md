# Arc 09 — The Hunt for the Sixth Relic (E137–E161)

## Story so far

Two months after the fall of Kierkenwall, the map is grim: Adune's Gidians hold the entire north, Dalacia and the Morian Kingdom have fallen, and only Vallon and Grellier remain Carasian. The old enemies — Carasia and Luzonovia — have stopped fighting each other and turned together toward the real threat (E137). Queen Beatrice and King Rupert tasked the Alambor Six to learn everything about Adune, and at the Archivists' citadel of Donum Vite the High Architect confirmed it all (E139): Adune is Morgenrath Gafar, a 1,200-year-old drow lich who lost his family in the Sundering and means to resurrect the Zeeven by opening a gate to another dimension. He holds four — soon five — of the six relics. Berrian filled an empty Archivist council seat, and the Arbor Alma itself greeted him: "Welcome back, Berrian." The mission crystallized: find the sixth relic, the Cloak of Displacement, first.

The trail ran through Aum Shai's logistics. At the cliff-fortress of Mag Stormeach the party located Rasheed Soltar ahead of a leadership meeting (Captain McKay, Trent Hightower, Zax, the Merchant, the Easterner) and hit him early, disguised as Gidian Navy (E141–E143). Rasheed died talking: the captured Harper spy Kieran lay caged beneath the Whispering Stacks, and a sparrow calling card pointed to Captain Mallie Grisham of Fortune's Favor. A filthy, glorious sewer escape with Mr. Whiskers the rat freed Kieran — tortured to death and revived over and over — and thirty slaves, capped by a stolen ship and a running harbor battle (E144–E146). Zax's Black Widow then drove Wrecked at the Reef onto a reef; Torgoth's Control Water raised it again, at a cost of 8 texair (E147–E148), and Kieran was recaptured by the Merchant's hunters.

Pirate diplomacy followed: Port Airsteach and the giant-blooded clans of Lila O'cane, a ship taken by pure con (Gregory 2.0 sacrificed to a cannon barrage), and finally Mallie Grisham himself (E149–E152). His price for the artifact's trail: capture the Kraken plaguing the Pirate Trinity. The party dove 60,000 feet, collared the beast in its bone-graveyard lair — Noctis swallowed and climbing back up its throat — and delivered its control to the Trinity (E153–E156). Mallie's confession: he had recovered the relic from the sunken Temple of Fenreal and traded it away; it now lies with the artifact-obsessed Yuan-Ti of Oojezz, deep in the jungles of Jazacha. He also leaked the party's destination — Hightower, the Merchant, and Zax are all converging.

The current push is Oojezz itself: aboleths, piranha pools, the rock-titan Zuk'taal (survived only by Mallie burning a stored Wish), and a disguised descent into the city's six-level sacrifice pyramid, where the party is currently fighting Yuan-Ti wizards and a Hydra floor by floor (E157–E161).

## Key outcomes (so far)

- Adune's identity, plan, and relic count confirmed by the Archivists; Berrian seated on their council.
- Rasheed Soltar killed; Kieran and thirty slaves freed (Kieran since recaptured); Aum Shai's Mandalonia meeting disrupted.
- The Kraken captured for the Pirate Trinity; Mallie Grisham allied; the sixth relic traced to the Yuan-Ti city of Oojezz.
